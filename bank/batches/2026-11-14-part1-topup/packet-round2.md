## m1-226 (module 1)
Source: Cross-boundary Movement of Physical Currency and Bearer Negotiable Instruments Ordinance, Cap. 629, ss.17–18 | 《實體貨幣及不記名可轉讓票據跨境流動條例》（第629章）第17至18條

EN: Regarding the seizure and detention of cash-type items (CBNIs) under the Cross-boundary Movement of Physical Currency and Bearer Negotiable Instruments Ordinance, which of the following are correct?
  1. Subject to sections 18 and 19, CBNIs seized under section 17 on suspicion that they are crime proceeds or terrorist property may be detained for not more than 10 working days after the day of seizure.
  2. For that purpose a working day under section 17(4) is a day that is not a general holiday or a Saturday, and not a black rainstorm warning day or a gale warning day as defined by section 71(2) of Cap. 1.
  3. An order made under section 18(2) authorising continued detention of CBNIs suspected to be crime proceeds may specify a period of up to 6 months beginning on the date of the order.
  4. Under section 18(5), a new order extending detention of CBNIs suspected to be crime proceeds must not take the total period beyond 2 years beginning on the date of the order made under section 18(2).
  5. Within the period mentioned in section 17(2), the authorized officer may apply to a magistrate for the order authorising continued detention of CBNIs suspected to be crime proceeds.
TC: 關於《實體貨幣及不記名可轉讓票據跨境流動條例》下檢取及扣留現金類物品的規定，下列哪些陳述正確？
  1. 除第18及19條另有規定外，根據第17條因懷疑屬犯罪得益或恐怖分子財產而檢取的現金類物品，可予扣留，惟扣留期間不得超過檢取日期後的10個工作日。
  2. 就此而言，第17(4)條所指的「工作日」指並非公眾假期或星期六，亦非《釋義及通則條例》（第1章）第71(2)條所界定的黑色暴雨警告日或烈風警告日的任何一個日子。
  3. 根據第18(2)條作出的、授權繼續扣留懷疑屬犯罪得益的現金類物品的命令，所指明期間不得超過自該命令日期起計的6個月。
  4. 根據第18(5)條，延長扣留懷疑屬犯罪得益的現金類物品的新命令，不得使總扣留期間超過自根據第18(2)條作出的命令的日期起計的2年。
  5. 獲授權人員可在第17(2)條所述的最初扣留期間內，向裁判官申請命令，以授權繼續扣留該人員合理地懷疑屬犯罪得益的現金類物品。

## m1-235 (module 1)
Source: C&ED Circular MSSB/FATF_02/2026 (3 July 2026) item (2) | 香港海關通函 MSSB/FATF_02/2026（2026年7月3日）第(2)項

EN: Regarding the FATF statement on jurisdictions under increased monitoring, as described in the C&ED circular of 3 July 2026, which of the following are correct?
  1. In addition to the statement on High-Risk Jurisdictions subject to a Call for Action, the FATF has issued an updated statement on Jurisdictions under Increased Monitoring, which the circular indicates can be found on the FATF website.
  2. The FATF will closely monitor the progress made by these jurisdictions in addressing the identified strategic deficiencies in their regimes to counter money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing.
  3. Jurisdictions placed under increased monitoring are, by that listing alone, already subject to a FATF call for applying countermeasures to protect the international financial system.
  4. The jurisdictions under increased monitoring, as described in that FATF statement, are limited to Myanmar only, and no other jurisdiction appears on the increased-monitoring list.
TC: 關於海關於2026年7月3日通函第(2)項所述，財務行動特別組織有關被加強監察的司法管轄區的聲明，下列哪些陳述正確？
  1. 除呼籲各方對高風險司法管轄區採取行動的聲明外，特別組織亦已發布一份有關被加強監察的司法管轄區的最新聲明，該聲明可於其網站（只備有英文版）取覽。
  2. 特別組織將會密切監察這些已被列入該份有關被加強監察的司法管轄區名單的司法管轄區，在處理該聲明所指已識別的策略性缺失方面所取得的進展。
  3. 單憑某一司法管轄區被列入被加強監察的司法管轄區名單，該司法管轄區便已經屬需遵從特別組織要求而採取針對措施的司法管轄區。
  4. 被加強監察的司法管轄區只限於緬甸一地，該份有關被加強監察的司法管轄區的最新名單並無載列緬甸以外的任何其他司法管轄區。
