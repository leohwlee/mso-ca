### How it works

Four stages of filtration, every glass:

1. **Sediment pre-filter** catches sand, rust and particles.
2. **Activated carbon filter** reduces chlorine taste and odour.
3. **Reverse-osmosis (RO) membrane** reduces dissolved lead by up to 99% and fluoride by up to 90% (independent laboratory test, March 2026).
4. **Remineralisation stage** adds calcium and magnesium back for taste.

### FAQ

**Can I use it with well water?**
The X2 is designed for municipally treated tap water. It is not intended for untreated well water or water that is microbiologically unsafe.

**Is it a water softener?**
No. The X2 is not a water softener.

**Does it need electricity?**
Yes. The pump and the UV indicator light need mains power; the X2 does not work during a power cut.

**Why does my water taste different at first?**
New carbon filters can release fine carbon dust. Discard the first two full tanks after installing a new carbon filter.
