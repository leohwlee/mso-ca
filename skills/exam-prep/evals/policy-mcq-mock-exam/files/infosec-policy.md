# Northwind Logistics — Information Security Policy

Version 3.1, effective 1 July 2026. Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
This is a fictional policy written for testing.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to all employees, contractors and temporary staff who use Northwind systems or data, wherever they work.

1.2 Suppliers who connect to Northwind systems are covered by the Supplier Security Standard, not by this policy.

## 2. Passwords and sign-in

2.1 Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases of four or more unrelated words are encouraged.

2.2 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all remote access and for every administrator account.

2.3 Passwords are not changed on a schedule. A password must be changed immediately if there is any suspicion that it has been disclosed or compromised.

2.4 Passwords may be stored only in the company-approved password manager. Writing passwords down or storing them in browsers is not permitted.

## 3. Devices

3.1 Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock the screen after 5 minutes of inactivity.

3.2 Personal devices may access company email and calendar only through the managed mobile app. They must not be used to store Confidential or Restricted data.

3.3 USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops unless the IT Service Desk has enabled an encrypted company-issued drive.

## 4. Data classification

4.1 All data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Data that has not been classified is treated as Internal.

4.2 Confidential data may be sent outside Northwind only by encrypted email or the approved file-transfer service.

4.3 Restricted data may be shared outside Northwind only with the written approval of its Data Owner, and only through the approved file-transfer service.

4.4 Printed Confidential and Restricted documents must be collected from the printer immediately and disposed of in the secure shredding bins.

## 5. Reporting incidents

5.1 Any suspected security incident, including a lost or stolen device, must be reported to the Security Desk within 1 hour of discovery.

5.2 Suspected phishing emails must be reported with the "Report phishing" button. They must not be forwarded to colleagues, including to the Security Desk.

5.3 Staff must not investigate an incident themselves or contact a suspected attacker.

## 6. Access

6.1 Access is granted on the principle of least privilege, on the request of the employee's line manager.

6.2 Line managers review their team's access every quarter.

6.3 A leaver's access is removed by the end of their last working day. Access for staff moving between teams is reviewed within 10 working days of the move.

6.4 Administrators must use a separate administrator account for privileged tasks and their standard account for everything else.

## 7. Exceptions

7.1 An exception to this policy may be approved only by the CISO.

7.2 An exception lasts at most 6 months and may be renewed once. Every exception is recorded in the exceptions register.

## 8. Training

8.1 New joiners must complete security training within 30 days of starting. All staff repeat it every year.

8.2 Staff who have not completed training by the deadline have 14 days' grace; after that their system access is suspended until they complete it.

## 9. Breaches of this policy

9.1 A breach of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
