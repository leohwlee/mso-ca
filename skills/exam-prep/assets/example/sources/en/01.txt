Harbourview Public Library — Lending Rules (edition of 1 March 2026)
This is a fictional document written to illustrate the exam-prep skill.

1. Membership
1.1 Any resident of the district aged 12 or over may apply for an adult card. Children under 12 may hold a junior card with the written consent of a parent or guardian.
1.2 An applicant must show one proof of identity bearing a photograph and one proof of address issued within the last three months.
1.3 A card is valid for three years and may be renewed in person or online.
1.4 A lost card must be reported within 7 days. A replacement card costs $20.

=== page 2 ===
2. Loans
2.1 An adult card holder may borrow up to 12 items at a time; a junior card holder up to 6.
2.2 The loan period is 21 days for books and 7 days for DVDs.
2.3 A loan may be renewed twice, unless another member has reserved the item.
2.4 Reference books, periodicals of the current month and items marked "Library use only" may not be borrowed.

3. Overdue items and fines
3.1 A fine of $1 a day is charged for each overdue book, up to a maximum of $30 per item. No fines are charged on junior cards.
3.2 Borrowing is suspended while fines of $50 or more are outstanding.
3.3 An item overdue for 60 days is treated as lost; the member pays the replacement cost plus a $10 processing fee.
3.4 To return an item, place it in the returns chute or hand it to staff; staff check the item, update the record and shelve it.
