    Who may hold a card, and what it takes誰可持證及申請要求

    Residents of the district aged 12 or over may apply for an adult card; children under 12 hold a junior card with a parent's or guardian's written consent.區內年滿12歲的居民可申請成人證；未滿12歲的兒童須有父母或監護人的書面同意，方可持有兒童證。

    01 r.1.1

    What an applicant shows申請人須出示2

    1. One proof of identity bearing a photograph一份附有相片的身分證明
    2. One proof of address issued within the last three months一份三個月內發出的住址證明

    Not on the list: proof of income, a reference letter.不在清單內：入息證明、推薦信。

    01 r.1.2

    A card lasts three years and may be renewed in person or online.圖書證有效期三年，可親身或於網上續期。 N1

    Trap"Renewal only in person" drops the online route; it is false.「只可親身續期」漏了網上途徑，屬錯誤。

    01 r.2.1, 3.1

    A loan may be renewed twice, unless another member has reserved the item. Reference books, this month's periodicals and "Library use only" items never leave the library.外借資料可續借兩次，但已有其他會員預約者除外。參考書、當月期刊及「只限館內使用」的資料不可外借。

    Returning an item歸還資料
