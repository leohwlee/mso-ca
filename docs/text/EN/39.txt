Key Legislation 
Legal Defense
Anyone, who has made a report to an authorized officer (JFIU) and the disclosure 
relates to that act, does not commit a money laundering offense in the following 
two situations: 
1. that disclosure is made before he does that act and he does that act with the 
consent of an authorized officer;
or
2. The report was made after he had disposed of the property: 
- on his own initiative; and 
- as soon as it is reasonable for him to make it.
