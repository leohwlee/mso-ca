Circular to Money Service Operators 
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing 
Amendments to Money Changers Ordinance (Cap. 34) 
Money Service Operators (“MSOs”) are reminded that the Money Changers Ordinance 
(“MCO”), Cap. 34, has been amended and came into effect on 24 August 2025.  MSOs 
carrying out money changing services should take note of their statutory obligations relating to 
transaction notes and rates display required in Schedule 2 and 3 to the MCO.   
 
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact us at 3742 
7742.
