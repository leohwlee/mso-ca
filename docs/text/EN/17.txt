23 June 2025                                                  Circular 
 
Circular to Money Service Operators 
 
Launch of E-form Submission for Passenger Information 
(Currency and Bearer Negotiable Instruments) 
(effective from 23 June 2025) 
 
The Customs and Excise Department (“C&ED”) has launched a new website “E-form 
Submission for Passenger Information (Currency and Bearer Negotiable Instruments)” 
(“E-form”), effective from 23 June 2025.  Travellers may use the E-form to pre-fill 
information of the currency and bearer negotiable instruments (“CBNIs”) with a total 
value of more than HKD120,000 (or its equivalent in foreign currencies) prior to their 
arrival in/departure from Hong Kong to obtain a QR code for making a declaration.   
 
As the business of Money Service Operators (“MSO”) may require to make a 
declaration in respect of the cross-boundary transportation of CBNIs through passenger 
channel, C&ED kindly invite the MSOs to use the E-form, which may reduce the 
clearance time on the spot.   
     
The declaration procedures are as follows:- 
1. Pre-fill information of the CBNIs in advance using the E-form 
2. Obtain a QR code  
3. Present the QR code to a Customs officer on the spot to make the declaration 
 
The E-form website can be accessed at 
https://www.cedp.customs.gov.hk/index_en.html.   
 
For details on how to use the E-form, please refer to the User Manual of the E-form 
available at 
https://www.customs.gov.hk/hcms/filemanager/common/pdf/cbni/user_manual_edm_
en.pdf. 
 
Should you have any queries regarding this Circular, please contact Customs 
Financial Investigation Bureau at 3759 3184 or send email to 
customs_ficd_cfib@customs.gov.hk.
