Circular to Money Service Operators 
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing 
 
Fraudulent Websites and Social Media Platforms related to Money Service Operators 
The Customs and Excise Department (“C&ED”) would like to draw the attention of money service 
operators (“MSOs”) to the upward trend of fraudulent websites and bogus advertisement on social media 
platforms impersonating MSOs’ identities to carry out illegal money service operations or fraudulent 
activities. 
 
MSOs are reminded to take appropriate security measures to protect personal data and stay vigilant of 
any websites or social media platforms getting hold of their business information without their 
permission.  In case of encountering suspected fraudulent websites related to the MSOs’ own business 
identity, they are recommended to notify the public, and report the case to the C&ED and the Hong Kong 
Police force. 
 
 
 
Money Service Supervision Bureau 
Customs and Excise Department
