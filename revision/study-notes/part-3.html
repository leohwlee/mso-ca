必記Remember金融機構、關係及對等司法管轄區Institutions, relationships and equivalent jurisdictions

  • 金融機構涵蓋認可機構、持牌法團、獲授權保險人、持牌個人保險代理／保險代理機構／保險經紀公司、MSO、郵政署署長、儲值支付工具持牌人、持牌虛擬資產服務提供者及穩定幣持牌人等法定類別；各類別並非適用完全相同的發牌部份。Financial institutions include authorized institutions, licensed corporations, authorized insurers, licensed individual insurance agents/agencies/broker companies, MSOs, the Postmaster General, SVF licensees, licensed VAS providers and stablecoin licensees. They do not all use the same licensing Part.
  • 業務關係是有持續性元素的業務、專業或商業關係，或機構在對方首次以準客戶身分接觸時預期有持續性元素的關係（OR）。非經常交易是機構與沒有業務關係的客戶之間的交易；一次交易金額小，不會消除本已存在的持續關係。A business relationship is a business, professional or commercial relationship with an element of duration OR one expected to have that element when the person first contacts the institution as a potential customer. An occasional transaction is with a customer who has no business relationship. A small transaction does not erase an existing ongoing relationship.
  • 對等司法管轄區指香港以外的FATF成員，或採取與附表2相若要求的司法管轄區。「營業日」的法定定義排除公眾假期、烈風警告日及黑色暴雨警告日；遇到營業日期限須使用法定定義。An equivalent jurisdiction is a FATF member other than Hong Kong or a jurisdiction imposing similar Schedule 2 requirements. The statutory business-day definition excludes public holidays, gale-warning days and black-rainstorm-warning days; apply it where a deadline uses business days.

必記Remember實益擁有人與政治人物Beneficial owners and PEPs

  • 公司：自然人直接／間接擁有或控制超過25%已發行股本、行使／控制超過25%股東大會投票權，或最終控制管理。合夥：自然人直接／間接超過25%資本／利潤或投票權，或最終控制管理。兩類也包括交易為其進行的另一「人」，不能把這個分支一概限於自然人。正好25%未達百分比門檻，仍須檢查控制。⚖B14For corporations: individuals directly/indirectly owning or controlling more than 25% of issued capital or general-meeting votes, or exercising ultimate management control. For partnerships: individuals with directly/indirectly more than 25% of capital, profits or votes, or ultimate management control. Both also include another “person” on whose behalf the transaction is conducted; that limb is not universally limited to individuals. Exactly 25% misses the percentage test, but control must still be examined.⚖B14
  • 信託實益擁有人包括：有權享有信託財產既得權益的受益人或某類別受益人（不論管有權、剩餘權或復歸權，亦不論可否廢除）；財產授予人；受託人；保護人／執行人；或最終控制信託的自然人。不得把受益人類別漏掉或限成個人。對公司、合夥、信託以外的人，第1條涵蓋最終擁有／控制的自然人，或該人代其行事的另一人。⚖B14Trust BOs include a beneficiary or class of beneficiaries entitled to a vested interest in trust property, whether in possession, remainder or reversion and whether defeasible or not; the settlor; trustee; protector/enforcer; or an individual with ultimate control. Do not omit beneficiary classes or limit beneficiaries to individuals. For a person other than a corporation, partnership or trust, section 1 covers an individual ultimately owning/controlling it or another person on whose behalf it acts.⚖B14
  • 法定政治人物定義針對香港以外獲委任重要公職的人，包括國家／政府首長、高級政客、政府／司法／軍事高層、國企高級行政人員及重要政黨幹事，亦包括法定家庭成員及密切聯繫者；不包括中／初級職員。指引另處理香港本地及國際組織政治人物。The statutory PEP definition concerns prominent public functions outside Hong Kong, including heads of state/government, senior politicians, government/judicial/military officials, state-enterprise executives and important party officials, plus statutory family/close associates. It excludes middle/junior officials. The Guideline separately addresses domestic and international-organisation PEPs.

  • 公共機構包括行政／立法／市政議會、政府部門／事業、地方或公共當局／事業、由行政長官或政府委任的委員會等組織，以及依法可用公共身分行事的組織；不只指政府部門。法人包括公共機構及有／無法團身分的團體。Public bodies include executive/legislative/municipal/urban councils, government departments/undertakings, local/public authorities/undertakings, CE/Government-appointed boards and bodies empowered to act publicly by enactment. This definition differs from the Guideline’s wider reliable-independent verification sources.

四項盡職審查與觸發條件Four CDD measures and their triggers

認識Recognise核實客戶身分的五類法定來源five statutory sources for customer identity verification

  • 附表2第2(1)(a)條列出五類替代來源（OR）：(1)政府機構；(2)有關當局或任何其他有關主管當局；(3)在香港以外執行相類職能的主管當局；(4)認可數碼識別系統；(5)有關當局認可的任何其他可靠及獨立來源。可採用來自這些來源的文件、數據或資料，不只限於一種紙本證件，亦不是任何看似可信的資料便自動合資格。Schedule 2 section 2(1)(a) provides five alternative sources (OR): (1) a governmental body; (2) the relevant authority or any other relevant authority; (3) an overseas authority performing similar functions; (4) a recognized digital identification system; or (5) any other reliable and independent source recognized by the relevant authority. Documents, data or information from these sources may be used: the rule is not restricted to one paper identity document, nor does every apparently credible source automatically qualify.
  • 適用於MSO的認可是海關關長的認可。另須分清：第2(1)(d)(i)條關於代表客戶行事的人，明列的是政府、相關當局、境外相類當局、及認可的其他可靠獨立來源四類；該清單沒有另列「認可數碼識別系統」一項。代表的身分核實與代表客戶行事的授權核實仍是兩項工作。For an MSO, recognition is by the CCE. Distinguish section 2(1)(d)(i) for a person acting for a customer: it expressly lists four categories—governmental bodies, relevant authorities, similar overseas authorities and recognized other reliable independent sources—and does not separately list a recognized digital identification system. Verifying the representative’s identity and verifying authority to act remain separate tasks.

必記Remember完整措施及時間Full measures and timing

  • 四項CDD：識別及核實客戶；識別實益擁有人並採合理措施核實及了解法人／信託結構；取得業務關係目的及擬具有的性質資料（明顯可見者除外）；識別／核實代表客戶行事的人及其授權。識別和核實是不同工作。The four CDD measures are: identify/verify the customer; identify beneficial owners and reasonably verify them, understanding legal-person/trust structures; obtain the relationship’s purpose/intended nature unless obvious; and identify/verify persons acting for the customer and their authority. Identification and verification are separate tasks.
  • 須在建立業務關係前、一般非經常交易等於或超過120,000元前、金融機構非經常電傳轉賬或虛擬資產轉移等於或超過8,000元前進行CDD。單次或看似有關連的多次操作須合併考慮。持牌虛擬資產服務提供者另有非轉賬非經常交易8,000元規則，不能套作所有MSO的一般門檻。⚖B16⚖B19CDD precedes a business relationship, general occasional transactions of at least HK$120,000, and financial-institution occasional wire/VA transfers of at least HK$8,000. Consider single or apparently linked operations together. Licensed VAS providers have a separate HK$8,000 non-transfer occasional-transaction rule; it is not every MSO’s general threshold.⚖B16⚖B19
  • 懷疑涉及洗錢／恐怖分子資金籌集，或懷疑以往識別／核實資料的真確／足夠程度，也須CDD，不受上述金額門檻限制。第13條另有非電傳匯款8,000元的特定識別及紀錄責任。⚖B19⚖B36Suspicion of ML/TF or doubt about previous identification/verification information also triggers CDD, regardless of those thresholds. Section 13 separately imposes specified identification/record duties for non-wire remittance of at least HK$8,000.⚖B19⚖B36
  • 延後核實只可在不打斷正常業務所必需、風險有效受管理時使用，並須在關係建立後合理切實可行的範圍內盡快完成。不能遵從時不得建立關係／作非經常交易；已建立的須盡快終止。⚖B16Delayed verification requires necessity to avoid interrupting normal business and effective risk management; complete it as soon as reasonably practicable after establishment. Inability to comply means no relationship/occasional transaction, or prompt termination of an existing relationship.⚖B16

簡化審查：只豁免指定措施Simplified CDD: only specified measures are omitted

必記Remember第4條的範圍Scope of section 4

  • 有合理理由相信客戶屬合資格類別時，可不做第2(1)(b)條的實益擁有人措施，其餘客戶、代表人及目的／性質措施仍適用。合資格實體出現在擁有權鏈內，豁免只及於該實體及其以上部分，不是整條鏈。Where there are reasonable grounds that the customer qualifies, section 2(1)(b) beneficial-owner measures may be omitted; customer, representative and purpose/nature measures remain. A qualifying entity within a chain stops BO enquiries at/beyond that entity, not throughout the chain.
  • 合資格類別：金融機構；在對等司法管轄區成立、經營相若金融業務、有相若措施且受相類當局監管的機構；在任何證券市場上市的法團；合資格投資公司；香港政府／公共機構；對等司法管轄區政府／相若公共機構。投資公司須由以下人士為全部投資者做相類CDD：金融機構，OR在香港／對等司法管轄區成立、有相類合規措施並受遵從監管的機構。⚖B31Qualifying categories: financial institutions; equivalent-jurisdiction institutions with similar financial business, similar compliance measures and comparable-authority supervision; corporations listed on any stock market; qualifying investment companies; Hong Kong Government/public bodies; and equivalent-jurisdiction governments/similar bodies. For an investment company, all investors must receive similar CDD from an FI OR an institution established in Hong Kong/an equivalent jurisdiction with similar compliance measures and supervision for compliance.⚖B31
  • 產品例外：供款從受僱收入扣除且不可轉讓權益的僱員退休計劃；沒有退保條款且不可作抵押的相關計劃保單；每年保費不多於8,000元或單次保費不多於20,000元的人壽保單。這些是指定產品，不是一般匯款的免審查門檻。Product exceptions cover employee retirement schemes funded by employment-income deductions with non-assignable interests; relevant scheme insurance without surrender/collateral; and life policies with annual premium no more than HK$8,000 or single premium no more than HK$20,000. These are specific products, not general remittance exemptions.
  • 律師／律師行客戶帳戶須同時是以其名義開立、混合其客戶金錢／證券、由其作代理管理，才適用第4(6)條的實益擁有人措施例外。出現懷疑或資料疑問仍須按第3條處理。⚖E22For solicitor/firm accounts, section 4(6) requires the account to be in the solicitor’s/firm’s name, mix clients’ money/securities, and be managed as their agent. Suspicion/information doubts still require section 3 handling.⚖E22

額外措施：遙距、政治人物及其他高風險Additional measures: remote customers, PEPs and other high risk

必記Remember先完成普通CDD，再加所需措施Additional to ordinary CDD

  • 非親身識別至少加一項：用未曾使用的第2(1)(a)／(ab)條資料再核實；補充核實已取得資料；或確保付款（多次時為首筆）經客戶同名戶口。該戶口須在認可機構，或同時符合四條件的境外機構：在對等司法管轄區成立、經營相若銀行業務、有相類合規措施、並受當地相當於金管局的主管當局監管。已按認可數碼識別系統資料完成第2(1)(a)／(ab)條措施者有第9(2)條例外，不是所有視像／電子程序都符合。⚖E17Non-face-to-face identification requires at least one: further verification with previously unused section 2(1)(a)/(ab) material; supplementary verification of obtained information; or payment (the first if multiple) through the customer’s same-name account. That account must be at an authorized institution OR an overseas institution meeting ALL four conditions: equivalent-jurisdiction establishment, similar banking business, similar compliance measures and supervision by a local authority with HKMA-like functions. Section 9(2) excepts customers whose section 2(1)(a)/(ab) measures use a recognized digital-ID system, not every video/electronic process.⚖E17
  • 非香港政治人物在建立／繼續關係前須高級管理層批准，並合理確立財富來源及資金來源。前政治人物經適當風險評估認定不再高風險時，法定例外才可使用；不能只因離任或過了固定年數便豁免。⚖B17⚖B32Non-Hong Kong PEP relationships require senior-management approval and reasonable establishment of both wealth and funds sources before establishment/continuation. A former PEP qualifies for the statutory exception only after appropriate assessment establishes no high risk, not automatically after leaving office or a fixed number of years.⚖B17⚖B32
  • 第15條其他高風險情況：建立／繼續關係須高級管理層批准，再合理確立財富及資金來源，或採其他額外緩解措施；現有關係另須合理核實實益擁有人。非經常交易須採額外緩解措施。管理層批准不是任選項，政治人物的全部指定措施亦不能被此選擇取代。⚖B17For section 15 other high-risk cases, establishing/continuing a relationship requires senior approval plus either reasonable establishment of wealth/funds sources or other additional mitigation; existing relationships also require reasonable BO verification. Occasional transactions require additional mitigation. Approval is not optional, and this choice does not replace all PEP measures.⚖B17
  • 不得開立／維持匿名或虛假姓名帳戶。認可機構不得與第17條所界定的空殼銀行法團建立／繼續往來銀行關係。Do not open/maintain anonymous or fictitious-name accounts. Authorized institutions must not establish or continue correspondent relationships with shell-bank corporations defined in section 17.

  • Ordinance (04) Sch. 2 s.5(1)(c), p. 367: identify transactions that “(i) are complex, unusually large in amount or of an unusual pattern; and (ii) have no apparent economic or lawful purpose” (Chinese 識辨符合以下說明的交易——(i)…；及(ii)…, p. 367)
  • AML/CFT Guideline (03) ¶5.1(b)(ii), p. 46: transactions that “(a) are complex, unusually large in amount or of an unusual pattern, and (b) have no apparent economic or lawful purpose”
  • AML/CFT Guideline (03) (Chinese) ¶5.1(b)(ii), p. 53: 識別(A)複雜、款額大得異乎尋常或進行模式異乎尋常的交易；及(B)沒有明顯經濟或合法目的之交易 (reads as two groups)
  • AML/CFT Guideline (03) (Chinese) ¶5.10(b), p. 55: 複雜、款額大得異乎尋常或進行模式異乎尋常；及並無明顯經濟或合法目的 (both)
裁決Decision
Learn “both together”: the duty to examine a transaction’s background and purposes and set out the findings in writing covers a transaction that is complex, unusually large or of an unusual pattern and also has no apparent economic or lawful purpose. One answer in both language views.
