客戶盡職審查：定義及四項措施Customer due diligence: definition and four measures

必記Remember四項基本措施Four basic measures

  • 客戶盡職審查（CDD）的第一項，是先確認你在跟誰做生意，再用可靠、獨立來源的文件、數據或資料，核實這個人或機構的身分。不能只聽對方自報姓名，便當成已經完成核實。The first customer due diligence (CDD) measure is to identify who you are dealing with, and then to verify that identity using documents, data or information from a reliable, independent source. A name the customer simply states is not, by itself, completed verification.
  • 如果有實益擁有人，還要識別這個人，並採取合理措施核實其身分，使機構信納自己知道對方是誰。對於法人及信託，還須了解擁有權及控制結構，不能只停在表面上的公司名稱。Where there is a beneficial owner, identify that person and take reasonable identity-verification measures, so the MSO is satisfied it knows who that person is. For legal persons and trusts, also understand the ownership and control structures; do not stop at the name on the surface.
  • 還要取得這段業務關係的目的，以及擬具有的性質。如果這些已經顯而易見，便不必另行再取一次。客戶不是自然人時，還須了解其業務性質。Also obtain information on the purpose and intended nature of the relationship, unless that is already obvious. Where the customer is not a natural person, also understand the nature of its business.
  • 如果有人代表客戶行事，須識別這名代表，並採取合理措施核實其身分，另外還要核實其授權。代表的身分，和這個人是否真的獲授權，是兩件都要處理的事，缺一不可。例如：櫃台來人出示公司名片，只能說明這個人是誰，並不能單憑名片證明公司已授權他簽約或發指示。If someone acts for the customer, identify that representative, take reasonable measures to verify the representative’s identity, and separately verify authority. Identity and authorisation are two distinct requirements, and both must be dealt with. For example: a business card produced at the counter may help show who the person is, but it does not, by itself, prove that the company has authorised that person to sign or give instructions.

理解Understand客戶的界定Who is the customer

  • 「客戶」通常是與機構建立業務關係的一方，或機構為其執行交易的一方，一般不包括交易裡的第三方。例如：對匯出機構而言，只是收款、而且與機構沒有其他關係的受款人，並不會自動成為該機構的客戶。The “customer” is generally the party with whom the relationship is established, or for whom the transaction is carried out, and usually excludes third parties to the transaction. For example: a beneficiary of an outward transfer who has no other relationship with the ordering MSO is not automatically that MSO’s customer.
  • 與法人建立關係時，須識別該法人本身。只識別到櫃台辦理手續的個人，並不能代替識別他所代表的公司。例如：職員只核對了來辦手續的張先生的身分證，卻沒有核實他所代表的有限公司，這樣並未完成對法人客戶的識別。When a relationship is established with a legal person, identify the legal-person customer itself. Identifying the individual at the counter does not substitute for identifying the company that person represents. For example: checking only Mr Cheung’s identity card, without verifying the limited company he represents, does not complete identification of the legal-person customer.

CDD觸發條件、金額及相連交易CDD triggers, thresholds and linked transactions

必記Remember必須執行的情況When CDD is required

  • 建立業務關係之前，不論金額多少，都必須先做客戶盡職審查。業務關係是具有持續性的業務、專業或商業關係，或者機構在首次接觸潛在客戶時，預期會具有這種持續性。一次過的小額兌換，如果機構並不預期會持續往來，通常還不是這裡所說的業務關係。Perform CDD before establishing a business relationship, regardless of the amount. A business relationship has an element of duration, or is expected to have one when the potential customer first contacts the MSO. A one-off low-value exchange, where no continuing relationship is expected, is not usually a business relationship of this kind.
  • 非經常交易，是與沒有業務關係的客戶進行的交易，並且有自己的金額觸發點。一般非經常交易達港幣120,000元或等值外幣、電傳轉帳達港幣8,000元或等值，以及虛擬資產轉帳涉及價值不少於港幣8,000元，均須在執行前完成客戶盡職審查。例如：一筆沒有業務關係的兌換剛好是港幣120,000元，已觸發一般非經常交易的CDD；不要把它跟電傳轉帳的港幣8,000元門檻混在一起。⚖B19⚖B16An occasional transaction is a transaction with a customer who has no business relationship with the MSO, and it has its own amount triggers. CDD is required before general occasional transactions of at least HK$120,000 or equivalent, wire transfers of at least HK$8,000 or equivalent, and virtual-asset transfers worth at least HK$8,000. In each case, the CDD must be completed before the transaction is carried out. For example: a one-off exchange of exactly HK$120,000 with a customer who has no business relationship already triggers general occasional-transaction CDD. Do not mix that figure up with the HK$8,000 wire-transfer threshold.⚖B19⚖B16
  • 計算門檻時，單筆交易要算，機構認為看來相連的多筆交易也要一併計算。同一受款人在短時間內，從一個或多個來源收到付款，或客戶經常向同一或多個目的地轉帳，都可以構成相連的線索。例如：兩筆看來相連的付款，即使每一筆都低於一般非經常交易的港幣120,000元門檻，合併計算後仍可能達標。When applying a threshold, count a single operation, and also operations that appear to the MSO to be linked. Payments to the same recipient from one or more sources over a short period, or regular transfers to the same or several destinations, may indicate linkage. For example: two payments that appear linked can still be counted together, and may meet the general occasional-transaction threshold of HK$120,000 even if each payment is below that figure.
  • 另外兩種情況也會觸發CDD，而且不受金額門檻限制：懷疑客戶或戶口涉及洗錢或恐怖分子資金籌集；或者懷疑原有識別或核實資料的真實性或充分性。即使金額很小，只要出現這類懷疑或疑問，仍須做審查。Two further situations also trigger CDD, and they are not subject to a monetary threshold: suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing involving the customer or the account; or doubts about the truth or adequacy of existing identification or verification information. Even a small amount still requires CDD if that suspicion or those doubts arise.
  • 不要只記得一般非經常交易的港幣120,000元門檻。非電傳的對外匯款如果達港幣8,000元，另有一套發款人核實及紀錄要求，會在本部分「匯款」一節說明。⚖B19⚖B36Do not rely solely on the general occasional-transaction threshold of HK$120,000. Non-wire outward remittances of at least HK$8,000 have separate originator-verification and record requirements, which are explained under Remittances later in this Part.⚖B19⚖B36

必記Remember第三方現金交易Third-party cash transactions

  • 如果不是戶口授權名單上的人，代為存入達港幣120,000元的現金，機構應要求其身分文件並保留副本，以便持續監察。與客戶風險背景不符的大額現金，須特別留意。For a cash transaction of at least HK$120,000 by a person who is not on the account mandate, request and copy that person’s identity document for ongoing monitoring. 身分資料：自然人、法人與信託Identity information: individuals, legal persons and trusts

必記Remember收集與核實須分開Distinguish collection from verification

  • 對自然人，至少要收集全名、出生日期、國籍、獨特識別號碼及文件類別；核實的則是全名、出生日期、號碼及文件類別。第4.3.3段的例子是：(a) 香港身份證或其他國家的身份證；(b) 有效的旅遊證件（例如未過期的護照）；或 (c) 其他可靠及獨立來源所提供的相關文件、數據或資料（例如政府機構發出的文件）。還須取得居住地址資料，但這並不表示每個客戶都一律要另交一份地址證明。For individuals, collect at least full name, date of birth, nationality, unique identification number and document type; verify the name, date of birth, number and document type. Paragraph 4.3.3 gives these examples: (a) a Hong Kong identity card or other national identity card; (b) a valid travel document (for example an unexpired passport); or (c) other relevant documents, data or information from a reliable and independent source (for example a document issued by a government body). Also obtain residential-address information; this does not impose a universal requirement for a separate address-proof document.
  • 對法人，至少要收集全名、成立、註冊或登記的日期及地點（包括註冊辦事處地址）、識別號碼及文件類別，以及在不同於註冊地址時的主要營業地點。核實的是名稱、法律形式、當時仍然存在，以及規管或約束其權力的安排。For legal persons, collect at least the full name, the date and place of incorporation, establishment or registration (including the registered office), the identification number and document type, and the principal place of business if it is different from the registered address. Verify the name, legal form, current existence, and the powers that govern or bind the legal person.
  • 核實法人時，可用的來源例子有八類，見指引第4.3.7段：(a) 公司註冊證明書；(b) 獨立公司註冊登記冊的記錄；(c) 職權證明書；(d) 良好聲譽證明書；(e) 註冊記錄；(f) 合夥協議或契約；(g) 章程文件；或 (h) 其他可靠及獨立來源所提供的相關文件、數據或資料（例如政府機構發出的文件）。通常，成立證明只證明名稱及法律形式，未必證明現時仍然存在，因此可能需要多於一份文件。When verifying a legal person, eight example sources are listed in paragraph 4.3.7: (a) a certificate of incorporation; (b) a record in an independent company registry; (c) a certificate of incumbency; (d) a certificate of good standing; (e) a record of registration; (f) a partnership agreement or deed; (g) a constitutional document; or (h) other relevant documents, data or information from a reliable and independent source (for example a document issued by a government body). An incorporation certificate alone may prove only the name and legal form, and may not prove current existence, so more than one document may be needed.
  • 對信託或類似法律安排，收集的最低資料與核實項目也不完全相同。至少須收集名稱、成立或設立日期、管限法律的司法管轄區、官方識別號碼及文件類別（如有），以及註冊辦事處地址（如適用）。核實名稱、形式、現時存在及約束權力時，可用信託契據、適當登記冊，或指定專業確認。⚖E24For a trust or similar legal arrangement, the minimum information to be collected is not identical to the items that must be verified. For trusts/similar arrangements collect the name, the date of establishment or settlement, the governing jurisdiction, any official identification number and document type, and the registered office if applicable. Verify the name, form, current existence and binding powers using a deed, a suitable register, or a specified professional confirmation.⚖E24
  • 如果受託人代表一個沒有獨立法人地位的信託，去建立關係或進行交易，該受託人通常本身也是客戶，須按其是自然人或法人的身分，依相應要求進行審查。Where a trustee acts for a trust that has no separate legal personality, in establishing a relationship or carrying out a transaction, the trustee is generally also the customer, and must be checked under the relevant individual or legal-person requirements.

必記Remember文件可靠性及關連人士Document reliability and connected parties

  • 用來核實的資料，在提供或取得時必須仍然有效，並按風險採取防偽措施。在場客戶的一般做法，是查閱正本並保留副本；客戶不能提供正本時，須另外確保資料可靠。如果資料是外文，還須採取適當步驟，確認它確能證明身分。⚖E4Verification information must still be current when it is supplied or obtained, and risk-based anti-fraud measures should be applied. Generally sight originals and keep copies for customers who are physically present; where originals cannot be provided, reliability must be ensured in another way. If the identity evidence is in a foreign language, take suitable steps to confirm that it does in fact prove identity.⚖E4
  • 身分文件應有照片。只有在確有例外，而且相關風險已經妥善評估及緩減的情況下，才可以接受沒有照片的文件。Identity documents should contain a photograph. A document without a photograph is acceptable only exceptionally, and only where the associated risks have been properly assessed and mitigated.
  • 對關連人士，須取得其姓名：公司董事、合夥人、信託受託人；其他情況則是高級管理層，或具執行權力的自然人。如果這個人同時是客戶、實益擁有人或代表，便須按較適用的要求去識別及核實，不能只記下一個姓名。Obtain the names of connected parties: directors, partners and trustees, or otherwise senior or executive natural persons. If the same person is also the customer, a beneficial owner or a representative, apply the corresponding identification and verification duties; collecting only a name is not enough.

理解Understand常見文件情況Common documentary situations

  • 旅行證件一般只須保留個人資料頁。如果姓名有改變，須用新文件核實，並與原有出生日期、識別號碼等核對。主要營業地址是指實際的主要營運地點，如果與資料不一致，便須查詢。可靠獨立文件已載有註冊地址，而且並不知道該地址已過期，便可接受作為地址資料。Generally retain the biodata page of a travel document. If the name has changed, verify it with new evidence and corroborate the date of birth and identifier. The principal business address is the actual main operating location; enquire into any inconsistency. A reliable independent document may be used as evidence of a registered address, unless it is known to be outdated.
  • 合夥可以用登記紀錄，或合夥協議、契據來核實。非高風險情況，可用適當的摘錄或刪節版；但登記紀錄未必載有規管權力，因此可能還需要額外文件。對於具有悠久歷史、良好聲譽，而且有大量公開的合伙人及控制人資料的知名機構，在所述條件下，可用專業或行業會員確認。A partnership can be verified using a registration record, or a partnership agreement or deed. In a case that is not high-risk, an appropriate extract or redacted version may be used. Registration may not show the governing powers, so another document may still be needed. For a well-known, reputable organisation with a long history and substantial public information about its partners or controllers, confirmation of professional or trade membership may suffice under the stated conditions.
  • 了解擁有權及控制結構是必須的，但這不表示每一次都要索取擁有權圖，也不表示必須例行核實每一層中間公司的全部資料。應按結構的複雜性、風險，以及現有資料是否足以識別實益擁有人，來決定要取多少。Understanding the ownership and control structure is mandatory. That does not mean an ownership chart must be obtained every time, or that every intermediate company must routinely be verified in full. Decide by complexity, risk, and whether the information is sufficient to identify the beneficial owners.
  • 可靠來源的電子正本可以使用。客戶自行提供的列印本，可能要與登記冊等核對；機構直接從可靠來源下載的，便不必再做同一項核對。翻譯必須可靠、適當，但不一定必須由專業第三方提供。舊文件日後到期，並不會自動要求重新核實，但仍須維持CDD資料更新。Reliable electronic originals may be used. A printout supplied by the customer may need to be corroborated against a registry or similar source, whereas a direct download by the MSO from a reliable source need not repeat that same check. A translation must be appropriate and reliable, but it does not have to come from a professional third party. Later expiry of an old document does not, by itself, automatically require re-verification; CDD information must still be kept current.

理解Understand國籍核實、地址及更名的條件Nationality verification, addresses and name-change conditions

  • 自然人的最低識別資料包括國籍，但「取得」和「核實」不是同一件事。第4.3.3段的一般核實項目，是姓名、出生日期、唯一識別號碼及證件類型，並不是一律要另行核實國籍。不能因此便略去應取得的國籍資料。例如：職員已從身分證抄下國籍，並核實了姓名、出生日期、證件號碼及類型，通常便已符合這項一般核實範圍；但不能因為國籍不是獨立核實項目，便完全不收集國籍。Minimum identification information for an individual includes nationality, but collecting a fact and verifying it are not the same task. The general verification set in paragraph 4.3.3 is name, date of birth, unique identifier and document type; nationality is not universally a separate verification item. Nationality must still be obtained. For example: copying nationality from an identity card, and verifying name, date of birth, document number and type, will usually meet this general verification set. 實益擁有人：擁有權、控制與代他人行事Beneficial owners: ownership, control and acting for another

必記Remember定義及控制標準Definitions and control tests

  • 實益擁有人（beneficial owner）和發牌裡的「最終擁有人」（ultimate owner）不是同一個概念。實益擁有人是最終擁有或控制該客戶的人，或者是該筆交易代其進行的人。自然人客戶通常本身就是自己的實益擁有人；但如果有跡象顯示這個人並不是為自己行事，便須作適當查詢。例如：顧客看起來像是為自己兌換，但其實是依照另一個人的指示，把錢交給那個人，便可能要進一步查這筆交易是代誰進行。⚖B14A beneficial owner is not the same concept as an “ultimate owner” in the licensing sense. A beneficial owner ultimately owns/controls the customer, or is the person on whose behalf the transaction is conducted. An individual customer is normally its own beneficial owner, but indications that the person is not acting for himself or herself require enquiries. For example: a customer who appears to be exchanging money for personal use, but who is in fact acting on another person’s instructions and handing the proceeds to that person, may need further enquiry into the person on whose behalf the transaction is conducted.⚖B14
  • 公司客戶的實益擁有人，包括直接或間接擁有或控制超過25%股本的自然人、可行使或控制超過25%表決權的自然人，或最終控制管理層的自然人；如果公司是代別人行事，該人亦包括在內。超過25%包括25.1%及25.5%，不是到26%才符合。恰好25%單獨並不符合這個比例條件，但仍可能因為最終管理控制等另一準則而符合。例如：某人剛好持有25%股份，而且沒有其他控制權，便不是因為持股而成為實益擁有人；如果同一人可以最終決定管理層，則仍可能按控制準則被識別。⚖B14For a corporate customer, the beneficial owners include individuals who directly or indirectly own or control more than 25% of the capital, or who can exercise or control more than 25% of the voting rights, or who exercise ultimate management control; also the person for whom it acts. More than 25% includes 25.1% and 25.5%; the threshold is not 26%. Exactly 25% does not, by itself, satisfy the percentage test, but another control test may still apply. For example: a person who holds exactly 25% of the shares and has no other control is not a beneficial owner by reason of that shareholding alone; the same person may still have to be identified if that person can ultimately decide management.⚖B14
  • 合夥的實益擁有人，是擁有超過25%資本、利潤或表決權的自然人，或最終控制管理的自然人。對於不是合夥的其他非法團組織，則是最終擁有或控制的自然人，以及該組織代其行事的其他人。For a partnership, the beneficial owners are individuals with more than 25% of the capital, profits or voting rights, or with ultimate management control. For other unincorporated bodies that are not partnerships, they are the individuals who ultimately own or control the body, and the persons for whom the body acts.
  • 信託的實益擁有人範圍，不能只看成「持股超過25%的人」。對信託財產有既得權益的受益人或受益人類別、財產授予人、受託人、保護人或執行人，以及最終控制的自然人，都要按定義識別。如果受益人是按特徵或類別指定的，須有足夠資料，以便在支付或行使既得權利時，能夠確定其身分。⚖B14⚖E20The beneficial owners of a trust are not simply “anyone who owns more than 25%”. For trusts: vested-interest beneficiaries or classes, the settlor, the trustee, the protector or enforcer, and individuals with ultimate control, must all be identified under their definitions. Where beneficiaries are designated by characteristics or class, obtain enough information to establish identity at the time of payout or of the exercise of vested rights.⚖B14⚖E20
  • 如果法人客戶沒有任何自然人符合實益擁有人的定義，須識別高級管理人員，並採取合理措施核實其身分。不能只因為沒有人持股超過25%，便立即停止追查控制權。識別這些高級管理人員，是在沒有符合定義的實益擁有人時的替代步驟，並不是把他們自動重新定義為實益擁有人。If no natural person meets the beneficial-owner definition for a legal-person customer, identify the senior managing officials and take reasonable measures to verify their identity. The mere absence of a shareholder above 25% does not, by itself, end the search for control. Identifying those officials is a fallback where no one meets the definition; it does not automatically redefine them as beneficial owners.

必記Remember高風險的處理Responding to high risk

  • 如果情況本質上屬於高風險，或關長以書面通知顯示高風險，便須採取相稱的加強盡職審查（EDD）。建立或繼續高風險關係，須取得高級管理層批准，並加強持續監察；如果措施仍不足夠，便再以限制交易或戶口功能等方式，減低剩餘風險。Apply proportionate enhanced due diligence (EDD) where the circumstances themselves, or a written notice from the CCE, indicate high risk. Senior approval is required to establish or continue a high-risk relationship, and monitoring must be enhanced. Where residual risk remains, impose further limits or controls, such as limits on transactions or account functions.
  • 一般高風險情況，和特定政治人物情況，要求並不相同。一般高風險情況，法例要求管理層批准，再加上確定財富及資金來源，或採其他額外緩解措施。特定政治人物情況則必須完成全部指定措施，不能只任選其中一項。例如：一般高風險客戶，可以在批准之外，用其他額外緩解措施代替確定財富來源；但如果客戶是非香港政治人物，便不能用「選一項額外措施」來代替全部指定要求。⚖B17General high-risk cases, and specified politically exposed person (PEP) cases, do not have the same requirements. Specified PEP cases require all of the prescribed measures, rather than a choice of one of them. 必記Remember財富來源與資金來源Source of wealth versus source of funds

  • 財富來源（SOW）問的是：這個人的整體資產是怎樣累積起來的，例如事業、投資或繼承所形成的總財富。重點是整個財富體系的來源，而不是只看這一筆錢從哪家銀行轉來。Source of wealth (SOW) asks how the person’s overall body of assets was accumulated, for example through business, investments or inheritance. The focus is the origin of the wealth as a whole, not merely the bank from which a particular sum was sent.
  • 資金來源（SOF）問的是：這次關係或交易所涉及的特定資金或資產，是怎樣產生的。只記下「從某銀行轉來」並不足夠，還要了解產生這些資金的活動，以及取得資金的實質理由。例如：客戶說這一筆錢來自某銀行轉帳，還要再問這筆錢是薪金、賣樓，還是其他活動得來的。Source of funds (SOF) asks how the specific money or assets involved in this relationship or transaction were generated. Naming the sending bank alone is not enough; understand the activity that generated the funds, and their substantive provenance. For example: if a customer says that a sum came by a bank transfer, still ask whether that money came from salary, a property sale, or some other activity.
  • 採取哪些措施，按風險決定：可以向客戶取得資料，並在有公開的資產或收入申報時加以核對。並非所有官方財產申報都是公開的；客戶沒有交出某一份非公開申報，並不自動證明行為不當。Choose measures according to risk: obtain information from the customer, and corroborate it against public asset or income declarations where those are available. Not every official declaration is public. The absence of a particular non-public declaration is not, by itself, proof of wrongdoing.

必記Remember政治人物的範圍Who is a PEP

  • 非香港政治人物，是在香港以外獲委以重要公職的人，現任或曾任都包括在內。例子包括國家或政府首長、高級政界、政府、司法或軍方人員、國有企業高層，以及重要政黨官員；不包括中級或初級人員。A non-Hong Kong PEP is a person who holds, or has held, a prominent public function outside Hong Kong. Middle-ranking or junior officials are excluded.
  • 香港政治人物，是在香港擔任或曾經擔任重要公職的人，例如政府首長、資深從政者、高級政府或司法官員、政府擁有企業的高級行政人員，以及重要政黨幹事；同樣不包括中級或更低級官員。國際組織政治人物，包括獲委重要職能的董事、副董事、董事會成員，或同等的高級管理人員，同樣不包括中級或更低級官員。A Hong Kong PEP is a person who holds, or has held, a prominent public function in Hong Kong, including heads of government, senior politicians, senior government or judicial officials, senior executives of government-owned corporations, and important party officials. Middle-ranking or more junior officials are excluded. An international-organisation PEP holds, or has held, a prominent function, including as a director, deputy director, board member or equivalent senior manager, and middle-ranking or more junior officials are likewise excluded.
  • 範圍還包括配偶或伴侶、子女、父母、子女的配偶或伴侶，以及關係密切的人。密切聯繫包括緊密的業務關係、共同實益擁有法人或信託，或者實益擁有為該政治人物的利益而設立的法人或信託。The scope also includes spouses or partners, children, parents, children’s spouses or partners, and close associates. 必記Remember依賴與外判的分別Reliance versus outsourcing

  • 依賴中介人，和外判或代理，不是同一種安排。中介人通常自己已有獨立的客戶關係，並按其自身程序做客戶盡職審查；外判或代理則按本機構的程序進行，並受本機構控制。無論哪一種安排，最終的客戶盡職審查責任，始終由本機構承擔。Reliance on an intermediary is not the same arrangement as outsourcing or agency. An intermediary normally has its own independent customer relationship and applies its own procedures. Outsourcing or agency applies the MSO’s procedures under the MSO’s control. In either case, ultimate CDD responsibility stays with the MSO.
  • 可以依賴中介人執行的，是附表2第2條的部分或全部客戶盡職審查，但不能把第5條的持續監察責任也交給中介人。例如：可以讓合資格中介人先完成開戶時的身分核實，但此後對交易是否異常的持續監察，仍須由本機構自己負責。⚖B34An intermediary can be relied on for some, but not all, of the CDD duties. Reliance may cover any part of s.2 CDD, but not the MSO’s s.5 ongoing-monitoring responsibility. For example: a qualifying intermediary may complete identity verification at onboarding, but ongoing monitoring of whether transactions are unusual remains the MSO’s own responsibility.⚖B34
  • 須取得書面確認，證明中介人同意，以及同意承擔哪些措施，並且信納對方可以按要求、不延誤地提供文件。有關措施完成後，須立即取得中介人所收集的資料或資訊；不必在同一時間取得全部底層文件的副本。⚖W8Written confirmation is required, not merely an informal understanding. Obtain written confirmation of consent and agreed measures, and be satisfied that documents will be supplied on request without delay. Obtain the collected data or information immediately after the measure is completed; simultaneous copies of every underlying document are not required.⚖W8
  • 還須取得承諾，在關係期間，以及關係完結後至少5年，或關長指定的較長期間，保留底層的客戶盡職審查資料，並且抽查可否及時供應。終止依賴時，須立即取回全部資料；如果對中介人的可靠性有疑問，便須覆核該中介人，並補做所需的客戶盡職審查。Also obtain an undertaking to retain the underlying CDD information for the duration of the relationship and for at least five years afterwards, or any longer period specified by the CCE, and sample-test whether the information can be retrieved in time. On termination, obtain all of the information immediately. If there is doubt about the intermediary’s reliability, review the intermediary and perform any necessary CDD.

持續監察與原有客戶Ongoing monitoring and pre-existing customers

必記Remember持續CDD及交易監察Ongoing CDD and transaction monitoring

  • 持續的客戶盡職審查，是不時更新客戶資料，使資料保持相關及最新；並不是每次覆核都要重新核實已經確認的身分，除非對舊資料產生疑問。高風險客戶至少每年覆核一次。對於休眠客戶，現有的盡職審查紀錄不必作定期覆核，但關係重新啟用時應覆核；政策及程序應清楚界定何謂休眠客戶。Ongoing CDD means keeping customer information relevant and current. It does not require routine re-verification of an identity that has already been verified, unless doubts arise about the old information. Review high-risk customers at least annually. Regular review of a dormant customer’s existing CDD records is unnecessary, but a review should be carried out when the relationship is reactivated. Policies and procedures should clearly define what a dormant customer is.
  • 交易監察有兩方面，缺一不可。一方面檢查交易與客戶業務、風險及資金來源是否一致，另一方面識別複雜、異常大額，或呈異常模式、而且沒有明顯經濟或合法目的的交易，調查其背景及目的，並把結果書面記錄。⚖E33⚖E41Transaction monitoring has two sides, and both matter. Monitoring checks consistency with the customer’s business, risk and sources of funds, and also identifies complex, unusually large, or unusual-pattern transactions that have no apparent economic or lawful purpose, examining their background and purpose and recording the findings.⚖E33⚖E41
  • 監察宜從整段關係來看，跨越不同戶口及關連客戶，而不是只逐筆分開來看。參數包括金額或頻率、相連的系列、對手、資金的來源地或目的地，以及平常的戶口活動，並且須定期覆核及作獨立驗證。Prefer a holistic view of the relationship, across accounts and related customers, rather than looking at isolated transactions. Parameters include amount or frequency, series of transactions, counterparties, geographical origin or destination, and normal account activity, and they should be reviewed regularly and independently validated.
  • 有兩類情況，任何一類出現，都須作適當查詢：交易與客戶背景不符，或屬異常而且沒有明顯目的的活動。如果解釋令人滿意，可以不再採取進一步行動，但仍應考慮更新風險；如果不能滿意解釋，而且已經形成懷疑，便須向聯合財富情報組舉報，並記錄結果及理由。⚖E6Either of two situations calls for appropriate enquiries. Either profile inconsistency or unusual activity lacking an apparent purpose must be followed up. A satisfactory explanation may mean that no further action is required, but the risk rating may still need to be updated. If the explanation cannot be accepted and suspicion arises, report to the JFIU and document the findings and reasons.⚖E6

必記Remember接受條件及審查Acceptance conditions and checks

  • 這裡所說的第三方，是指客戶以外的人。由第三方付款，可能會掩蓋真正的實益擁有人或資金來源，因此應認真考慮拒絕。如果沒有足夠措施管理這種固有的高風險，並且符合有關規定，便不應接受。A third party, in this context, is anyone other than the customer. Payment by a third party can conceal the true beneficial owner or the source of funds, so refusal should be considered seriously. Do not accept the payment where adequate measures cannot manage the inherent high risk and meet the requirements.
  • 只有在例外、而且情況合法、又合理符合客戶背景及正常商業做法時，才可以接受第三方付款，並且須依據高級管理層批准的詳細政策，以及嚴格的管理批准來執行。政策的五項內容見下文。Accept a third-party payment only exceptionally, in legitimate circumstances that are reasonably consistent with the customer’s profile and with normal commercial practice, and only under detailed policies approved by senior management and under stringent management approval. The five policy components are set out below.
  • 接受之前，須評估付款原因及是否有必要；按風險合理核實第三方的身分，以及與客戶的關係；並由負責打擊洗錢的高級管理層批准，同時記錄查詢、佐證及批准。要記住，這個第三方本身，也可能就是真正的實益擁有人。Before accepting, assess the rationale and the necessity of the payment; reasonably verify the third party’s identity and relationship with the customer on a risk-sensitive basis; obtain approval from senior management responsible for anti-money laundering; and document the enquiries, corroboration and approval. For example: a husband paying for a reasonable family remittance by his wife may be a lower-risk illustration, but it still has to be assessed under the policy and is not automatically acceptable.

  • 有相關戶口時，須使用該戶口號碼；只有在沒有該戶口時，才使用由相應匯款機構或收款機構編配、可追查該筆交易的獨特參考號碼。這項先後條件，同時適用於匯款人及收款人的所需資料，不是可以任意選一個方便的號碼。例如：客戶在本機構已有戶口，付款訊息便應使用該戶口號碼，而不是另編一個內部參考號碼來代替。Use the relevant account number when one exists. Only if there is no such account may the traceable unique reference assigned by the appropriate ordering or beneficiary institution be used. This order of priority applies to the required information on both the originator and the recipient; it is not a free choice of whichever number is convenient. For example: if the customer already has an account with the institution, the payment message should carry that account number, and not a separately invented internal reference in its place.
  • 客戶識別號碼，是機構用來唯一識別匯款人的號碼，必須指向一份紀錄，而該紀錄至少載有以下其中一項：地址、身分證明文件號碼，或出生日期及地點。獨特交易參考號碼，則是用來追查該筆交易。兩者不是同一種號碼。A customer identification number uniquely identifies the originator to the institution, and must point to a record containing at least one of the following: an address, an identification-document number, or date and place of birth. A unique transaction reference traces the transaction itself. The two identifiers serve different purposes.
  • 直接撥付機制（cover payment，例如 MT202COV 付款）仍然要遵守本章適用的電傳要求。這只是付款機制的名稱，不是「掩蓋付款」或洗錢的同義詞。匯款機構還須有有效程序，防止匯出不合規的電傳：合理檢查本地或跨境轉帳所需雙方資料是否缺漏，按風險處理缺漏的轉帳，並及時修補管控上的缺失，包括在適當時作定期的內控或審核測試。Cover-payment mechanisms, such as MT202COV payments, remain subject to the applicable Chapter 10 wire-transfer requirements. The term names a payment mechanism; it is not a synonym for concealment or money laundering. Ordering institutions also need effective procedures to prevent non-compliant outgoing wires: reasonable checks for missing required information on both sides of domestic or cross-border transfers, risk-based handling of incomplete transfers, and timely repair of control gaps, including appropriate regular control or audit testing.

必記Remember本地轉帳的範圍及兩層資料要求Domestic scope and two levels of information requirements

  • 「本地」電傳，不能只看發款人和受款人在哪裏。匯款機構、收款機構，以及每一間中介機構，都必須是位於香港、而且符合《打擊洗錢條例》定義的金融機構。只要鏈中出現境外中介機構，便不符合這項本地電傳的定義。例如：發款人和受款人都在香港，但如果中間經過一間境外銀行，這筆轉帳便不再屬於這裡所說的本地電傳。Whether a wire is “domestic” is not decided only by where the originator and the recipient are. The ordering institution, the beneficiary institution and every intermediary must be financial institutions as defined by the AMLO, and they must all be located in Hong Kong. An overseas intermediary takes the chain outside this domestic-wire definition. For example: even if both the originator and the recipient are in Hong Kong, a transfer that passes through an overseas bank is no longer a domestic wire in this sense.
  • 本地電傳其實有兩層資料要求，不能只記較窄的法例文字，便忽略指引。條例附表2第12(6)及(9)條允許本地轉帳隨附發款人戶口或獨特參考號碼，收款機構則須追索缺漏的該號碼。文件03第10.11至10.12段只簡化發款人資料，並沒有免除所需的受款人資料；第10.20段還要求收款機構追索本地或跨境轉帳中，雙方缺漏的所需資料。這兩層要求都要分開認識：較窄的法例文字，並不能成為可以忽略指引的理由。⚖B20Domestic wires in fact have two layers of information requirements, and the narrower statutory wording must not be used to ignore the Guideline. Schedule 2 sections 12(6) and (9) permit the originator account or unique reference number to accompany a domestic wire, and require the beneficiary institution to obtain that number if it is missing. Document 03 paragraphs 10.11 to 10.12 simplify originator information, without exempting the required recipient information; paragraph 10.20 requires the beneficiary institution to obtain missing required information on either side, for domestic or cross-border wires. Recognise both levels: the narrower statutory wording does not justify ignoring the Guideline.⚖B20

非電傳匯款：發款人與必備紀錄Non-wire remittances: originator and required records

必記Remember匯款的特別要求Special remittance requirements

  • 「匯款」和「電傳轉帳」不是同義詞。匯款交易，是把錢送往或安排送往香港以外的地方；電傳轉帳，則是按電子機構鏈來定義。屬於電傳的，按第12條處理；非電傳的對外匯款，則按第13條處理。“Remittance” and “wire transfer” are not synonymous. A remittance sends, or arranges to send, money outside Hong Kong. A wire transfer is defined by the electronic institutional chain. Apply section 12 to wires, and section 13 to non-wire outward remittances.
  • 非電傳的對外匯款，如果達港幣8,000元或等值外幣，須在執行前，用身分文件識別及核實發款人。發款人是提供款項的戶口擁有人；如果沒有戶口，則是發出指示的人。例如：顧客以現金委託一筆港幣8,000元、不是經電傳鏈處理的對外匯款，執行前便須核實發款人，不能只記住一般非經常交易的港幣120,000元門檻。⚖B19⚖B36⚖B35A non-wire outward remittance has its own threshold, which is not the general occasional-transaction figure of HK$120,000. Before a non-wire outward remittance of at least HK$8,000 or equivalent, identify and verify the originator using an identity document. The originator is the holder of the account that funds the remittance or, if there is no account, the person who gives the instructions. For example: a customer who tenders cash for a HK$8,000 outward remittance that is not processed through a wire chain must be identified and verified before execution; the general HK$120,000 occasional-transaction threshold is not the relevant line here.⚖B19⚖B36⚖B35
  • 還須在執行前，記錄發款人姓名、證件號碼（如果是旅行證件，還要另記簽發地）、地址、幣種及金額；指示的接收日期及時間、受款人姓名及地址，以及交付方式。這些資料須可供關長查閱。Before execution, also record the originator’s name, identity-document number (and, for a travel document, the place of issue), address, currency and amount, the date and time when the instruction was received, the recipient’s name and address, and the delivery method. The information must be available to the CCE.

必記Remember概念及使用指標的方法Concept and use of indicators

  • 貿易洗錢（TBML）是利用貿易交易，掩飾犯罪得益或轉移價值，使非法來源看起來合法，或資助非法活動。常見手法包括虛假貿易、錯報價格、數量或品質、重複單據，或使用空殼公司。實際貨物、合約、單據與付款，應能合理地互相對應。⚖E32Trade-based money laundering uses trade transactions to hide criminal proceeds or to move value, so that an illegal origin looks legitimate, or so that illegal activity can be financed. TBML disguises criminal proceeds and moves value through trade, including through fictitious trade, misreported price, quantity or quality, duplicate documents, and shell companies. Goods, contracts, documents and payments should have a reasonable relationship to one another.⚖E32
  • 風險指標只是警號，單獨一項並不能證明已經犯罪。清單並非盡錄，機構也未必能看到全部資料。須連同客戶、交易，以及可以取得的外部資料，作整體判斷；如果多個指標同時出現，便應提高關注，並及時呈報管理層。Indicators are warning signs, not proof on their own. The lists are not exhaustive, and an MSO may not be able to observe every piece of data. Assess the customer, the transaction and any available external information as a whole.
