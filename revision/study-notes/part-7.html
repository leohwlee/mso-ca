01

可疑交易舉報：觸發條件及時間Suspicious transaction reporting: trigger and timing

必記Remember知悉、懷疑與法定義務Knowledge, suspicion and the statutory duty

  • 如果知道或懷疑某財產與販毒、可公訴罪行或恐怖分子財產有關，便須在合理情況下盡快向聯合財富情報組（JFIU）披露，並交代懷疑的依據。這包括財產全部或部分、直接或間接代表販毒或可公訴罪行的得益，曾經用於或擬用於該等罪行，或屬恐怖分子財產。財產不限於現金，也包括貨品、土地及權利；完整定義見第1部分「洗錢」。If you know or suspect that property is connected with drug trafficking, an indictable offence or terrorist property, you must disclose that to the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (JFIU) as soon as is reasonable, and explain the basis of the knowledge or suspicion. This covers property that wholly or partly, and directly or indirectly, represents proceeds of drug trafficking or an indictable offence, that was or is intended to be used in such an offence, or that is terrorist property. Property is not limited to cash; it also includes goods, land and rights. See Part 1, on money laundering, for the full definition.
  • 「知悉」和「懷疑」的門檻都不要求你先證明資金必定來自犯罪，也不要求先知道確切的上游罪行。知悉可以包括實際知道，也可以包括合理人士會據以認知事實的情況，或會促使合理人士進一步查詢的情況。懷疑則較為主觀，而且低於以確實證據證明。例如：職員覺得一筆匯款「不對勁」，但還說不出對方犯了哪一條罪，這已經可以構成須要考慮的懷疑，不必等到有確實證據才處理。Neither “knowledge” nor “suspicion” requires you first to prove that the funds came from crime, or first to identify the exact predicate offence. Knowledge includes actual knowledge, and also circumstances that would indicate the facts to a reasonable person, or that would put a reasonable person on inquiry. Suspicion is more subjective, and falls short of firm proof. Identifying the exact predicate crime, or proving a criminal origin, is not a prerequisite. For example: a staff member may feel that a remittance “is not right”, without being able to name the underlying offence. That can already be suspicion that needs to be considered. You do not have to wait for firm proof.
  • 舉報沒有最低金額門檻。即使交易還沒有完成、已被拒絕，或只是一次嘗試，也可以觸發舉報。不能因為沒有交易入帳，或金額低於盡職審查門檻，就不報。例如：顧客要匯出港幣3,000元，職員已經懷疑資金涉及犯罪，即使這筆交易最後被拒絕、也沒有入帳，仍可能須要舉報，不能因為「沒做成」或「未達12萬元」就不處理。There is no minimum amount for making a report. An attempted, rejected or uncompleted transaction may still trigger reporting. The duty is not removed merely because no transaction was booked, or because the amount is below a CDD threshold. For example: a customer tries to remit HK$3,000 and staff already suspect the funds are connected with crime. Even if the transaction is then refused and never booked, a report may still be required. “It was not completed” or “it is below HK$120,000” is not a reason to leave it unreported.
  • 知悉或懷疑而不披露，最高可監禁3個月及罰款港幣50,000元。這與洗錢罪，以及通風報訊罪的刑罰並不相同，不要記混。Failure to disclose knowledge or suspicion carries a maximum of three months’ imprisonment and a fine of HK$50,000. This is not the same penalty as the money-laundering offence, or the tipping-off offence, and the three should be kept distinct.

02

內部報告與MLRO評估Internal reporting and MLRO assessment

必記Remember從員工到JFIUFrom staff to the JFIU

  • 員工必須知道洗錢報告主任是誰，以及怎樣提交內部報告；報告還必須不受不當延誤地送到該人手上。不是負責洗錢舉報或合規職能的主管，在任何情況下都不得把員工的報告篩走。匯報線應盡量短。例如：櫃檯職員向主管說「這筆交易很可疑」，主管覺得「先不要驚動上面」便把報告壓下，這樣是不可以的。內部報告必須送到 MLRO，不能在中間被篩掉。Staff need to know who the MLRO is, and how to make an internal report. The report must then reach the MLRO without undue delay. Under no circumstances should a manager who does not have reporting or compliance responsibility filter those reports out. Reporting lines should be as short as possible. For example: a counter clerk tells a supervisor that a transaction looks suspicious, and the supervisor holds the report back so as “not to alarm senior management”. That is not allowed. The internal report must reach the MLRO, and must not be filtered out on the way.
  • 員工按照機構程序，向洗錢報告主任報告懷疑之後，便已履行相關的個人法定舉報義務。內部報告應包括充分的客戶詳情，以及引起懷疑的資料。洗錢報告主任應確認已經收妥，並提醒員工避免通風報訊；這一句英文用 should，中文則用「必須」，複習時要記得兩種文本的用字並不完全相同。⚖U4Staff who report suspicion to the MLRO under the institution’s procedures have discharged the relevant personal statutory reporting duty. Internal reports should include full customer details and the grounds for suspicion. The MLRO should acknowledge receipt and remind staff about tipping off (the Chinese uses 必須). When revising, remember that the English and Chinese wording of this reminder is not identical.⚖U4
  • 洗錢報告主任須採取合理步驟，考慮全部相關資料，包括盡職審查、持續監察、關連戶口的交易、歷史指示和關係年期。但搜尋資料必須與及時舉報取得平衡，不能無限等待「收齊所有資料」才決定。例如：還有一些歷史紀錄要調檔，並不表示可以一等再等、把已經形成的懷疑一直拖着不報。⚖W11The MLRO must take reasonable steps to consider all relevant information, including CDD, ongoing monitoring, activity on connected accounts, historical instructions and the length of the relationship. Balance information gathering with prompt reporting rather than waiting indefinitely for completeness. For example: the fact that some historical records still have to be retrieved does not justify waiting on and on, and leaving an already formed suspicion unreported.⚖W11
  • 評估之後如果仍然有知悉或懷疑，應盡快向 JFIU 披露。即使最後決定不報，也必須保留考慮過程、理由及行動的紀錄。如果涉及緊急移走款項或關閉戶口等情況，應在報告上標明；在特殊緊急情況，應考慮先以電話通知。If knowledge or suspicion remains after the assessment, disclose it promptly to the JFIU. If the decision is not to report, still keep a record of the deliberations, the reasons and the actions taken. Urgent movements of funds, or account closures, should be flagged in the report. In an exceptional urgent case, an initial telephone notification should be considered.
  • 如果同一間金錢服務經營者同時控制一筆電傳轉帳的兩端，舉報範圍可能不止香港。該機構亦應在受該可疑轉帳影響的司法管轄區提交報告，並向當地的財富情報組提供相關交易資料。例如：一筆匯款由你在香港這端匯出，又由你在另一地的業務接收，若該筆轉帳可疑，除了向香港 JFIU 披露外，還應考慮在受影響的對方司法管轄區作出報告。⚖E30If the same money service operator controls both ends of a wire transfer, reporting may not be limited to Hong Kong. An MSO controlling both sides of a wire transfer should also report in jurisdictions affected by the suspicious transfer and make relevant transaction information available to their FIUs. For example: you send a remittance from the Hong Kong end and also receive it through your business in another place. If the transfer is suspicious, disclosure to the Hong Kong JFIU may not be the end of the matter; reporting in the other affected jurisdiction should also be considered.⚖E30

  • 可疑交易報告的質素要持續改善，並應參考 JFIU 季度報告裡的分析、關注事項及回饋，以及海關不時發出的指引。這項季度回饋，與金錢服務經營者的半年定期申報，是兩件不同的事，目的和周期都不一樣。The quality of STRs should be kept under review. 理解Understand真誠評估後決定不報告的條件Conditions when deciding in good faith not to report

  • 洗錢報告主任如果真誠行事，並且在考慮全部可得資料後，結論是沒有懷疑，一般不大可能只因為沒有提交可疑交易報告而負上刑責。但必須妥存考慮過程及行動紀錄，以證明當時是合理行事。這不是只要寫一句「不懷疑」便得到絕對免責，也不是可以忽略尚未查明的警號。對應：指引 paragraph 7.20。例如：檔案裡只寫「沒有懷疑」，但沒有顯示曾考慮過哪些資料、為什麼排除那些警號，這樣並不足夠證明當時是合理行事。If the MLRO acts in good faith and, after considering all available information, concludes that there is no suspicion, criminal liability for not filing an STR is generally unlikely. Proper records of the deliberations and of the actions taken are still vital, because they demonstrate that the MLRO acted reasonably. Merely writing down “no suspicion” is not absolute immunity, and it is not permission to disregard unresolved indicators. This is Guideline paragraph 7.20. For example: a file note that only says “no suspicion”, without showing what information was considered or why warning signs were ruled out, is not enough to demonstrate that the decision was reasonable.

必記Remember可疑交易報告制度的三項組成The three components of the STR reporting system

  • 為了履行法定舉報責任，並妥善管理和減低可疑交易報告所涉客戶或交易的風險，金錢服務經營者應執行適當的打擊洗錢及恐怖分子資金籌集制度。這套制度應包括三項：(a) 委任一名洗錢報告主任；(b) 就內部報告、向 JFIU 作出報告、作出報告後如何減低風險，以及如何防止通風報訊，執行清晰的政策和程序；(c) 備存內部報告及可疑交易報告的妥善紀錄。對應：指引 paragraph 7.7。To fulfil the statutory reporting duty, and to manage and mitigate the risks of any customer or transaction involved in an STR, an MSO should implement appropriate AML/CFT systems. Those systems should include three components: (a) appointment of an MLRO; (b) clear policies and procedures covering internal reporting, reporting to the JFIU, post-reporting risk mitigation and prevention of tipping off; and (c) proper records of internal reports and STRs. This is Guideline paragraph 7.7.
  • 除此以外，還須制訂措施，持續查核這套舉報制度是否符合法律及監管規定，並且行之有效。措施的類別及程度，應與洗錢或恐怖分子資金籌集風險，以及業務的性質和規模相稱。paragraph 7.7 只列出上面三項；員工培訓見第9章，獨立審核見第3章，屬於其他制度要求，不要把它們算進這三項裡面。對應：指引 paragraph 7.8。In addition, measures must be put in place to check, on an ongoing basis, that these reporting systems comply with legal and regulatory requirements and that they operate effectively. The type and extent of those measures should suit the money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk, and the nature and size of the business. Paragraph 7.7 lists only the three components above. Staff training (Chapter 9) and independent audit (Chapter 3) are separate system requirements, and should not be counted as part of those three. This is Guideline paragraph 7.8.

通風報訊與向客戶查詢的界線Tipping off and enquiries to customers

必記Remember避免妨礙調查Avoid prejudicing an investigation

  • 通風報訊，簡單來說，就是在知道或懷疑已經有披露之後，再把可能妨礙調查的資料透露出去。這可以構成罪行，最高可監禁3年及罰款。即使內部已經提出懷疑，但報告尚未送到 JFIU，同樣可以落入這個範圍。例如：職員已向 MLRO 提交內部報告，顧客隨後來問「是不是有人在查我」。如果這時把「我們已經向上報告」告訴顧客，因而可能妨礙調查，即使 STR 還未交到 JFIU，仍可能構成通風報訊。⚖W10Tipping off, in plain terms, is revealing information that is likely to prejudice an investigation, after you know or suspect that a disclosure has been made. Revealing information likely to prejudice an investigation after knowing/suspecting a disclosure has been made may constitute tipping off, punishable by up to three years and a fine. The rule also covers the situation in which suspicion has already been raised internally, but the STR has not yet reached the JFIU. For example: a staff member has already made an internal report to the MLRO, and the customer later asks “is someone investigating me?”. Telling the customer that “we have already reported this upwards”, in a way that may prejudice an investigation, may still be tipping off, even if the STR has not yet been filed with the JFIU.⚖W10
  • 適當而且出於真誠的查詢，本身並不構成通風報訊。但如果合理地相信，繼續做盡職審查會讓客戶察覺，便可以停止追查，記錄這個判斷的依據，並提交可疑交易報告。例如：再追問下去，很可能會讓顧客明白機構已經在懷疑他，這時可以停止繼續查詢，把評估記錄下來，並提交 STR。這與主動向顧客透露「我們已報案」並不是同一回事。Proper enquiries made in good faith are not, themselves, tipping off. If, however, it is reasonably believed that continuing with CDD would tip the customer off, the MSO may stop pursuing those enquiries, document the assessment, and file an STR. For example: further questioning is likely to make the customer realise that the institution already suspects him. In that situation, the enquiries may be stopped, the assessment recorded, and an STR filed. That is not the same as volunteering to the customer that “we have already reported this”.

報告之後：同意、抗辯與持續風險After reporting: consent, defence and continuing risk

必記Remember報告不會自動清除風險Reporting does not clear the relationship

  • 向 JFIU 提交報告之後，並不表示這段關係的風險已經自動消失，也不是每份報告都必然獲得同意。JFIU 會確認收到 STR。如果不需要立即採取行動（例如就戶口發出限制令），一般會給予同意，讓戶口繼續運作；否則機構應採取適當行動，並按需要徵詢法律意見。這是指引 paragraph 7.24 所述的處理方式，不是保證每份 STR 都會獲同意，或自動解除限制。Filing a report with the JFIU does not mean that the risk in the relationship has automatically disappeared, and it does not mean that consent will be given in every case. The JFIU acknowledges receipt of the STR. If imminent action, such as a restraint order over the account, is not necessary, consent to operate the account will usually be given. Otherwise the MSO should act appropriately and, where necessary, seek legal advice. This is the approach described in Guideline paragraph 7.24. It is not a guarantee of consent, or an automatic removal of restrictions, for every STR.
  • 法定抗辯是有條件的，不能把「有交 STR」當成無條件免責。條件是：在作出該等被披露的行為之前先報告，並在取得 JFIU 同意之後才行事；或者，在行為之後，主動在合理情況下盡快報告。The statutory defence is conditional. Filing an STR is not unconditional immunity. The conditions are: report before the acts that are being disclosed, and then act with the JFIU’s consent; or, after the acts, report on the MSO’s own initiative as soon as is reasonable.
  • 提交 STR 之後，應立即適當覆核這段關係，並採取風險緩減措施；有需要時交由高級管理層決定，不能等到 JFIU 回覆才處理。JFIU 給予同意，並不是證明這個戶口已經沒有風險，也不免除其他法律、監管及聲譽方面的風險。例如：報告交出去的當天，機構便應檢視是否繼續與該客戶往來、要不要加強監察；不能把「等 JFIU 回覆」當成在這段時間甚麼都不用做。Once an STR has been filed, the relationship should be reviewed promptly and appropriate mitigation applied, escalating the matter to senior management where necessary, rather than waiting for JFIU feedback. Consent from the JFIU is not a clean bill of health, and it does not remove other legal, regulatory or reputational risks. For example: on the day the report is filed, the institution should already be considering whether to continue the relationship and whether monitoring should be intensified. “Waiting for the JFIU to reply” is not a reason to do nothing in the meantime.
  • 如果計劃終止這段關係，建議在初次 STR 裡說明。之後如果再出現同類或不同的可疑事件，仍須向洗錢報告主任報告，由他決定是否進一步向 JFIU 報告。不能因為已經交過一份 STR，就把後來的新事件略過。If termination of the relationship is intended, it is recommended that this be stated in the initial STR. Later suspicious events, whether similar or different, must still be reported to the MLRO, who then decides whether a further report to the JFIU is needed. An earlier STR does not mean that later events can be ignored.

  • 盡職審查紀錄要能夠重現當時是怎樣識別和評估客戶的。它包括客戶、實益擁有人、受款人、代表客戶行事的人及關連人士的識別和核實文件或資料，也包括簡化及加強審查、風險評估、關係目的及性質、重要業務通訊，以及對異常交易背景與目的的分析結果。CDD records should make it possible to reconstruct how the customer was identified and assessed.   • 全部向洗錢報告主任作出的內部報告，以及全部向 JFIU 提交的報告，都必須保存。內部報告紀錄應包括日期、經手職員、評估結果、有否向 JFIU 報告，以及文件存放位置；STR 登記則應包括日期、報告人及文件存放位置。兩份登記可以合併。All internal reports made to the MLRO, and all reports made to the JFIU, must be retained. Internal-report records should show the dates, the staff who handled the report, the assessment, whether an STR followed, and where the documents are kept. The STR register should show the date, the reporter and the document locators. The two registers may be combined.

保存期限：起算點不同Retention periods: different starting points

必記Remember五年與三年的分別Distinguish five years from three

  • 業務關係的盡職審查紀錄，保存期要分兩段來看：在整段關係期間都要保存，並且在關係結束之後，至少再保存5年。CDD records for a business relationship have two stages. They must be kept throughout the relationship, and they must then be kept for at least a further five years after the relationship ends.
  • 達到盡職審查門檻的非經常交易，其盡職審查紀錄從交易完成之日起，至少保存5年。這裏的門檻是：電傳或虛擬資產轉帳為港幣8,000元；其他類型交易為港幣120,000元。For an occasional transaction that reaches a CDD threshold, the CDD records must be kept for at least five years after the transaction is completed. The thresholds here are HK$8,000 for a wire or virtual-asset transfer, and HK$120,000 for other types of transaction.
  • 交易紀錄是另一個起算點：每宗交易完成後至少保存5年；即使關係在中途結束，也不會把這段保存期縮短。培訓紀錄則至少保存3年，不要把「三年」和「五年」記混。例如：顧客在2020年做了一筆匯款，即使2021年已經終止往來，該筆交易紀錄仍須至少保存到2025年該交易完成日之後，不能因為關係已結束就提早銷毀。Transaction records have a different starting point. They must be kept for at least five years after each transaction is completed. Ending the relationship in the meantime does not shorten that period. Training records, by contrast, are kept for at least three years. Do not mix the three-year period up with the five-year periods. For example: a customer makes a remittance in 2020 and the relationship ends in 2021. The records of that transaction must still be kept for at least five years after the 2020 completion date, into 2025. They cannot be destroyed early just because the relationship has already ended.
  • 關長可以用書面要求，就指定客戶或交易延長保存期，原因包括正在進行的調查，或通知上指明的其他目的。即使紀錄存放在境外，仍須符合香港的要求。The CCE may, by written notice, require a longer retention period for specified customers or transactions, including where there is an ongoing investigation, or for another purpose specified in the notice. Storing the records offshore does not remove the Hong Kong requirements.
  • 即使盡職審查是由中介人代做，備存紀錄的責任仍在本機構。必須立即取得審查資料，並確保在接獲要求後，可在合理切實可行的範圍內盡快取得文件。中介服務終止時，亦應確保把文件及紀錄取回。例如：你依賴一間合資格中介人核實客戶，後來中介合約結束，不能說「文件在對方那裡」便算。本機構仍須負責備存，並應在服務終止時把紀錄取回。⚖W8Even if an intermediary carries out the CDD, the duty to keep the records stays with the institution. The MSO remains responsible when intermediaries perform CDD. CDD information must be obtained immediately and requested documents supplied as soon as reasonably practicable. The MSO should also ensure that the records are obtained when the intermediary’s services come to an end. For example: you rely on a qualifying intermediary to verify a customer, and the intermediary contract later ends. It is not an answer to say “the documents are still with them”. The institution remains responsible for keeping the records, and should retrieve them when the service ends.⚖W8

懷疑的評估與呈報流程Assessing and reporting suspicion

必記RememberSAFE四步The four SAFE steps

評估懷疑可以用 SAFE 四個步驟來記。第一步 S，是篩查交易或客戶，找出可疑指標。Suspicion can be assessed in four SAFE steps. The first step, S, is to screen the customer or the transaction for suspicious indicators.
第二步 A，是在適當而且不會構成通風報訊的範圍內，向客戶查詢，了解交易或關係的理由。The second step, A, is to ask the customer appropriate questions, so as to understand the rationale, without tipping the customer off.
第三步 F，是找出並考慮所有已知、以及可以合理取得的紀錄，包括客戶資料及其他關連交易。The third step, F, is to find and consider all records that are known or reasonably available, including customer information and other linked transactions.
第四步 E，是評估全部資料；如果懷疑仍然存在，便按內部及法定程序呈報，並記錄結論及理由。SAFE 這套流程，不可用來拖延已經必須作出的披露。例如：四步還沒有「走完」，但懷疑已經形成，便不能以「還在做 SAFE」為理由，把應向 JFIU 作出的披露一直延後。The fourth step, E, is to evaluate all of the information. If suspicion remains, report it through the internal and statutory procedures, and record the conclusions and the reasons. The SAFE process cannot be used to justify delaying a disclosure that is already due. For example: if suspicion has already been formed, the fact that the four steps have not all been “finished” is not a reason to keep putting off a disclosure that should be made to the JFIU.
