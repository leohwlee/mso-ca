01

營業處所、本地管理辦事處與紀錄儲存地方Business premises, local management office and record storage

三種地方的用途不同Three places with different purposes

  • 特定處所指用以經營貨幣兌換及／或匯款的處所。第4.4段列的例子是：(1)被佔用作經營金錢服務；(2)被宣傳（包括招牌）為會見客戶的處所；(3)經常由持牌人以業主或租戶身分控制。不要把這些例子改寫成「必須同時自置／租用、掛招牌並已完成客戶交易」才需登記。Particular premises are premises used to operate money changing and/or remittance. Paragraph 4.4 gives examples: (1) occupied for operating money service; (2) advertised, including by a signboard, for meeting customers; (3) regularly controlled by the licensee as landlord or tenant. Do not turn these examples into a requirement that ownership/tenancy, advertising and a completed customer transaction must all exist before registration is needed.
  • 只是食肆／銀行的訪客或顧客，或純為處理公司會計／法律文件而到會計師行／律師行，並不使該處成為自己的特定處所。沒有固定處所的上門服務、在客戶辦公室會客，以及中文所列「只使用流動電子設備」的交易，可屬無特定處所模式；英文原文為使用流動電子設備。店舖同時使用手機不會因此變為無處所經營，無特定處所亦仍須持牌。Being only a visitor/customer at a restaurant or bank, or visiting an accountant/solicitor solely for company documents, does not make it the MSO’s particular premises. Door-to-door service without fixed premises, customer-office meetings and transactions using mobile electronic devices are examples of no-particular-premises operation; the Chinese text says “only” using such devices. A shop does not become a no-premises operation merely by also using phones, and no-premises operation still requires a licence.
  • 不適合的例子包括：位於純住宅樓宇內的處所；另一MSO正在用作經營金錢服務或另一申請人已填報申牌／續牌的處所；共用其他業務的入口、未得其佔用人／業主准許或協助便不能進入；或招牌業務名稱與提交的商業登記不同。混合商住處所須每名佔用人書面同意獲授權人進入視察。Unsuitable examples include premises in a wholly residential building; premises already used by another MSO or declared by another grant/renewal applicant; premises whose entrance is another business’s area and cannot be accessed without its occupant/owner’s permission or assistance; or a signboard business name differing from the submitted BR. Mixed commercial/residential premises need every occupant’s written consent for authorized persons to enter for inspection.
  • 沒有特定營業處所者須維持本地管理辦事處（LMO）：香港實體辦事處兼通訊地址，可親身及電話聯絡，由獨資經營者、合夥人、董事、最終擁有人及／或合規主任駐守。不接納經營者的住址／住宅處所或其服務供應商（如公司秘書、會計師行、律師行）的地址／處所；須有業主准許作金錢服務用途的租約條款或信件。不是所有有特定處所MSO都另外須設LMO。No-particular-premises operators maintain an LMO: a physical Hong Kong office/correspondence address reachable in person/by phone, staffed by the proprietor, partner, director, ultimate owner and/or CO. The MSO’s residential address/premises or its service provider’s address/premises, such as a company secretarial firm, accountant or solicitor, is not accepted. Obtain landlord permission for money-service use in the tenancy agreement or a letter. Not every MSO with particular premises needs a separate LMO.
  • 所有申請人須有香港實體本地紀錄儲存地方（LPS），保存受持牌人控制的完整金錢服務交易帳目及紀錄；不接納經營者的住址／住宅處所或其服務供應商的地址／處所。未交LMO／LPS所需資料的申請無效、不獲處理，亦可成為拒絕發牌理由；未能持續維持可引致暫時吊銷／撤銷。All applicants need a physical Hong Kong LPS holding the complete money-service transaction books/records under the licensee’s control. The MSO’s residential address/premises or its service provider’s address/premises is not accepted. An application missing required LMO/LPS information is invalid and not processed, and the omission can also ground refusal. Failure to maintain the required place can lead to suspension/revocation.

與其他業務同址及商業登記Sharing with another business and business registration

  • 可與其他業務同址時，金錢服務須與其他業務清楚分隔和識別；與另一MSO共用處所經營金錢服務不獲接納。另須持有由稅務局局長發出的有效商業登記證（BR）；沒有有效證明可令申請不獲處理，已發牌者可被暫停／撤銷牌照。Where premises are shared with another business, money service must be clearly partitioned and distinguished. Sharing with another MSO for money service is not accepted. Maintain a valid Business Registration (BR) Certificate issued by the Commissioner of Inland Revenue; absence can prevent application processing or expose an existing licence to suspension/revocation.
  • LMO及LPS的業主須准許把處所用於金錢服務業務，並在租約或業主信件中列明。只提供一個物理地址而沒有許可，並不足以符合指引。For an LMO and LPS, the landlord must permit use for money-service business in the tenancy agreement or a landlord’s letter. Supplying a physical address alone does not satisfy the Guide.

02

新牌申請：文件、面見及正式聲明New applications: documents, interview and declarations

完整申請的主要組成Main components of a complete application

  • 用表格1及適用補充表／附件，連同身分／公司與商業登記、業務計劃、AML政策、處所及人員證明等按清單提交。新牌及續牌的適用營業處所須至少兩張102×152毫米（4R）照片：一張內部、一張外部；每個另外用作LMO／LPS的處所亦須同樣照片。可親身、郵遞或使用指定電子渠道；未在指明期限內交妥所需資料／文件，申請無效，不獲處理。Submit Form 1 and applicable supplements/annexes with checklist identity/corporate/BR, business-plan, AML-policy, premises and personnel evidence. New/renewal applications for particular premises require at least two 102×152 mm (4R) photographs: one interior and one exterior. Each separate LMO/LPS premises needs the same. Use prescribed in-person/post/electronic routes; failure to provide required information/documents within the specified period makes the application invalid and unprocessed.
  • 面見時須帶會面通知、繳費紀錄及相關文件正本，並在海關人員面前簽署牌照申請書；須澄清及闡述已交的業務計劃／AML政策。獨資經營者本人出席；合夥由每名合夥人書面授權的合夥人或員工出席；法團由董事會書面授權的董事或員工出席。獲授權的普通員工可面見，但不因此取得應考能力評核的資格。At interview bring the notice, payment record and relevant originals; sign the licence application in the presence of Customs officers and clarify/elaborate the submitted business plan/AML policy. The proprietor attends personally. A partnership sends partner(s) or employee(s) authorized in writing by every partner; a corporation sends director(s) or employee(s) authorized in writing by its board. Interview authority for an ordinary employee does not confer assessment eligibility.
  • 個人填表格3A連附錄I及II；法團填表格3B。第5.8段的見證程序特別針對3A附錄I：聲明人在獲授權海關人員、執業專業人士（如律師、會計師、核數師）、公證人或太平紳士面前簽署。見證人核對身分證明文件正本與所列個人資料，證明資料真確，並證明簽署是在其面前作出；不能籠統當作所有表格都須採相同見證程序。Individuals complete Form 3A with Appendices I and II; corporations use Form 3B. Paragraph 5.8 specifically applies the witnessing procedure to Appendix I of Form 3A: the declarant signs before an authorized Customs officer, practising professional such as a solicitor/accountant/auditor, notary public or Justice of the Peace. The witness checks the particulars against original identity documents, certifies their accuracy and certifies that the signature was made in the witness’s presence. Do not apply this same witnessing procedure indiscriminately to every form.
  • 處理時間沒有固定保證：須視乎收齊文件、處所實地視察、向其他機關索取適當人選判定紀錄，以及完成能力評核所需時間。海關會核對本部門、其他政府部門及執法機構持有的資料。There is no guaranteed fixed processing period: time depends on collecting documents, inspecting premises, obtaining other authorities’ fitness-test records and completing the Assessment. Customs checks information held by itself, other government departments and law-enforcement agencies.

證明文件按身分及用途準備Evidence by legal form and purpose

  • 獨資／合夥提供有效商業登記及商業登記冊資料摘錄。本地公司另備成立證明、最新周年申報表NAR1及其後所有登記文件；新公司用NNC1（股份有限公司）或NNC1G（股份有限公司以外的公司）。非香港公司備非香港公司註冊證明、最新周年申報表NN3及其後存檔文件；新成立公司用NN1（在香港註冊的非香港公司詳情）。另按清單備章程及集團架構／持股比例。這些是公司文件，不是MSO半年交易申報表。Proprietors/partnerships provide valid BR and business-register extracts. Local companies add incorporation and the latest annual return NAR1 plus subsequent filings; new companies use NNC1 (limited by shares) or NNC1G (not limited by shares). Non-Hong Kong companies provide their non-Hong Kong-company registration certificate and latest annual return NN3 plus subsequent filings; newly established companies use NN1 (particulars of a non-Hong Kong company registered in Hong Kong). Add constitution and group/ownership structure as listed. These corporate documents are not MSO half-yearly transaction returns.
  • 每個適用處所、LMO及LPS提供已加蓋印花租約／業權、平面圖、內外兩張4R照片及業主准許指定用途信；LMO另備有效商業登記，商住處所另備全體佔用人同意。For each applicable premises, LMO and LPS provide stamped tenancy/ownership, floor plan, two interior/exterior 4R photos and owner permission for the relevant use. LMOs add valid BR; mixed premises add every occupant’s consent.
  • 獨資經營者及每名個人合夥人／董事／最終擁有人備3A連附錄I及II；香港居民備香港身分證複本，非香港居民備旅遊證件個人資料頁複本。法人合夥人／董事備3B；本地／非香港公司另備有效商業登記、相應註冊證明及組織章程大綱／細則；未在港註冊的法團則備按第32B章《公司（表格）規例》第3條核證的成立證明或等同文件。The sole proprietor and every individual partner/director/ultimate owner provide Form 3A with Appendices I and II; Hong Kong residents provide an HKID copy and non-residents a travel-document biodata-page copy. Corporate partners/directors provide Form 3B; local/non-Hong Kong companies add valid BR, the corresponding incorporation/registration certificate and memorandum/articles. Corporations unregistered in Hong Kong provide incorporation or equivalent evidence certified under regulation 3 of the Companies (Forms) Regulations, Cap.32B.
  • 業務計劃及AML政策須由適用獨資／每名個人合夥人、董事及最終擁有人確認。另按清單備本地／境外第三方合約、CO／MLRO身分證、有效僱傭合約及申請日期前3個月內發出的地址證明、銀行戶權證明及銀行確認作金錢服務用途。第16.1段要求文件蓋上申請人公司印章；清單非盡錄，海關可再索資料。Plans/policies require applicable proprietor/every individual partner, director and ultimate-owner endorsement. The checklist also covers local/foreign third-party contracts, CO/MLRO HKID, valid employment contracts and address proof issued within three months before application, bank ownership and bank acknowledgement of money-service use. Paragraph 16.1 requires documents stamped with the applicant’s company chop. Customs may request more; the checklist is not exhaustive.

理解：業務計劃與打擊洗錢政策各要說明甚麼Understand: what the business plan and AML policy explain

  • 業務計劃須綜合概述業務，包括詳細運作模式、組織架構、支付系統、目標客戶、職員編制及財政等。若不用銀行帳戶經營，須具體交代在這種安排下如何運作；「不經銀行」不是可以省略支付路徑的理由。The business plan provides a comprehensive overview, including detailed operating mode, organisational structure, payment system, target customers, staffing and finances. If operating without a bank account, explain how the business operates in that arrangement; not using a bank does not justify omitting the payment route.
  • AML政策說明機構本身相關業務的政策、程序及管控如何減低其面對的洗錢／恐怖分子資金籌集風險及符合規定；例如須闡述匯款的支付／結算系統、制裁篩查及高風險客戶篩查機制。只列「本公司遵守法例」不足以說明上述內容。The AML policy explains the business’s own policies, procedures and controls for mitigating its ML/TF risks and meeting requirements; examples include explaining remittance payment/settlement systems and sanctions/high-risk-customer screening mechanisms. A statement that the company complies with the law does not explain these elements.
  • 首次申牌時，每個額外營業處所列入同一申請，逐個繳附加費，毋須每處另交新牌申請。收費數字見Part 3「附表3：金錢服務牌照費用」；已持牌後才新增處所則按下文表格5事先申請。At initial application include each additional business premises in the same application and pay the fee for each; no separate new-licence application is needed for every premises. Amounts are in Part 3, Schedule 3: MSO licensing fees. Adding premises after licensing instead requires prior application using Form 5 below.

發牌審查：拒絕理由須連同適用條件讀Grant assessment: read refusal grounds with their conditions

  • 第5.10段列舉的申請缺失包括：適當人選判定不合格、不合適處所、商住處所欠每名佔用人同意、欠LPS資料、無特定處所經營而欠LMO資料。處所適合及所需佔用人同意是法例的發牌前提，不能因指引寫「可拒絕」便當作可任意豁免。Paragraph 5.10 lists fitness-test failure, unsuitable premises, missing consent from every occupant of mixed premises, missing LPS information and, for no-particular-premises operation, missing LMO information. Suitable premises and required occupant consent are statutory conditions for grant; the Guide’s “may refuse” wording does not make them freely waivable.
  • 其餘例子是：業務計劃／AML政策不符合相關指引、未委任合資格CO或MLRO、不完整／無效申請、沒有高級管理層應考或取得合格，以及本身屬第25條豁免者。已獲法定豁免者的申請依第3.1段不獲接納。這是列舉拒絕理由，並非說已交齊文件便保證獲牌。Further examples are a business plan/AML policy not meeting the relevant guidelines, no competent CO or MLRO, an incomplete/invalid application, no senior manager attending or passing, and section 25 exemption. Applications by exempt persons are not accepted under paragraph 3.1. This is a list of refusal grounds, not a guarantee that submitting documents secures a licence.

適當人選：持續而非一次性的要求Fitness and propriety: an ongoing requirement

法定因素與補充評核Statutory and supplementary factors

  • 須考慮指定香港／境外罪行、持續違反規管要求、破產／清盤／接管等法定事項，以及其他與適當人選有關的因素。被定罪或有某一負面因素不必然自動失格；關長按全部相關情況作整體判斷。獲牌後仍須持續符合。Consider statutory Hong Kong/overseas offences, persistent regulatory non-compliance, bankruptcy/winding-up/receivership and other relevant matters. A conviction or single adverse factor does not automatically determine failure; the CCE assesses all circumstances. Fitness/propriety continues after licensing.
  • 第10份指引補充：不遵守AMLO、牌照條件或其他規管義務、誠信／刑事紀錄及財政狀況。第11份再涵蓋對顧客是否公平、是否準備並能持續提供服務、CO等人員能力／知識、評核表現、資料是否真確及是否配合海關。Document 10 adds AMLO/licence/regulatory compliance, honesty/criminal history and financial standing. Document 11 further considers fair customer treatment, readiness/ability to provide services, CO/personnel competence/knowledge, assessment performance, truthful information and cooperation with Customs.
  • 其他因素包括組織、人手、財政及系統資源、有效內控／審核、利益衝突、其他罪行或待決控罪、專業／監管譴責、其他牌照限制、董事失格、未清償判決債項，以及其他業務帶來的洗錢／財務風險。停業很久或空有牌照而無服務準備也可影響判斷。Other factors include organisation, staff, finance/systems, controls/audit, conflicts, other offences/pending charges, professional/regulatory censure, other licence restrictions, director disqualification, judgment debts and ML/financial risks from other businesses. Long inactivity or lack of service readiness can also matter.

適當人選：法定必考慮的事項Fitness and propriety: the statutory mandatory factors

  • 香港定罪包括AMLO指定的CDD、視察／調查、妨礙或虛假資料、審裁處等罪行；UNATMO第14條罪行；DTROP第25(1)、25A(5)或(7)條及附表1罪行；OSCO第25(1)、25A(5)或(7)條及附表1或2罪行。重點不只是曾否洗錢，也包括指定不披露、通風報訊及不配合監管等行為。Hong Kong convictions include the AMLO offences specified in section 30(4)(a), covering CDD, inspection/investigation, obstruction/false information and tribunal matters; UNATMO section 14; DTROP sections 25(1), 25A(5)/(7) and Schedule 1 offences; OSCO sections 25(1), 25A(5)/(7) and Schedule 1/2 offences. The test extends beyond laundering itself to specified disclosure, tipping-off and supervisory-compliance offences.
  • 境外定罪包括：若在港作出會構成上述罪行的行為、洗錢／恐怖分子資金籌集，以及定罪必須裁定存在欺詐、貪污或不誠實的罪行。另須考慮持續不遵從AMLO／第51條規例；個人的未解除破產或破產程序；公司的清盤、清盤令或接管人。關長亦可考慮其他相關事項，並非只有一張定罪清單。Foreign convictions include conduct that would constitute those offences in Hong Kong, ML/TF offences, and offences requiring a finding of fraud, corruption or dishonesty. Also consider persistent AMLO/section 51 regulation non-compliance; an individual’s undischarged bankruptcy/proceedings; and corporate liquidation, winding-up orders or receivership. The CCE also considers other relevant matters; this is not only a conviction checklist.

  • 第12份屬發牌紀律罰款（第43條最高100萬元）；第13份屬指明CDD／紀錄等違規（第21條最高1,000萬元或利益／避免成本3倍的較大者）。不是每次違規都固定判上限或固定按利益3倍。Document 12 concerns section 43 licensing penalties, capped at HK$1 million. Document 13 concerns section 21 specified CDD/record-related contraventions, capped at the greater of HK$10 million or three times profit/cost avoidance. Neither ceiling nor a three-times figure is an automatic penalty.
  • 考慮違規性質、嚴重性、持續時間、是否故意／罔顧、是否系統性、造成損害及利益；亦看糾正措施、合作／主動披露、過往紀錄、其他相關處分及一致性。罰款應有阻嚇力，並按整體情況考慮財政影響，不應有很可能令經營者陷入財務困境的效果。Consider nature, seriousness, duration, intent/recklessness, systemic failures, harm and benefit; also remediation, cooperation/voluntary disclosure, history, other sanctions and consistency. Penalties should deter, with financial impact assessed in context without the likely effect of putting the operator into financial jeopardy.

罰款評估的四組因素Four groups used in pecuniary-penalty assessment

  • 第一組有九項性質、嚴重性及影響因素：(i)蓄意、罔顧或疏忽，純粹疏忽／技術性違規一般較輕；(ii)持續時間及頻密程度；(iii)可能損害香港國際金融中心聲譽，第12份另提金錢服務業務的廉潔穩健；(iv)造成／可能造成他人損失或使他人承擔支出，第12份亦明列可能使他人承擔支出。Group 1 has nine nature, seriousness and impact factors: (i) intent, recklessness or negligence, with merely negligent/technical breaches generally less serious; (ii) duration and frequency; (iii) potential harm to Hong Kong’s reputation as an international financial centre, with document 12 also naming the integrity of money-service operations; (iv) actual/potential loss to others or costs imposed on them, with document 12 expressly also covering potential costs.
  • 其餘是：(v)獨自違規，還是作為某集團一部分，以及在集團中的角色；(vi)整體／部分業務的CDD及紀錄程序管理制度或內控是否有嚴重／系統性弱點；(vii)是否顯示一套導致違規的行事模式／違規模式。單次孤立錯誤、共同參與及持續模式，不能一概只寫成「不合規」。The next factors are (v) acting alone or as part of a group and the role played in that group; (vi) serious/systemic management or internal-control weaknesses in CDD and record procedures for all/part of the business; and (vii) a pattern resulting in contraventions/a pattern of contraventions. An isolated error, group participation and a continuing pattern are not adequately described only as non-compliance.
  • 最後是：(viii)多項較小問題，單項未必足以施罰款，但合併考慮可足以施罰；(ix)違規所促成、引致或可歸因於該違規的任何金融罪行之性質及程度。只看有否實際金錢損失，會漏掉系統風險、行為模式及金融罪行影響。Finally, (viii) smaller issues that may not individually justify a pecuniary penalty but do so collectively; and (ix) the nature and extent of financial crime facilitated, occasioned or otherwise attributable to the contravention. Actual monetary loss alone misses systemic risk, patterns of conduct and financial-crime impact.
  • 第二組：違規後行為，包括隱瞞、補救、合作及再犯風險。第三組：既往紀律／合規紀錄，包括相類違規、過往承諾及其他機關已採／可能採的處分。Group 2—post-contravention conduct: concealment, remediation, cooperation and recurrence risk. Group 3—disciplinary/compliance history: similar breaches, previous undertakings and other authorities’ actual/likely action.
  • 第四組：其他相關因素，包括行為當時有效指引、相類個案一致性、得益／避免成本、主動及完整披露等減輕因素。一般不就符合當時有效指引的行為作紀律處分；關長一般公開罰款決定。這是非盡列、按個案評估的指引，不是把每項固定加減分。Group 4—other relevant factors: guidance current at the time, consistency with similar cases, benefit/cost avoidance and mitigation such as prompt complete disclosure. Generally no discipline is taken for conduct in line with then-current guidance; pecuniary decisions are usually publicized. These are non-exhaustive, case-specific factors, not a fixed points formula.

第34章：兌換交易單及顧客撤銷權Cap.34: transaction notes and rescission

適用範圍及交易步驟Scope and transaction steps

  • 第34章不適用於認可機構、符合條件的酒店兌換，以及顧客交付款額超過100,000元的交易。正好100,000元仍在此金額範圍內；這與AMLO一般非經常交易CDD的120,000元門檻完全不同。貨幣亦包括支票及旅行支票。Cap.34 excludes authorized institutions, qualifying hotel exchange and transactions where the customer tenders more than HK$100,000. Exactly HK$100,000 remains within that monetary scope. This differs from AMLO’s HK$120,000 general occasional CDD threshold. Currency includes cheques and travellers’ cheques.
  • 交易前按附表1填具一式兩份交易單，包括日期、貨幣／金額、已含所有收費的淨匯率、付予顧客的貨幣等值及總額（編號可選填），以及中英文名稱、地址、電話等；收銀員簽署，按實際可行情況解釋並要求顧客簽署。不同售出貨幣分單，按交易單全額付款，不另扣費。Before exchange, prepare duplicate Schedule 1 notes with date, currencies/amounts, net rate inclusive of charges, equivalent amount of the currency issued and total (serial/transaction numbers are optional) and bilingual name/address/phone details. The cashier signs, explains as practicable and requests customer signature. Use separate notes for different currencies sold and pay the noted amount without further deduction.
  • 正本交顧客，副本至少保存12個月並供警務人員依法查閱。交易完成前顧客拒絕，須立即退還全數款項；違反第4條的交易，顧客可在交易後3日內按第5條撤銷。Give the original to the customer; retain the duplicate at least 12 months for lawful police inspection. A customer refusing before completion receives the full amount back immediately. Where section 4 is breached, the customer may rescind within three days under section 5.

匯率展示、廣告及責任Rate displays, advertising and liability

買入／賣出從兌換商角度理解Buying/selling from the changer’s perspective

  • 淨匯率是已包括全部收費及佣金的每單位外幣港幣價格。對附表3內自己準備買入／賣出的貨幣，須在清楚、光線足夠及無遮擋的告示牌展示相應淨率；不是強制買賣全部17種貨幣。多塊牌的匯率須相同，可按10或100單位列示。展示的是最不利率，可以給顧客更佳率。The net rate is the HKD price per foreign-currency unit inclusive of all charges and commission. Display the applicable net rates for Schedule 3 currencies the changer is prepared to buy or sell, clearly, visibly and with adequate lighting; there is no requirement to trade all 17 currencies. Multiple boards must show identical rates; units of 10 or 100 may be used. Display the least favourable rate; better rates may be offered.
  • 附表3的17種貨幣：澳洲、加拿大、中國（不含香港、澳門、台灣）、歐盟、印度、印尼、日本、馬來西亞、菲律賓、韓國、新加坡、瑞士、台灣、泰國、英國、美國、越南。第16份通函解釋2025年8月24日起的附表更新。The 17 Schedule 3 currencies are Australia, Canada, China excluding Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan, EU, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Philippines, Korea, Singapore, Switzerland, Taiwan, Thailand, UK, USA and Vietnam. Document 16 explains the amendments effective 24 August 2025.
  • 未列於附表3的貨幣，在收取款項前須書面向顧客提供買入及賣出兩個淨率，並另按交易單規則辦理。不能只因不在展示清單便省略報價或交易單。For currencies outside Schedule 3, provide both buy and sell net rates in writing before accepting money, as well as the transaction note. Absence from the display list does not dispense with quotation or note requirements.
  • 廣告中買入與賣出率須同等顯著；不得宣傳收取／不收取佣金或其他收費，也不得就匯率、條款或條例適用性誤導。附表2允許的告示包括顧客自行承擔外幣管制限制及離開前核對款項；不是可任意添加免責條款。Buy and sell rates in advertisements need equal prominence. Do not advertise charging/not charging commission or other fees, or mislead about rates, terms or applicability. Schedule 2 permits notices about currency-control responsibility and checking money before departure, not arbitrary disclaimers.
  • 僱員違例可使僱主及符合條件的公司高級人員／合夥人同負責，法定合理防止措施等抗辯另論。第4條初犯屬第1級罰款，再犯第2級及6個月；第6、7、8、10條違規可第2級及6個月。Employee breaches can engage employer and qualifying corporate-officer/partner liability, subject to statutory reasonable-prevention defences. Section 4 first offences carry level 1, subsequent offences level 2 and six months; sections 6, 7, 8 and 10 carry level 2 and six months.

同板比較及法定責任推定Same-board comparison and statutory liability presumptions

  • 同一貨幣的買入／賣出匯率須同等顯眼，並讓顧客毋須參看多於一塊告示牌便能比較兩者。多塊牌的匯率一致要求，與可在同一塊牌比較買賣率，是兩項相關而不同的條件。Buying/selling rates for a currency need equal prominence and must be comparable without referring to more than one board. Identical rates across multiple boards and comparing both rates on one board are related but distinct conditions.
  • 第11條在指定情況下把僱員的違規連繫至僱主，並可涉及公司董事、經理、秘書、相類高級人員及合夥人。沒有相反證據時，適用者被推定未採合理防止措施；須讀連法定適用身分及合理防止抗辯。僅提供專業意見，不會單因此成為董事慣常依其指示行事的人。Section 11 links employee breaches to employers and can engage directors, managers, secretaries, similar officers and partners within its specified scope. In the absence of contrary evidence the applicable persons face presumptions about failure to take reasonable preventive steps, subject to the statutory actor scope and defence. Giving professional advice alone does not make its giver the person whose directions the directors habitually follow.
