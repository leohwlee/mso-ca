## Ruling marks, 23 October / 10月23日裁決標記

At the owner's request, every place where the notes apply a source-conflict ruling now
carries a purple number: 131 marks (118 points and 13 figures) across 93 rulings. The
numbers are the decisions in `revision/CONFLICT-CALLS.md` (B, W, E, N and D26). A dashed
number means each language keeps its own wording. Four U numbers mark language differences
the text handles that have no CONFLICT-CALLS entry. Each Part ends with "Rulings in this
part": the question, the decision, every place it is used, links to other Parts and the
recorded entry with links to the cited originals. v1.12.0 flagged three points that did
not match their ruling; on the owner's decision, v1.12.1 corrects them: the HK$945
fitness-test fee is paid for each tested person at grant and every renewal (B6, Part 3),
the CCE must have regard to any other relevant matter (B13, Part 4), and simplified CDD
stops on doubts about veracity or adequacy (E23, Part 6). Each now also cites the
Schedule 3 or Schedule 2 provision it follows where that was missing.

應擁有人要求，筆記中每個套用來源矛盾裁決的地方，現都標上紫色編號：共131處（118個要點及13個圖表），
涉及93項裁決。編號對應`revision/CONFLICT-CALLS.md`的裁決（B、W、E、N及D26）；虛線編號表示兩語各按本身語文。
四個U編號標示正文已處理、但未列入CONFLICT-CALLS的兩語差異。每部分末設「本部分的裁決」，列出問題、裁決、
所有出現位置、其他部分連結，以及附原件連結的裁決記錄原文。v1.12.0曾標出三個與裁決不一致的要點；按擁有人決定，
v1.12.1已修正：適當人選測試費每名受測試人士HK$945，批牌及每次續牌均須繳付（第3部分B6）；關長須考慮其認為有關的
任何其他事宜（第4部分B13）；對資料的真實性或充分性有疑問即停止簡化盡職審查（第6部分E23）。欠缺的附表3／附表2出處亦已補上。

## Maintain / 維護

`part1.py`–`part7.py` hold the consolidated manuscripts, including the former
second-pass additions; the `essentials.py` module that carried them was removed on
24 October 2026 and its pre-consolidation source stays in
`independent-audit-2026-10-19/baseline.zip`. `memory_panels.py` holds explicit source references for
the selected comparisons. `visual_panels.py` adapts/adds source-bound diagrams, including
the list cards; `visual_render.py` provides native, offline HTML rendering. `tiers.py`
gives every block its study tier; `traps.py` holds the exam traps, each tied to a
CONFLICT-CALLS decision number that `build.py` checks. `rulings.py` ties each ruling mark
to one point or figure by its English text; `amounts.py` holds the numbers page. `build.py`
checks every anchor, number and section, and validates and renders without reading the bank.

`part1.py`至`part7.py`保存整合後文稿；舊第二輪補充已併入相應主題，
原載補充的`essentials.py`已於2026年10月24日移除，整合前原稿保存在`independent-audit-2026-10-19/baseline.zip`。`memory_panels.py`的圖表有明確原文引用。
`visual_panels.py`處理圖解內容及改編，`visual_render.py`負責離線HTML表達；`tiers.py`為每個內容組定學習層次，
`traps.py`保存易錯提示，每項對應CONFLICT-CALLS裁決編號並由`build.py`核對；`rulings.py`以英文原句把每個裁決標記連到一個要點或圖表，
`build.py`核對每個定位及編號；生成器不讀題庫。

`gap_check.py` separately checks 525 identifiers and source routes after authoring;
it cannot establish semantic completeness. Its current output is
`visual-refinement-2026-10-20/bank-source-routes.json`. Earlier outputs remain historical evidence. Manual question-stem concept
checking is described in the audit report; no question content supplies the notes.

題庫只於最後作題幹概念及來源路徑查漏，發現問題後回原文核實；
不把自動來源對應稱為525題每個陳述均已覆蓋。題目、答案及評分未改。

```text
python revision/study-notes/build.py
python revision/study-notes/gap_check.py
python revision/study-notes/build.py --check
python revision/study-notes/gap_check.py --check
```

Build the practice target with `go run . -export-html dist/mso-ca.html`.
See [current visual checks](visual-refinement-2026-10-20/REPORT.md), the
[depth review checks](depth-review-2026-10-20/REPORT.md) and the preserved
[earlier verification](independent-audit-2026-10-19/VERIFICATION.md) for actual results.
