洗錢：犯罪得益及三個階段Money laundering: criminal proceeds and three stages

必記Remember定義與過程Definition and process

  • 洗錢的法定定義著重行為的意圖：令犯罪得益或全部／部分、直接／間接代表該得益的財產，表面上不像犯罪得益。另須區分DTROP／OSCO第25條的處理犯罪得益罪：處理財產時知道或有合理理由相信它代表犯罪得益。財產不限現金，亦不必表面可疑。The statutory ML definition concerns an act intended to make crime proceeds, or property wholly/partly and directly/indirectly representing them, appear not to be such proceeds. Distinguish the DTROP/OSCO section 25 dealing offence: dealing while knowing or having reasonable grounds to believe the property represents crime proceeds. Property is not limited to cash and need not look suspicious.
  • 存放：把犯罪現金放入金融體系，例如分拆存款或兌換。分層：透過多次轉移、不同帳戶或司法管轄區掩藏來源。整合：使資金以投資、資產或業務收益等形式重新出現為合法財富。Placement introduces criminal cash into the financial system, for example through split deposits or exchange. Layering obscures its origin through transfers, accounts or jurisdictions. Integration returns it as apparently legitimate investments, assets or business income.
  • 三個階段是理解典型模式的工具，實際交易可同時涉及多個階段。匯款或兌換業務可能協助存放及分層，交易有商業外觀也不表示資金合法。The stages explain typical patterns; actual transactions can overlap them. Remittance and exchange can facilitate placement and layering, and a commercial appearance does not establish legitimacy.

理解Understand財產及境外犯罪得益的涵義Property and proceeds from overseas conduct

  • AMLO的財產不限於現金，也包括貨品、據法權產及土地，不論在香港或境外；以及由該等財產產生的義務、地役權、各類產業／權益／收益，無論現在或將來、既得或或有。洗錢不能只以有沒有實體鈔票判斷。AMLO property includes money, goods, choses in action and land in Hong Kong or elsewhere, and associated obligations, easements, estates, interests and profits, present/future and vested/contingent. Choses in action are rights enforceable by legal action; laundering is not limited to physical banknotes.
  • 洗錢定義涵蓋香港可公訴罪行所得，以及若在香港發生便會構成可公訴罪行的境外行為所得；財產可全部／部分、直接／間接代表得益。「境外所得」並不自動免除香港洗錢風險。The ML definition covers proceeds of Hong Kong indictable offences and overseas conduct that would constitute an indictable offence in Hong Kong. Property may represent proceeds wholly/partly and directly/indirectly. Overseas origin does not by itself remove Hong Kong laundering risk.

理解Understand處理財產與上游罪行的證明dealing with property and proof of predicate crime

  • 2022年報告第3.3段說明，第405／455章第25條的洗錢罪涉及「處理」財產，以及明知或有合理理由相信財產代表犯罪得益。處理包括收受、取得、隱藏、掩飾、處置、轉換、運入香港或調離香港，以及用作借貸或保證；並不限於替別人轉帳。Paragraph 3.3 of the 2022 report describes the section 25 DTROP/OSCO offence through dealing with property and knowing or having reasonable grounds to believe it represents crime proceeds. Dealing includes receiving, acquiring, concealing, disguising, disposing of, converting, bringing into/removing from Hong Kong, and using property to borrow or as security; it is not confined to transferring funds for others.
  • 控方無須證明某項上游可公訴罪行、具體行為或販毒行為曾經發生，亦無須證明該財產的確是該等犯罪得益。這不表示免除證明處理行為及上述知情／合理理由相信的要件。原文解釋，境外上游罪行及多層轉換可令來源難以證明。The prosecution need not prove commission or specific conduct of the predicate indictable offence or drug trafficking, or that the property actually is such proceeds. This does not dispense with proving the dealing and knowledge/reasonable-grounds-to-believe elements. The report explains the difficulty of proving overseas predicates and origins obscured by multiple transformations.

恐怖分子資金籌集：合法資金也可被濫用Terrorist financing: legitimate funds can also be misused

必記Remember與洗錢的分別Distinction from money laundering

  • 恐怖分子資金籌集包括：有意圖或知道財產會全部／部分用於恐怖主義行為而提供／籌集財產，不論是否實際使用；向某人提供財產／金融或相關服務，或為其利益籌集財產／要求服務，而知道或罔顧該人是否恐怖分子或聯繫者。可直接或間接進行，不能只看資金來源。TF includes providing/collecting property intending or knowing it will be used wholly/partly for terrorist acts, whether actually used or not; making property or financial/related services available to a person, or collecting property/soliciting services for their benefit, knowing or reckless as to whether they are a terrorist/associate. Direct and indirect activity is covered; origin alone is insufficient.
  • 洗錢著重犯罪得益的來源；恐怖活動可以由合法收入、捐款或犯罪收入資助。金額小、來源合法或經註冊組織轉移，均不能自動排除恐怖分子資金籌集風險。Money laundering concerns the criminal origin of proceeds. Terrorist activity may be funded by legitimate income, donations or crime. Small amounts, lawful origins or transfers through registered organisations do not automatically exclude TF risk.

理解Understand合法資金也可被用於恐怖融資lawful funds can finance terrorism

  • 2022年報告第8章說明，資金可來自捐款、會員費、商業收益或犯罪；轉移可經銀行、MSO、現金攜帶者或其他渠道。資金來源合法不代表用途或受益對象合法，評估仍須連同人物、目的、地區及交易背景。Chapter 8 of the 2022 report discusses donations, membership fees, commercial income and crime as possible sources, with banks, MSOs, cash couriers and other channels for movement. Lawful origin does not establish lawful purpose or beneficiary; assess persons, purpose, geography and transaction context together.
  • 報告把香港非牟利組織（NPO）的TF脆弱程度評為低；較易被濫用的是在香港運作及籌款、支援衝突地區人道服務的組織。原文也列出緩減因素：不少屬國際網絡、具內部盡職審查，捐款大多可追蹤，並有匿名捐款風險評估及銀行監察。這是2022年報告的評估，不能因此把所有慈善團體列為高風險，亦不是現時地區評級。The report rates Hong Kong non-profit organisations’ (NPOs’) inherent TF vulnerability low. Greater exposure concerns organisations operating and raising funds in Hong Kong to support humanitarian services in conflict zones. It also identifies mitigation: many belong to international networks with internal due diligence, donations are mostly traceable, and anonymous-donation risk assessment and bank monitoring apply. These are findings in the 2022 report, not grounds to classify every charity as high risk or current geographical ratings.

香港法律：各條例處理的問題Hong Kong’s laws: the role of each ordinance

必記Remember六組核心法例Six core legal frameworks

  • 第615章《打擊洗錢及恐怖分子資金籌集條例》（AMLO）：訂明客戶盡職審查、備存紀錄、監管及金錢服務經營者發牌制度。第405章《販毒（追討得益）條例》（DTROP）及第455章《有組織及嚴重罪行條例》（OSCO）：處理犯罪得益、披露、調查、限制及沒收。⚖N1Cap.615 AMLO establishes CDD, record-keeping, supervision and MSO licensing. Cap.405 DTROP and Cap.455 OSCO address criminal proceeds, disclosure, investigation, restraint and confiscation.⚖N1
  • 第575章《聯合國（反恐怖主義措施）條例》（UNATMO）：恐怖分子財產、相關服務及披露。第537章《聯合國制裁條例》（UNSO）：在香港實施聯合國安全理事會決議所要求的制裁。第526章《大規模毀滅武器（提供服務的管制）條例》：管制可能協助大規模毀滅武器發展、生產、獲取、儲存及運載方式的服務；有合理理由相信或懷疑服務可能涉及擴散融資時，禁止提供。⚖E34Cap.575 UNATMO addresses terrorist property, related services and disclosure. Cap.537 UNSO implements sanctions required by UN Security Council resolutions. Cap.526 controls services that will/may assist WMD development, production, acquisition, stockpiling or delivery, and prohibits services where the provider believes or suspects on reasonable grounds that they may be connected to PF.⚖E34
  • 第405／455章的洗錢罪最高可罰款500萬元及監禁14年；不披露知悉／懷疑事項最高可罰款5萬元及監禁3個月；通風報訊最高可監禁3年。恐怖分子資金籌集相關罪行可監禁14年及罰款。須分清犯罪、披露及監管處分是不同責任。The DTROP/OSCO laundering offence carries up to HK$5 million and 14 years. Failure to disclose carries up to HK$50,000 and three months; tipping-off up to three years. Relevant TF offences carry up to 14 years and a fine. Criminal liability, disclosure duties and regulatory sanctions are distinct.
  • 第1.20段：除其他事項外，OSCO (a) 賦予香港警方及香港海關人員調查有組織罪行及三合會活動的權力；(b) 賦予法院司法管轄權，沒收有組織及嚴重罪行的得益，並就被告人的財產發出限制令及押記令；(c) 增訂一項有關可公訴罪行得益的洗錢罪行；(d) 容許法院在適當情況下收取違法者及罪行的資料，以決定是否適宜作出更重的判刑。Paragraph 1.20: among other things, OSCO (a) gives Police and Customs and Excise officers powers to investigate organized crime and triad activities; (b) gives the Courts jurisdiction to confiscate proceeds of organized and serious crimes and to issue restraint and charging orders over a defendant’s property; (c) creates an ML offence for proceeds of indictable offences; and (d) enables the Courts, in appropriate circumstances, to receive information about an offender and an offence to decide whether a greater sentence is appropriate.

  • AMLO是第615章《打擊洗錢及恐怖分子資金籌集條例》；DTROP是第405章《販毒（追討得益）條例》；OSCO是第455章《有組織及嚴重罪行條例》。不要把一般犯罪得益的洗錢責任只限於毒品得益。AMLO: Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance, Cap.615. Their counter-terrorism, UN-sanctions and WMD-service controls are distinct.

參考Reference限制、沒收與恐怖分子財產凍結restraint, confiscation and terrorist-property freezing

  • 2022年報告把限制財產與沒收／充公分開：第3.15段舉已展開刑事法律程序時，向原訟法庭申請限制可變現財產；定罪後可由原訟法庭或區域法院頒發沒收令。原訟法庭亦可向潛逃者或死者發出沒收令，不能理解為一律必須先把當事人定罪。限制的可變現財產亦不必本身與所控罪行相連。The 2022 report distinguishes restraint from confiscation/forfeiture. Paragraph 3.15 describes applying to the Court of First Instance to restrain realisable property where criminal proceedings have been instituted, and confiscation by that Court or the District Court following conviction. The Court of First Instance may also order confiscation against absconders or deceased persons, so personal conviction is not an invariable prerequisite. Restrained realisable property need not itself be connected to the offences.
  • 同段的不同制度不可混算：OSCO指明罪行定罪後的沒收涉及得益≥100,000港元；DTROP販毒罪行可涉及任何金額。DTROP第24D條另有民事充公機制，針對輸入／輸出時被扣押、金額>125,000港元且屬販毒得益或曾用／擬用於販毒的現金。這是2022年報告中的資產追討制度說明，不是MSO的CDD或跨境申報門檻，也不是所有沒收權力的完整清單。The regimes in that paragraph have different conditions: post-conviction OSCO confiscation for a specified offence concerns proceeds ≥HK$100,000; DTROP drug-trafficking proceeds may be of any amount. DTROP section 24D separately provides civil forfeiture of cash >HK$125,000 seized/detained on import/export that represents drug-trafficking proceeds or was used/intended for drug trafficking. These are asset-recovery descriptions in the 2022 report, not MSO CDD or cross-boundary declaration thresholds or an exhaustive list of confiscation powers.
  • UNATMO下，保安局局長可指示凍結懷疑恐怖分子財產；原訟法庭可命令充公恐怖主義行為所得、曾用或擬用於資助／協助該行為的財產。2018年增訂的全面凍結條款另禁止處理指明恐怖分子財產及指明恐怖分子／聯繫者的財產。不要把MSO的風險決定、局長的凍結權與法院的充公命令當成同一權力。Under UNATMO the Secretary for Security may direct freezing of suspected terrorist property; the Court of First Instance may order forfeiture of property derived from a terrorist act or used/intended to finance or assist it. The blanket freezing provision introduced in 2018 separately prohibits dealing with specified terrorist property and property of specified terrorists/associates. An MSO risk decision, the Secretary’s freezing power and a court forfeiture order are different powers.

FATF與司法管轄區風險FATF and jurisdiction risk

必記Remember標準、名單與法律須分開理解Distinguish standards, lists and law

  • 財務行動特別組織（FATF）是1989年成立的跨政府組織，制定標準並推動法律、監管及操作措施的有效實施，以打擊洗錢、恐怖分子資金籌集及擴散資金籌集。香港是成員，須實施最新建議。特別組織透過司法管轄區相互評估及嚴格跟進監察合規，並識別高風險及其他受監察地區；建議不是香港法例的替代品。The Financial Action Task Force (FATF), an inter-governmental body formed in 1989, sets standards and promotes effective legal, regulatory and operational measures against ML, TF and PF. Hong Kong is a member obliged to implement its latest Recommendations. FATF monitors compliance through jurisdiction evaluations and stringent follow-up, including identifying high-risk and other monitored jurisdictions; its Recommendations do not replace Hong Kong law.
  • 「呼籲採取行動」類別要求加強盡職審查；特定高風險地區另有反制措施。「加強監察」類別須納入風險評估，但並非單憑列名便一律要求加強盡職審查。FATF風險名單亦不是聯合國的指定人士制裁名單。⚖B18⚖B39The call-for-action category requires enhanced due diligence, with countermeasures for specified high-risk jurisdictions. 理解Understand貨物的合併與申報責任Cargo aggregation and declaration responsibility

  • 非承運人：合併同一人以同一跨境運輸工具輸入／輸出的現金類物品。承運人：合併同一承運人為同一客戶在同一運輸工具運送的物品；客戶須直接聘用承運人，或直接聘用把物品運送或安排運送給承運人的代運人。同一客戶聘用多名代運人時，只有同一代運人運送或安排運送予同一承運人的物品才按這項規則合併。客戶指非承運人，不能把物流代運人自行當成客戶。For a non-carrier, aggregate CBNIs imported/exported by the same person on the same cross-boundary conveyance.
