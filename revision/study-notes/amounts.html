數字與期限總表Numbers and deadlines

七個部分的金額、百分比、日數、年期、費用及刑罰集中在一頁：每行寫明適用情形、起算點或條件，並連到筆記小節及原文。相近的數字放在一起，方便比較。Every amount, percentage, number of days, period, fee and penalty from the seven parts on one page: each row says when it applies, where its clock starts or what it requires, and links to the notes section and the source. Similar figures sit together for comparison.

01

金額門檻：先認制度，再比「≥」還是「>」Amount thresholds: identify the regime, then ≥ or >

同一個數字在不同制度下用途不同；恰好等於門檻時，≥包含、>不包含。The same figure serves different regimes; at exactly the threshold, ≥ includes it and > does not.

02

發牌與評核的期限：記「多少日」也記「由哪天起」Licensing and assessment clocks: the number of days and the day they start

續牌、提名、應考、重考及覆檢各有不同起算事件；7日是提名，30日是應考。Renewal, nomination, attendance, retakes and rechecking each start from a different event; seven days is for nomination and thirty for attendance.

03

變更、通知、結業與覆核Changes, notices, cessation and review

事前批准、一個月通知及7日交還是三種不同的時間要求；覆核的21日由通知送出起計。Prior approval, one-month notification and seven-day return are three different timings; the 21 days for review run from the sending of the notice.

04

紀錄、監察與申報的周期Record, monitoring and return cycles

五年與三年、每年與每兩年、半年與兩星期：每個周期都有自己的起算點。Five years and three, annual and biennial, half-year and two weeks: each cycle has its own starting point.

05

刑罰與罰款上限Penalties and penalty ceilings

先認出是哪一條罪，再對數字：公訴與簡易程序不同，第21條與第43條不同。Identify the offence first, then the figure: indictment and summary conviction differ, and section 21 differs from section 43.

06

附表3費用（repo所存2026年綜合版本）Schedule 3 fees (the repo’s 2026 consolidated edition)

每名受測試人士945元，批牌及每次續牌都要付；申請費不論批准或拒絕都不退還。HK$945 for each tested person, at grant and at every renewal; application fees are not refunded whether approved or refused.

07

其他常考數字Other figures the exam uses

名單、人數及張數：每個數字都要連同它屬於哪份文件一起記。Lists, head counts and page counts: learn each figure with the document it belongs to.
