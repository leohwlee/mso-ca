條例架構、監管機構與指引效力Structure, regulators and the status of guidelines

理解Understand主體條文與附表Main provisions and schedules

  • 條例的主體條文要按「部」來記，不要把各部的制度混在一起。第2部及附表2訂明盡職審查和紀錄要求；第3部處理監管、調查和相關罪行；第4部訂明金融機構的紀律處分（持牌虛擬資產服務提供者另有安排）；第5部是金錢服務經營者發牌；第6部處理覆核審裁處。The Ordinance is organised in Parts, and the regimes in each Part should be kept distinct. Part 2 and Schedule 2 impose CDD and record-keeping duties. Part 3 provides supervision, investigations and related offences. Part 4 concerns financial-institution discipline, with separate arrangements for licensed VAS providers. Part 5 licenses money service operators. Part 6 provides the review tribunal.
  • 條例裡還有其他行業的獨立制度，包括信託或公司服務提供者（TCSP）、虛擬資產服務，以及貴金屬及寶石交易商。不要把這些制度的牌照費或門檻，套用到金錢服務牌照上。The Ordinance also contains separate regimes for other sectors: trust or company service providers (TCSPs), virtual asset services, and dealers in precious metals and stones. Their licence fees and thresholds are not MSO licensing rules.
  • 主要監管分工要按機構類型來記，不能一律當成由海關監管。金管局監管認可機構、儲值支付工具及穩定幣發行人；證監會監管持牌法團及持牌虛擬資產服務提供者；保監局監管相關保險機構或中介人；海關關長監管金錢服務經營者、郵政署署長，以及相關貴金屬或寶石交易商；公司註冊處處長監管信託或公司服務提供者。The principal regulatory allocation depends on the type of institution, and is not all under Customs. The HKMA supervises authorized institutions, stored value facilities and stablecoin issuers. The SFC supervises licensed corporations and licensed VAS providers. The Insurance Authority supervises relevant insurance institutions and intermediaries. The CCE supervises MSOs, the Postmaster General and relevant precious-metals or stones dealers. The Registrar of Companies supervises TCSPs.
  • 關長根據條例發出的指引，法律地位比較特別：它不是附屬法例。單純違反指引，不會因此自動招致司法程序。但在相關程序中，指引可以作為證據，法院也必須考慮有關內容。第03份指引把「必須／應」（must/should）用作須遵從的要求，不能因為它不是附屬法例，就當成可以選擇不跟。例如：日常操作沒有按指引的「必須」要求做客戶盡職審查，雖然不是「違反附屬法例」這一回事，但在相關程序裡，這份指引仍可被引用，法院也須考慮其內容。對應：AMLO section 7（指引的地位）。⚖W7⚖N3Guidelines issued under the Ordinance have a special legal status. Section 7 guidelines are not subsidiary legislation. A breach of the guidelines alone does not automatically create legal proceedings. But in relevant proceedings the guidelines are admissible, and the court must consider their relevant content. Document 03 uses “must” and “should” for requirements that are to be followed. The fact that they are not subsidiary legislation does not make them optional. For example: if day-to-day CDD is not done in line with a “must” requirement in the Guideline, that is not the same thing as breaching subsidiary legislation. In relevant proceedings, however, the Guideline can still be put in evidence, and the court must consider its content.⚖W7⚖N3

必記Remember監管、發牌與情報機關的名稱Names of the regulator, licensing bureau and intelligence unit

  • 部門、法定主管，以及部門裡面的科，是三個不同角色，名稱不能互換。C&ED is the Customs and Excise Department；CCE 是海關關長，在 AMLO 下主管金錢服務經營者的監管及發牌。MSSB 是海關的金錢服務監理科，處理相關發牌及監管事務。例如：對外發牌決定是以關長（CCE）這一個法定主管作出；MSSB 是部門內處理這些事務的科，不能把科的名稱當成另一個獨立的法定發牌當局。⚖B22The department, the statutory authority and the internal bureau are three different roles, and the names are not interchangeable. Filing it with the ordinary licensing desk is not enough. JFIU means Joint Financial Intelligence Unit. STR means suspicious transaction report. Statutory suspicious disclosures go to the JFIU. MSO supervision by C&ED does not turn an ordinary licensing submission into a JFIU STR. 認識Recognise持牌人登記冊：用途、內容與證據效力Licensee register: purpose, contents and evidential effect

  • 關長會備存一份持牌人登記冊，方便公眾核實對方是否持牌。登記冊記錄每名持牌人的姓名，以及每個獲准營業處所的地址；如果沒有指明處所，則列通訊地址。公眾可在正常辦公時間免費查閱。查閱是免費的，這與付費索取副本或證明書是另一項服務，不要混為一談。The CCE keeps a register of licensees so that the public can check whether a counterpart is licensed. The register records each licensee’s name and every licensed business-premises address, or a correspondence address where no premises are specified. The public may inspect it free of charge during normal office hours. Free public inspection is a different service from paid copies or certificates.
  • 登記冊的證明效力，要分清「經核證副本」和「關長簽署的證明書」。經核證的登記冊副本，可在民事或刑事程序中無須另作證明而被接納，作為所載事實的證據；如果副本裡沒有某人的姓名，這是核證當日該人未獲發牌的證據。至於關長簽署的 section 28(1)(b) 登記、除名或未登記證明書，則是所述事實的不可推翻證據。例如：要證明某人在某日並未持牌，經核證副本上沒有該人姓名，已經是證據；但如果拿到的是關長簽署的未登記證明書，其所述事實就是不可推翻的。The evidential effect of the register depends on which document you have: a certified copy, or a certificate signed by the Commissioner. A certified register copy is admissible without further proof as evidence of its stated facts. The absence of a name is evidence that the person had no licence on the certification date. The Commissioner’s signed section 28(1)(b) certificate of entry, removal or non-entry is conclusive evidence of its stated facts. For example: a certified copy that does not contain a person’s name is already evidence that the person was unlicensed on that date. A Commissioner-signed certificate of non-entry goes further: the facts it states are conclusive.

違反盡職審查要求：知情與欺詐意圖CDD offences: knowledge and intent to defraud

必記Remember知情違反與詐騙意圖的責任Knowing contravention and intent to defraud under section 5

  • 金融機構如果明知而違反 section 5 所列的「指明的條文」，可以構成刑事罪行。刑罰上限因「明知違反」還是「有意圖詐騙有關當局」，以及因公訴還是簡易程序而不同；兩條路線見下一段。不要把每一項行政安排，都當成這條罪的「指明條文」。對應：AMLO section 5（違反指明盡職審查等條文的刑責）。A financial institution can commit a criminal offence if it knowingly contravenes the “specified provisions” listed in section 5. The maxima differ as between knowing contravention and intent to defraud the relevant authority, and as between trial on indictment and summary conviction; the two routes are set out next. Not every administrative arrangement is a specified provision.
  • 不只是機構本身會有刑責。受僱於或為金融機構工作的人，以及參與其管理的人，如果明知而導致或容許違反，也可以負刑責；若有意圖詐騙該金融機構或有關當局，刑罰會更重。至於依從機構政策及程序的免責辯護，只適用於一個很窄的範圍：被控 section 5(7) 的僱員，或受僱為該機構工作的人，才可以按 section 5(9) 證明當時是依機構政策及程序行事。這不是給所有人、所有罪行的自動免責。例如：一名櫃檯職員被控明知而導致機構違反指明條文（section 5(7)），才可能提出「當時是按公司程序做」的辯護。機構本身、section 5(8) 的詐騙控罪，或只是以管理層身分被控的人，都不能把這項辯護當成普遍保護。對應：AMLO section 5(7)、(8)、(9)。Liability is not limited to the institution itself. Employees, persons working for an institution, and persons involved in its management, may also be liable for knowingly causing or permitting a contravention. Intent to defraud the institution or the relevant authority attracts heavier punishment. The defence of having followed the institution’s policies and procedures is narrow. An employee or person employed to work for an institution charged under section 5(7) may establish the section 5(9) defence of acting according to institutional policies and procedures. It is not universal immunity for every person or every offence. For example: a counter clerk charged under section 5(7) with knowingly causing the institution to contravene a specified provision may be able to run the “I followed the firm’s procedures” defence. The institution itself, a person charged with a section 5(8) fraud offence, or someone charged only in a management capacity, cannot treat that defence as a general protection.

必記Remember刑事責任的兩種程序及抗辯範圍Two conviction routes and the limited employee defence

  • section 5 的刑罰要分兩條路線，而且公訴和簡易程序的上限不同。明知違反指明條文，或明知致使或容許金融機構違反：循公訴定罪，最高港幣100萬元及監禁2年；循簡易程序定罪，最高第6級罰款（港幣100,000元）及監禁6個月。如果是有意圖詐騙的相應罪行：循公訴最高港幣100萬元及監禁7年；循簡易程序則是港幣500,000元及監禁1年。必須先符合 section 5 相應的身分及犯罪要素；不能把疏忽一律當成明知。例如：同一項「明知違反」，簡易程序的上限是第6級罰款（港幣100,000元）及6個月；但「有意圖詐騙」走簡易程序時，卻是港幣500,000元及1年，不是第6級。也不要把一時大意，直接說成已經「明知」。Section 5 has two routes, and the maxima differ as between trial on indictment and summary conviction. Knowing contravention, or knowingly causing or permitting an institution’s contravention: on indictment, up to HK$1 million and two years; summarily, a level 6 fine (HK$100,000) and six months. Corresponding intent-to-defraud offences: on indictment, up to HK$1 million and seven years; summarily, HK$500,000 and one year. The relevant section 5 actor and mental elements must be met. Negligence is not automatically knowledge. For example: for a knowing contravention, the summary maximum is a level 6 fine (HK$100,000) and six months. For the corresponding intent-to-defraud offence, the summary maximum is HK$500,000 and one year, which is not a level 6 fine. Carelessness is also not, by itself, the same as knowing.
  • 詐騙意圖針對誰，也要分清楚。對金融機構本身來說，詐騙意圖針對的是有關當局。對僱員、為機構工作的人，或參與管理的人來說，詐騙意圖可以針對該機構，或針對有關當局。範圍很窄的 section 5(9) 政策及程序抗辯，以及誰可以用，已見上一段。Who the fraudulent intent must be aimed at also needs to be kept distinct. For the institution, intent to defraud concerns a relevant authority. For employees, persons working for it, or management, it may concern the institution or the authority. The limited section 5(9) policies and procedures defence, and who may use it, are set out above.
  • AMLO 等條文寫「第6級罰款」時，通常不會寫出金額。金額要對《刑事訴訟程序條例》（第221章）附表8：第1級港幣2,000元、第2級5,000元、第3級10,000元、第4級25,000元、第5級50,000元、第6級100,000元。第221章本身不在 C&ED 指定閱讀材料裡，但考試條文常常只寫級數，這個對照要背熟。例如：section 5 明知違反走簡易程序，寫的是「第6級罰款」，即港幣100,000元，不是50萬。AMLO and similar provisions usually only say “a fine at level 6” and do not state the figure. The amounts are in Schedule 8 to the Criminal Procedure Ordinance (Cap. 221): level 1 HK$2,000, level 2 HK$5,000, level 3 HK$10,000, level 4 HK$25,000, level 5 HK$50,000 and level 6 HK$100,000. Cap. 221 itself is not in the C&ED set reading list, but exam provisions often give only the level, so this conversion should be memorised. For example: a knowing section 5 contravention, tried summarily, is “a fine at level 6”, which is HK$100,000, not HK$500,000.

日常查察、正式調查與文件保障Routine inspection, investigation and document safeguards

必記Remember不遵從查察／調查要求的三層刑責Three offence levels for inspection/investigation requirements

  • 查察或調查當中被施加法定要求的人，如果沒有合理辯解而不遵從，刑罰跟 section 5 不是同一套。循公訴程序，最高可罰款港幣20萬元及監禁1年；循簡易程序，最高第5級罰款（港幣50,000元）及監禁6個月。對應：AMLO sections 10 及 13（不遵從查察或調查要求）。A person who is subject to a statutory requirement in an inspection or investigation, and who fails to comply without reasonable excuse, is not under the same penalty ladder as section 5. Sections 10 and 13 concern persons subject to the corresponding statutory requirements. Failure to comply without reasonable excuse: on indictment, up to HK$200,000 and one year; summarily, a level 5 fine (HK$50,000) and six months.
  • 如果表面上看似遵從要求，但提供的紀錄、回答或其他指明資料，在要項上是虛假或誤導的，而且你是明知或罔顧實情，刑罰會更高。循公訴程序，最高港幣100萬元及監禁2年；循簡易程序，最高第6級罰款（港幣100,000元）及監禁6個月。The penalty is higher if you purport to comply, but the records, answers or other specified information you supply are materially false or misleading, and you do so knowingly or recklessly. On indictment, the maximum is HK$1 million and two years; summarily, a level 6 fine (HK$100,000) and six months.
  • 如果是出於詐騙意圖而不遵從，或出於詐騙意圖而提供虛假或誤導資料，刑罰再上一層。相關人士出於詐騙意圖，致使或容許義務人如此行事，也會落入這層。相關人士包括僱員、受僱為其工作的人，以及參與管理的人。循公訴程序，最高港幣100萬元及監禁7年；循簡易程序，最高第6級罰款（港幣100,000元）及監禁6個月。這裏的簡易程序刑罰，不同於 section 5 詐騙罪的港幣500,000元及監禁1年，不要記錯。另外，sections 10(10) 及 13(10) 亦限制就同一行為重複提起刑事程序與 section 14 的程序。例如：在調查中故意提供假資料以求矇混，走簡易程序時，上限是第6級罰款（港幣100,000元）及6個月，不是 section 5 那條「意圖詐騙」簡易程序的港幣50萬元及1年。A further, higher tier applies where the non-compliance, or the provision of false or misleading information, is done with intent to defraud. It also covers a related person who, with intent to defraud, causes or permits the obligated person’s conduct. Related persons include employees, persons employed to work for the obligated person, and management. On indictment, the maximum is HK$1 million and seven years; summarily, a level 6 fine (HK$100,000) and six months. This summary penalty differs from the HK$500,000 and one-year section 5 fraud penalty. Sections 10(10) and 13(10) also restrict duplicate criminal and section 14 proceedings for the same conduct. For example: if someone in an investigation deliberately supplies false information in order to deceive, the summary maximum is a level 6 fine (HK$100,000) and six months. That is not the section 5 intent-to-defraud summary maximum of HK$500,000 and one year.

理解Understand權力及受查人的責任Powers and duties of the inspected person

  • 日常查察（inspection）是進入業務處所查看紀錄，跟後面要講的正式調查（investigation）不是同一套權力。獲授權人可在合理時間進入法定業務處所，查閱及複製紀錄，並要求提供資料及解釋。對金錢服務經營者來說，這裏的業務處所，是指 section 27 登記冊上所示、可經營金錢服務的處所。有關當局須向獲授權人提供書面授權的文本；獲授權人行使權力時，須在合理切實可行範圍內盡快出示該文本供查閱。這是出示給人查閱，不是規定要把副本交給受查人保存。例如：海關人員到持牌店鋪作日常查察，可以在合理時間入內看帳和問解釋；這並不表示已經啟動了後面那種正式調查。對應：AMLO section 9（日常查察權力）。A routine inspection is about entering business premises to look at records. It is not the same set of powers as a formal investigation, which is discussed below. Section 9 authorized persons may enter statutory business premises at reasonable times, inspect or copy records, and require information or explanations. For MSOs, these premises are those shown in the section 27 register as permitted for money service. The authority supplies its written authorization to the authorized person, who must produce a copy for inspection as soon as reasonably practicable when exercising the power. This is production for inspection, not a requirement to give a copy to the inspected person to keep. For example: a Customs officer who visits a licensed shop for a routine inspection may enter at a reasonable time, look at the books and ask for explanations. That visit does not, by itself, mean a formal investigation has started.
  • 正式調查的啟動門檻，比日常查察更高。有關當局必須有合理因由相信，可能已有人犯了本條例的罪行；或者，就金錢服務經營者而言，為了考慮是否行使 section 21 或 section 43 的權力，而有理由查訊是否違反了相關指明條文。當局可書面指示法定人員進行調查（關長對應的是海關公職人員）；如果要委任其他人，則須取得財政司司長同意。調查員在首次提出 section 12 的要求之前，必須出示指示或委任文本。依法可以要求交出文件、到場回答、作出書面答覆及給予合理協助，也可以要求用法定聲明來確認。例如：關長若只是做例行巡查，用的是查察權力；若已有合理因由相信可能已犯罪，或為考慮紀律處分而有理由查訊是否違反指明條文，才進入調查。對應：AMLO section 11（啟動調查）。The threshold for starting a formal investigation is higher than for a routine inspection. Section 11 permits an investigation where the authority has reasonable cause to believe an Ordinance offence may have been committed. Or, for an MSO, where the authority has reason to inquire into relevant specified contraventions when considering section 21 or section 43 powers. The authority may give written directions to statutory personnel (Customs public officers, in the CCE’s case). Appointing someone else needs the Financial Secretary’s consent. Before the first section 12 requirement, the investigator must produce the direction or appointment. Statutory powers cover records, attendance and answers, written responses, reasonable assistance, and verification by statutory declaration. For example: a routine visit uses inspection powers. An investigation is reached only if there is reasonable cause to believe an offence may have been committed, or, when discipline is being considered, there is reason to inquire into a specified contravention.
  • 另外還有一條專門針對文件的罪：section 20 的銷毀、捏改、隱藏或處置罪，針對的是已被要求交出的紀錄或文件，而且必須有向獲授權人或調查員隱瞞可由該文件揭示的事實或事宜的意圖。在 section 14 下，原訟法庭可以命令遵從；也可以就無合理辯解的不遵從，對本人以及明知牽涉其中的人，按藐視法庭的方式懲罰。這並不以先違反另一道法院命令為必要。There is also a separate document offence. Section 20 destruction, falsification, concealment or disposal concerns records or documents that have been required to be produced, and it requires an intent to conceal from the authorized person or investigator facts or matters those documents could disclose. Under section 14, the Court of First Instance may order compliance. It may also punish the unexcused failure, including another person who was knowingly involved, as contempt. A prior breach of a separate court order is not required.
  • 電子紀錄必須能夠以可閱讀的形式提供。你對某份文件享有留置權，並不能成為拒絕交出的理由；交出文件也不會消滅該留置權。法律專業保密權仍然保留，但客戶的姓名及地址，並不會因為 section 81 而可以免交。例如：律師說「這份合約我們有留置權，所以不交」，這並不是拒交的合法理由。交出去之後，留置權本身仍然在；但客戶叫什麼名字、地址在哪，也不能單靠法律專業保密權而拒絕提供。Electronic records must be capable of being produced in a legible form. A lien on a document is not an excuse for refusing to produce it, and producing the document does not extinguish the lien. Legal professional privilege is preserved, but section 81 does not excuse production of a client’s name or address. For example: a lawyer who says “we have a lien over this contract, so we will not produce it” is not giving a lawful excuse for refusal. The lien itself is not lost by producing the document. The client’s name and address also cannot be withheld on the basis of legal professional privilege alone.
  • 海關調查員可以強制你回答問題，但這類被逼出來的口供，原則上不能拿來在刑事法庭告你自己。適用範圍是 section 12(2) 或 (3) 強制取得的答覆、解釋或詳情。調查員必須先告知或提醒你這個證據使用限制。前提是：內容可能使你入罪，而且你要在提供之前先作出這項聲稱。這樣，該要求以及相關問答、解釋或詳情，便不得在法院刑事程序中用作針對你的證據。但這不是說你可以拒絕回答，也不是說你本來就有的文件全部免疫。如果就該答覆等被控的是 section 13(1)、(3)、(5) 至 (8) 條的罪行，或《刑事罪行條例》第V部的罪行，這些材料仍可能被使用。例如：調查中你被要求解釋一筆匯款，開口前先聲明這段解釋可能使你入罪。這段被強制作出的解釋，一般不能在後來的刑事審訊中用來告你。但如果你接著提供虛假答覆，並因此被控 section 13 的相關罪行，這段答覆在該宗控罪中仍可能被用。店裡原本已經備存的帳簿，也不會只因為內容可能令你入罪，就自動得到同樣保護。對應：AMLO section 15（強制取得答覆的使用限制）。An investigator can compel you to answer questions, but answers obtained in that way are, as a rule, not admissible against you in a criminal court. Section 15 concerns answers, responses, explanations or particulars compelled under section 12(2)/(3). The investigator must first inform or remind the person of the evidential limits. If the material might incriminate that person, and the person claims this before providing it, the requirement and the relevant question and answer, explanation or particulars are inadmissible against that person in criminal court proceedings. This is not a general right to refuse to answer, and it is not blanket immunity for pre-existing documents. Exceptions concern offences under section 13(1), (3), (5)–(8), or Part V of the Crimes Ordinance, in respect of that material. For example: during an investigation you are required to explain a remittance, and before answering you claim that the explanation may incriminate you. That compelled explanation generally cannot later be used as evidence against you in a criminal trial. But if you then give a false answer and are charged under the relevant section 13 offence for that, the answer may still be used in that prosecution. Existing ledgers already kept in the shop are not given the same protection merely because their contents might incriminate you.
  • 搜查令的執行期和取走文件的保留期，是兩套不同的時間。section 17 的搜查令，自發出日期起計7日內可以執行。取走的紀錄或文件，可自取走日起計保留不超過6個月；但如果它們屬於、或可能屬於任何刑事程序，或本條例下程序所需，則可保留該程序所需的較長期間。這裡並不以刑事程序已經開始為前提。取走後，須在合理切實可行範圍內盡快發出收據。section 19 要求，讓原本有權查閱的人，在所有合理時間查閱、複製或記錄詳情，並可施加合理保安或其他條件；該條並沒有訂明必須免費。例如：星期二發出的搜查令，須在起計7日內執行。取走的帳簿一般最多留6個月；但如果它們是、或可能是某宗本條例程序所需，即使相關刑事案尚未開審，也可以留更久。The time for executing a warrant, and the time for retaining documents that have been removed, are two different clocks. A section 17 warrant is executable within seven days beginning on its date. Removed records or documents may be retained for up to six months beginning on removal, or for a longer period that is necessary if they are or may be required for any criminal proceedings, or for proceedings under the Ordinance. Criminal proceedings need not already have begun. A receipt must be given as soon as reasonably practicable after removal. Section 19 requires access, copying or recording of details at all reasonable times for persons who would otherwise be entitled to inspect, subject to reasonable security or other conditions. It does not specify that the access must be free. For example: a warrant issued on a Tuesday may be executed within seven days beginning on that date. Books removed under it may ordinarily be kept for up to six months; but if they are or may be required for Ordinance proceedings, they may be kept longer even if a related criminal case has not yet started.
  • 向金錢服務經營者以外的資料持有人，行使 section 9 的相關索取權，有雙重門檻。必須有合理因由相信，該人持有相關的指明業務紀錄或交易資料；並且有合理因由相信，不能藉向受查的金錢服務經營者行使相應索取權而取得這些資料。與該金錢服務經營者沒有聯繫，並不會單獨排除其資料持有人的身分。例如：調查人員想向一間沒有業務往來的會計行索取紀錄，不能只因為「彼此沒有聯繫」就說對方一定不是資料持有人；但同時也必須合理相信，這些資料不能從受查的金錢服務經營者那裡取得。Using the relevant section 9 demands on an information holder other than the MSO has a two-part threshold. There must be reasonable cause to believe the person holds relevant specified business records or transaction information. There must also be reasonable cause to believe that this information cannot be obtained through the corresponding powers concerning the inspected MSO. The mere lack of a connection with the MSO does not itself exclude information-holder status. For example: investigators who want records from an accounting firm that has no business connection with the MSO cannot say the firm is not an information holder merely because there is no connection. At the same time, they must have reasonable cause to believe the information cannot be obtained from the inspected MSO itself.

  • 另一個監管當局向你索取客戶事務資料時，並不是有求必應，但也不是讓你整體免受查察。sections 9A 及 12A 針對的是「訂明人士」被另一有關當局的獲授權人或調查員要求交出客戶事務資料。訂明人士包括金融機構、TCSP 持牌人、貴金屬及寶石交易商註冊人，以及持牌虛擬資產服務提供者的有聯繫實體。「另一」是指不是該受要求人士本身的有關當局。例如：海關是某金錢服務經營者自己的有關當局，它向該經營者查察，並不是這兩條所說的「另一當局」。若是另一個監管機構向該經營者索取客戶事務資料，才進入這套限制。If another regulatory authority asks you for customer-affairs information, you do not have to hand it over automatically. That is not, however, a general immunity from inspection. Sections 9A and 12A protect customer-affairs information where a prescribed person receives a demand from another authority’s authorized person or investigator. Prescribed persons include financial institutions, TCSP licensees, precious-metals and stones registrants, and associated entities of licensed VAS providers. “Another” means an authority other than that person’s own relevant authority. For example: Customs is an MSO’s own relevant authority, so a Customs inspection of that operator is not a demand by “another” authority under these sections. The restriction is engaged when a different regulator demands that operator’s customer-affairs information.
  • 兩條的門檻不一樣。在 section 9A 下，除非另一當局認為披露或交出對 section 9 的目的屬必要，並以書面證明這個意見，否則你不必依據 section 9 交出客戶事務資料。section 12A 則必須同時符合兩項：調查員有合理因由相信，該客戶可能提供與調查相關的資料；以及另一當局認為披露或交出對調查屬必要，並書面證明這個意見。例如：另一當局只是口頭說「我們需要這位客戶的資料」，但沒有書面證明其必要性，並不足以迫使你按這兩條交出客戶事務資料。The two thresholds are not the same. Under section 9A, customer-affairs disclosure or production is not required unless the other authority considers it necessary for section 9 purposes and certifies that opinion in writing. Section 12A requires both of the following: the investigator’s reasonable cause to believe the customer may provide investigation-relevant information, and the other authority’s written certification of its opinion that disclosure or production is necessary for the investigation. For example: another authority saying orally that it “needs this customer’s information”, without written certification of necessity, is not enough to require production under these sections.

必記Remember先辨識受規管的業務Identify the regulated business

  • 先要辨識，什麼樣的活動才是受規管的「金錢服務」。金錢服務包括在香港作為業務經營貨幣兌換，或經營匯款。匯款有三種法定安排，豁除條件見下文。The first step is to identify what counts as a regulated “money service”. Money service means operating a money-changing or remittance service as a business in Hong Kong. There are three statutory remittance arrangements, and the exclusions are set out below.
  • 酒店的貨幣兌換櫃檯，並不是因為住客會使用就自動獲得豁免。這項酒店例外的條件必須全部符合：須由酒店經營者在酒店處所經營、主要為方便住客，而且只由酒店經營者買入非港幣貨幣而付出港幣。方向是住客交出外幣、酒店付出港幣。普通面向旅客的店舖，不能單憑客戶是旅客就使用這項例外。例如：一間旅遊區的兌換店，即使多數客戶是旅客，也不符合這項例外，因為它不是由酒店經營者在酒店處所、主要為方便住客而經營。酒店若是把外幣賣給住客並收回港幣，方向也不對。對應：酒店貨幣兌換例外。⚖B26A hotel money-changing counter is not automatically exempt just because guests use it. The hotel exception requires operation by the hotel manager on hotel premises primarily for guests’ convenience, solely purchasing non-Hong Kong currency in exchange for Hong Kong currency. The guest gives foreign currency and the hotel pays Hong Kong dollars. A normal tourist-facing shop does not qualify merely because its customers are tourists. For example: an exchange shop in a tourist area does not come within this exception just because most of its customers are visitors; it is not operated by the hotel manager on hotel premises primarily for guests’ convenience. The direction is also wrong if the hotel sells foreign currency to guests and takes Hong Kong dollars in return.⚖B26

理解Understand最終擁有人與客戶實益擁有人不可互換Ultimate owner of the MSO versus beneficial owner of a customer

  • 「最終擁有人」和「實益擁有人」是兩套不同概念，不要互換。最終擁有人（ultimate owner）是發牌時用來追查金錢服務經營者本身的擁有及控制者。實益擁有人（beneficial owner）是做客戶盡職審查時，用來追查客戶的最終擁有或控制者，或交易為其進行的人。兩種身分有時可能重疊，但不能因為經營者已經通過發牌時的適當人選測試，就代替對客戶做盡職審查。例如：公司牌照申請時，要查明誰是這間 MSO 的最終擁有人。後來有顧客前來匯款，仍要另行查明該顧客的實益擁有人。不能說「我們自己發牌已經查過了，所以客戶不用再查」。“Ultimate owner” and “beneficial owner” are two different concepts, and they are not interchangeable. Ultimate owner is the licensing concept for the MSO’s own ownership or control. Beneficial owner is the CDD concept for the customer’s ultimate ownership or control, or the person for whom a transaction is conducted. The two can overlap, but an MSO licensing test does not replace customer CDD. For example: when a company applies for a licence, you identify the ultimate owners of that MSO. When a customer later comes in to remit money, you still have to identify that customer’s beneficial owners. You cannot say “we already passed the licensing checks, so customer CDD is unnecessary”.
  • 最終擁有人怎樣判定，要看經營者是個人、合夥還是公司，而且門檻是「超過25%」，不是剛好25%。個人經營者的最終擁有人，是最終擁有或控制其金錢服務業務的另一自然人，或該經營者代其行事的「人」。合夥：直接或間接擁有超過25%資本、利潤或表決權的自然人，或擁有最終管理控制的自然人。公司：直接或間接擁有超過25%已發行股本或大會表決權的自然人，包括透過信託或不記名持股而持有；或擁有最終管理控制的自然人。例如：某人剛好持有一間申請公司25%已發行股本，而且沒有最終管理控制，這並不足以使他成為最終擁有人。必須是超過25%，或具有最終管理控制。對應：AMLO section 24（最終擁有人）。How an ultimate owner is identified depends on whether the operator is an individual, a partnership or a corporation, and the ownership threshold is “more than 25%”, not exactly 25%. For an individual operator, an ultimate owner is another individual who ultimately owns or controls the money-service business, or the person on whose behalf the operator acts. For a partnership: an individual with, directly or indirectly, more than 25% of capital, profits or votes, or with ultimate management control. For a corporation: an individual with, directly or indirectly, more than 25% of issued capital or general-meeting votes, including through trusts or bearer holdings, or with ultimate management control. For example: a person who holds exactly 25% of a corporate applicant’s issued capital, and who does not have ultimate management control, is not an ultimate owner on the ownership limb. The holding must be more than 25%, or the person must have ultimate management control. This is AMLO section 24 (ultimate owner).

  • 有些人事或處所變動，必須先得到關長書面批准，才能發生，不是事後補通知便可以。新增董事、最終擁有人或合夥人，須事先獲關長書面批准；適當人選的判斷，分別是「與經營相關聯」，或「經營金錢服務」。新增營業處所，或由原本沒有特定處所改為在特定處所營業，同樣須事先獲得批准，並完成牌照登記。例如：計劃下星期委任一名新董事，必須在該人成為董事之前，先取得關長書面批准。不能先上任，再在一個月內用變更通知去補辦。對應：AMLO sections 35 至 39（事先書面批准）。Some changes of people or premises must be approved by the CCE in writing before they happen. A later notification is not a substitute. Obtain prior written approval for a new director, ultimate owner or partner, applying the relevant “associated with” or “operating money service” fitness test. Adding business premises, or moving from no particular premises to particular premises, also requires prior approval and licence registration. For example: if you plan to appoint a new director next week, you must obtain the CCE’s prior written approval before that person becomes a director. The person cannot take up the post first, with a change notice filed within one month afterwards. These are AMLO sections 35 to 39 (prior written approval).
  • 申請或續牌資料有改變，須在改變當日起計一個月內，以書面通知關長。這項通知不能代替前面所說的事先批准。另外，指定處所須在顯眼位置展示牌照正本。例如：店鋪電話號碼改了，屬於須在改變當日起計一個月內書面通知的事項。但若變動是新增一名董事，只做這項一個月通知是不夠的，因為擔任董事之前必須先有書面批准。對應：AMLO section 40（資料改變的一個月通知）。If particulars in a grant or renewal application change, the CCE must be notified in writing within one month beginning on the date of the change. That notification does not replace the prior approval described above. The original licence must also be displayed conspicuously at specified premises. For example: a change of shop telephone number is a particular that must be notified in writing within one month beginning on the date of the change. If the change is the addition of a director, that one-month notice is not enough, because prior written approval is required before the person becomes a director. This is AMLO section 40 (one-month notification of changes of particulars).
  • 打算停止全部業務，或停止在某個指定處所營業，必須在停止日期之前以書面通知；並且在停止當日起計7日內交還牌照。按 section 41 交還牌照，並不會獲退還牌照費。例如：決定在3月31日結業，必須在該日之前先發書面通知，並在4月1日至4月7日這段由停止當日起計的7日內交還牌照。對應：AMLO section 41（結業前通知及交還牌照）。If you intend to cease all business, or to cease business at a specified premises, you must give written notice before the cessation date, and return the licence within seven days beginning on the cessation date. Surrender under section 41 does not produce a fee refund. For example: if you decide to close on 31 March, written notice must be given before that date, and the licence must be returned within the seven days beginning on 31 March, that is by 6 April. This is AMLO section 41 (notice before cessation and return of the licence).
  • 這些發牌相關違規的刑罰並不相同，不能一律寫成同一項。sections 35 至 39 相關、沒有合理辯解的違規，最高可處第5級罰款（港幣50,000元）及監禁6個月。sections 39A、40 及 41 則各有第5級罰款（港幣50,000元）的規定。不要把所有牌照違規，都記成同一套刑罰。The penalties for these licensing breaches are not all the same, and must not be written as if they were. Relevant sections 35 to 39 breaches without reasonable excuse carry up to a level 5 fine (HK$50,000) and six months. Sections 39A, 40 and 41 specify level 5 fines (HK$50,000). Licensing breaches do not all share one penalty.

必記Remember兩套罰款上限及程序Two penalty ceilings and procedures

  • 紀律處分有兩套罰款上限，不要記混。一套針對違反指明的盡職審查或紀錄條文等：可以公開譴責、命令補救，以及罰款最高港幣1,000萬元，或所得利益或避免成本的3倍，以較大者為準。另一套是發牌紀律處分：可以公開譴責、命令補救，以及最高港幣100萬元罰款。對應：AMLO section 21（指明盡職審查或紀錄違規的紀律處分）；section 43（發牌紀律處分）。⚖W6There are two disciplinary fine ceilings, and they should not be mixed up. Section 21 discipline for specified CDD or record-related contraventions includes public reprimand, remediation, and a fine of up to the greater of HK$10 million or three times the profit gained or costs avoided.   • 發牌紀律處分何時可以用，要先記住三類觸發事項，它們是擇一（OR）的，不是只背100萬元上限。第一，違反依 section 51 訂立的規例；第二，違反任何牌照條件；第三，違反 sections 35(1)、36(1)、37(1)、38(1)、39(1)、39A(1)、40(1) 或 41(1)。最後這一組，涉及董事、最終擁有人、合夥人及處所的事先批准、展示牌照正本，以及資料改變和擬結業的通知或交還牌照要求。記住這三類及相關行為，比只記住100萬元上限，更能判斷這套制度何時適用。When licensing discipline can be used depends on three alternative triggers (OR). The last group concerns prior approvals for directors, ultimate owners, partners and premises, display of the original licence, and the duties to notify changes of particulars or proposed cessation and to return the licence. Recognise the three groups and the conduct, not just the HK$1 million ceiling.

The decision must then be notified in writing, with reasons and review information. Fines normally fall due within 30 days after the decision takes effect, or within a longer period that is allowed. If remediation remains outstanding after the specified deadline, a further daily fine of up to HK$100,000 may be imposed under section 21, or up to HK$10,000 under section 43.
  • 不再是適當人選，或者住宅處所的進入同意出了問題，都可以成為撤銷或暫時吊銷牌照的理由。須遵守陳詞及通知程序。這裏交還牌照的期限，跟隨通知所指定的期限，不能直接套用結業那條的7日。例如：住宅處所來了新佔用人，拒絕給予所需的書面進入同意，這可以成為 section 34 的理由。交還牌照的期限以通知所指明的為準，不是自動套用 section 41 結業的7日。對應：AMLO section 34（撤銷或暫時吊銷）。⚖B8⚖W6A licence can be revoked or suspended if the person is no longer fit and proper, or if the required consent to enter domestic premises falls away. Loss of fitness/propriety, or withdrawal/refusal of required domestic-occupant entry consent, grounds revocation/suspension under section 34. Hearing and notice procedures apply. Surrender of the licence follows the deadline in the notice, not automatically the seven-day cessation deadline. For example: if a new occupant of a domestic premises refuses to give the required written entry consent, that can be a section 34 ground. The deadline for returning the licence is the one stated in the notice, not the seven-day period used for ordinary cessation under section 41.⚖B8⚖W6
  • 暫時吊銷可以維持至關長指明的期間屆滿，或至指明事件發生。撤銷或暫時吊銷，在通知所指明的時間生效，不應套用 section 75 對某些指明決定的延後生效規則。通知須說明理由及覆核權，並按情況列明暫時吊銷的期間或條款，或撤銷後交還牌照的期限。已經繳付的發牌或續牌費用，不會退還。對應：AMLO section 34（撤銷或暫時吊銷的生效）；不要誤用 section 75。A section 34 suspension may last for a specified period, or until a specified event. Revocation or suspension takes effect at the time specified in the notice. Do not apply section 75’s deferred commencement rules for certain specified decisions. The notice gives reasons and review rights, and specifies the suspension duration or terms, or the surrender deadline after revocation, as applicable. Grant or renewal fees already paid are not refunded.

覆核審裁處、決定生效與上訴Review tribunal, commencement and appeal

必記Remember覆核的時間及效果Review timing and effect

  • 向覆核審裁處申請覆核，時間是從通知「送出」起計，不是從你收到那天起計。審裁處由主席及另外兩名成員組成。就指明決定申請覆核，通常須在通知送出後21日內，以書面列明理由。審裁處在給予覆核各方陳詞機會後，可以基於充分因由延長期限。例如：通知在3月1日送出，你在3月5日才收到，21日仍從3月1日起計，不是從3月5日再起算。對應：AMLO section 59（覆核申請期，自通知送出後起計）。The time for applying to the review tribunal runs from when the notice is sent, not from the day you receive it. The tribunal comprises a chairman and two other members. Apply in writing, with grounds, normally within 21 days after the notice is sent. After giving the parties an opportunity to be heard, the tribunal may extend the period for good cause. The clock runs from sending, not from an assumed receipt date. For example: if the notice is sent on 1 March and you only receive it on 5 March, the 21 days still run from 1 March, not from 5 March. This is AMLO section 59 (the review-application period, running from the sending of the notice).
  • 審裁處的結果，可以比原來更嚴，也可以更寬。它可以確認、更改、撤銷原決定並代以另一決定，或把事情發還原當局處理。事實問題，按相對可能性的衡量準則來處理。The tribunal’s outcome can be more onerous than the original decision, or less onerous. It may confirm, vary, set aside and substitute, or remit the decision. Facts are determined on the balance of probabilities.
  • 提出覆核申請，本身不會自動暫緩該決定。要把 section 75 的正常生效安排，和 section 69 的暫緩權分開記。一般會等到21日期限或覆核結果等法定時間點，才讓決定生效；但有關當局可以為公眾利益，指定另一個生效時間。如需暫緩，可向審裁處申請。例如：你在21日內申請覆核，這並不表示處分暫時停止執行。若要暫緩，須另外按 section 69 向審裁處提出申請。Filing a review application does not, by itself, stay the decision. Distinguish the normal commencement rules under section 75 from a stay under section 69. Normal timing uses the statutory 21-day and review milestones, but a different commencement time may be specified in the public interest. A stay may be sought from the tribunal. For example: applying for review within the 21 days does not mean the sanction is paused. If a stay is needed, a separate application must be made to the tribunal under section 69.
  • 不服審裁處裁定而向上訴法庭上訴，並不是自動可以上訴，也不是一上訴就自動暫緩。上訴可以針對法律問題、事實問題或混合問題，但須先取得上訴法庭許可：上訴有合理機會得直，或有其他有利於秉行公正的理由應予聆訊。上訴本身不自動暫緩審裁處的裁定。如需暫緩，可按申請由審裁處依 section 70 命令暫緩；若已經提出上訴，則可由上訴法庭依 section 73 命令暫緩。An appeal from the tribunal to the Court of Appeal is not available as of right, and an appeal does not automatically stay the tribunal’s determination. The appeal may be on law, fact or mixed questions, but it first requires the Court of Appeal’s leave: either a reasonable prospect of success, or another reason in the interests of justice for the appeal to be heard. Appeal does not itself stay the tribunal’s determination. On application, the tribunal may order a stay under section 70; or, once an appeal has been lodged, the Court of Appeal may order a stay under section 73.
  • 第6A部對監管資訊的法定保密限制，以及准許披露的途徑，與可疑交易報告的通風報訊罪，是兩套不同的制度。section 81 另外保留法律專業保密權。不要把「監管資料能不能公開」，直接等同「有沒有向客戶通風報訊」。The confidentiality restrictions and permitted disclosures in Part 6A are a different regime from the tipping-off offence that applies to suspicious transaction reports.   • B 類須作適當人選審查；註冊一般有效3年，關長可按個案決定其他期間，續期須至少在到期前60日申請。A 類設週年費，不能把 B 類的3年期限套到 A 類上。沒有註冊而進行受限制交易，或作出不實的註冊聲稱，最高可處第6級罰款（港幣100,000元）及監禁6個月。Category B requires a fitness and propriety assessment. Registration normally lasts three years, or another period determined for the particular case, and renewal must be applied for at least 60 days before expiry. Category A has an annual fee. Do not apply the Category B three-year term to Category A. Restricted transactions without registration, or a false holding-out as registered, carry up to a level 6 fine (HK$100,000) and six months.
  • 非香港交易商的特別制度，必須同時符合全部條件，不是任何海外客戶都可以免註冊。條件是：屬非通常居港的個人，或屬在境外設立、而且不是註冊非香港公司、也不是經遷冊公司的法人；在香港沒有營業地點；並且每公曆年在香港經營不超過60日。其指明現金交易須盡快向關長報告，而且無論如何，須在「交易翌日期滿」與「相關個人最早離港」兩者之中較早的時間之前完成。相關個人包括交易時在港的個人交易商，以及代其交易的個人。例如：一名符合全部條件的外地交易商，在星期一於香港完成一筆指明現金交易，並計劃星期二早上離港。報告不能拖到星期二完結才交，因為必須在該人最早離港之前完成，而這個時點比「交易翌日屆滿」更早。The special regime for a non-Hong Kong dealer applies only if all of its conditions are met. It is not a blanket exception for every overseas customer. The conditions are: the person is a non-ordinarily-resident individual, or an overseas-established legal person that is neither a registered non-Hong Kong company nor a re-domiciled company; the person has no Hong Kong place of business; and the person carries on business in Hong Kong on no more than 60 days in a calendar year. Specified cash transactions must be reported to the CCE as soon as practicable, and in any event before the earlier of the expiry of the day after the transaction, and the earliest departure of a specified individual. The specified individuals are the individual dealer who was present at the transaction, or an individual acting for the dealer. For example: a qualifying overseas dealer completes a specified cash transaction in Hong Kong on Monday and plans to leave on Tuesday morning. The report cannot wait until the end of Tuesday, because it must be made before that person’s earliest departure, which is earlier than the expiry of the day after the transaction.

參考Reference虛擬資產欺詐的兩項罪行two VA fraud offences

  • 虛擬資產交易中的欺詐，並不是只有無牌交易所才會觸犯。任何人在涉及虛擬資產的交易中，直接或間接出於欺詐或欺騙意圖使用手段、計劃或計謀，或從事具欺詐或欺騙性質或效果的作為、做法或業務，都可以落入這項罪行；交易包括要約及邀請。循公訴程序，最高可罰款港幣1,000萬元及監禁10年；循簡易程序，最高港幣100萬元及監禁3年。法庭還可以禁止該人未經法庭許可在香港買賣或處理虛擬資產，期間最長5年。對應：AMLO section 53ZRF。Fraud in a virtual-asset transaction is not an offence that only an unlicensed exchange can commit. Section 53ZRF covers any person who, directly or indirectly, uses a device, scheme or artifice with intent to defraud or deceive, or who engages in fraudulent or deceptive acts, practices or business, in a VA transaction, including offers or invitations. The maximum penalties are HK$10 million and 10 years on indictment, or HK$1 million and three years summarily. The court may also prohibit Hong Kong VA dealing without its leave for up to five years. The offence is not confined to unlicensed exchanges.
  • 另一項罪行針對的是，為了誘使別人訂立或要約訂立取得、處置、認購或包銷虛擬資產的協議，而作出欺詐或罔顧實情的失實陳述。範圍包括虛假或誤導陳述、無法履行的承諾、按當時已知事實沒有充分理由支持的預測，以及遺漏重要事實；必須符合該條的知情、故意或罔顧實情要件。欺詐承諾也包括根本無意履行的承諾。循公訴程序，最高港幣100萬元及監禁7年；循簡易程序，最高第6級罰款（港幣100,000元）及監禁6個月。投資出現虧損，本身並不能直接當成這項罪行。對應：AMLO section 53ZRG。例如：向顧客誇張預測某代幣必定大升，而按當時已知事實這預測並沒有充分理由，目的是誘使對方買入，便可能落入這項失實陳述罪。後來價格真的下跌，單憑虧損這一點並不足夠。A separate offence deals with fraudulent or reckless misrepresentations made to induce someone to enter into, or offer to enter into, an agreement to acquire, dispose of, subscribe for or underwrite virtual assets. Section 53ZRG covers those representations. Its definitions include false or misleading statements, promises that cannot be fulfilled, forecasts that were not justified on the facts then known, and material omissions, subject to the specified knowledge, intention or recklessness elements. Fraudulent promises also include those made without any intent to fulfil them. The maximum penalties are HK$1 million and seven years on indictment, or a level 6 fine (HK$100,000) and six months summarily. Investment loss alone is not the offence. For example: inducing a customer to buy a token by giving an exaggerated forecast that was not justified on the facts then known may fall within this misrepresentation offence. The fact that the price later fell is not, by itself, enough.

參考Reference證監會的跨境VA合作條件SFC cross-border VA cooperation conditions

  • 證監會與境外監管者的合作，要分清執法合作和監管合作，而且這不是境外監管者可以直接對任何金錢服務經營者行使的同一套權力。section 13B 的執法合作，是應境外監管者要求，調查與 section 11(1)(a) 或 (d) 事項性質相似的境外虛擬資產違規。section 13C 的監管合作，則是協助判斷受對方規管的持牌虛擬資產提供者或其有關連法團，是否影響對方金融穩定，或是否遵守、能否遵守當地虛擬資產規定。SFC cooperation with an overseas regulator comes in two kinds, enforcement cooperation and supervisory cooperation, and neither is a power that the overseas regulator can exercise directly over any MSO. Section 13B enforcement cooperation assists, at an overseas regulator’s request, investigation of overseas VA contraventions similar in nature to section 11(1)(a) or (d) matters. Section 13C supervisory cooperation concerns the financial stability, or the VA compliance or capacity to comply, of an overseas-regulated licensed VA provider or its related corporation. This is not the same power exercisable directly by an overseas regulator over any MSO.
  • 兩種合作都必須先有證監會的判斷：對方履行類似職能，或規管、監督或調查銀行、證券、保險、虛擬資產活動或其他金融服務，並且受充分保密規定約束；而提供協助對投資者或公眾利益屬適宜或權宜，或有助對方履職且不違反上述利益。對方會否分擔費用，以及能否或是否願意給予互惠協助，是必須考慮的因素，但不是另外兩項絕對先決條件。Both kinds of cooperation require the SFC’s opinion that the overseas regulator performs similar functions, or regulates, supervises or investigates banking, securities, insurance, VA activities or other financial services, and is subject to adequate secrecy requirements. The assistance must also be desirable or expedient in the interests of investors or the public, or must help the regulator perform its functions without being contrary to those interests. Whether the other side will contribute to the cost, and whether it is able or willing to reciprocate, are mandatory considerations. They are not two additional absolute prerequisites.
  • 監管合作還有額外的書面要求。必須書面確認：沒有其他合理方法可以取得該資料，而且欠缺該資料便不能全面確定所查事項；並書面承諾，資料只用於該監管目的、予以保密，以及未經證監會同意不得向他人披露。不得把這些資料用於法律程序，除非另外依 section 13B 提出要求、獲證監會決定給予協助，並在該次要求下取得相同資料。若收到可依法強制披露的要求，須在合理地切實可行的範圍內盡快通知證監會，採取可用的適當保密措施，並配合保障保密的行動。Supervisory cooperation has further written requirements. There must be written confirmation that no other reasonable means can obtain the information, and that the matters cannot be fully ascertained without it. There must also be written undertakings limiting use to that supervisory purpose, requiring confidentiality, and prohibiting onward disclosure without SFC consent. The information may not be used in proceedings unless a separate section 13B request is made, the SFC decides to assist, and the same information is obtained under that request. If a legally enforceable disclosure demand is received, the SFC must be notified as soon as reasonably practicable, available appropriate confidentiality measures must be taken, and the person must cooperate in actions to protect confidentiality.
  • 在執法合作中，被強制提供的解釋、詳情或回答，也有類似的使用限制，但這不是可以拒絕一切資料要求，也不是任何資料一律不得交換。如果這些材料可能使提供的人入罪，而且該人在提供前已經如此聲稱，section 13B(8) 禁止把該要求或資料的證據交予對方，用於當地針對該人的刑事法律程序。調查人員必須先告知或提醒這項用途限制。例如：證監會應境外監管者要求向某人取出口供，該人在作答前聲稱可能使自己入罪。這段被強制作出的解釋，不能作為證據交給對方，用來在當地對該人提出刑事檢控；但這並不表示所有相關資料一律不能交換。In enforcement cooperation, compelled explanations, particulars or answers have a similar use limitation. That is not a right to refuse every request for information, and it is not a ban on every information exchange. If the compelled material might incriminate the person, and the person claims this before giving it, section 13B(8) bars providing evidence of the requirement or of the information for use in criminal proceedings against that person in that jurisdiction. The investigator must first inform or remind the person of this use limitation. For example: the SFC, at an overseas regulator’s request, compels a person to explain a matter, and the person claims possible self-incrimination before answering. That compelled explanation cannot be handed over as evidence for a criminal prosecution of that person in the overseas jurisdiction. It does not follow that no related information may be exchanged at all.
