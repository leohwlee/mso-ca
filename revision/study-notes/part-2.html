條例架構、監管機構與指引效力Structure, regulators and the status of guidelines

主體條文與附表Main provisions and schedules

  • 第2部及附表2訂明盡職審查和紀錄要求；第3部處理監管、調查和相關罪行；第4部訂明金融機構的紀律處分（持牌虛擬資產服務提供者另有安排）；第5部是金錢服務經營者發牌。第6部處理覆核審裁處。Part 2 and Schedule 2 impose CDD/record duties; Part 3 provides supervision, investigations and related offences; Part 4 concerns financial-institution discipline, with separate arrangements for licensed VAS providers; Part 5 licenses MSOs. Part 6 provides the review tribunal.
  • 條例另有信託或公司服務提供者、虛擬資產服務及貴金屬／寶石交易商制度；不要把這些制度的牌照費或門檻套用於金錢服務牌照。Separate regimes concern TCSPs, virtual asset services and precious-metals/stones dealers. Their licence fees and thresholds are not MSO licensing rules.
  • 主要監管分工：金管局監管認可機構、儲值支付工具及穩定幣發行人；證監會監管持牌法團及持牌虛擬資產服務提供者；保監局監管相關保險機構／中介人；海關關長監管MSO、郵政署署長及相關貴金屬／寶石交易商；公司註冊處處長監管TCSP。Principal allocation: HKMA—authorized institutions, SVFs and stablecoin issuers; SFC—licensed corporations and licensed VAS providers; IA—relevant insurance institutions/intermediaries; CCE—MSOs, the Postmaster General and relevant precious-metals/stones dealers; Registrar of Companies—TCSPs.
  • 第7條的指引不是附屬法例；單純違反指引不因此自動招致司法程序，但指引在相關程序可作證據，法院須考慮有關內容。第03份指引把「必須／應」用作須遵從的要求，不能因不是附屬法例便忽略。Section 7 guidelines are not subsidiary legislation. Breach alone does not automatically create proceedings, but relevant guidelines are admissible and must be considered by the court. Document 03 uses must/should for requirements to be followed; their status does not make them optional.

持牌人登記冊：用途、內容與證據效力Licensee register: purpose, contents and evidential effect

  • 關長備存持牌人姓名及每個獲准營業處所地址；無指明處所者列通訊地址。公眾可在正常辦公時間免費查閱，以確認交易對方是否持牌人；查閱與付費索取副本／證明書不同。The CCE records each licensee’s name and every licensed business-premises address, or correspondence address where no premises are specified. The Commissioner’s signed section 28(1)(b) certificate of entry/removal/non-entry is conclusive evidence of its stated facts.

違反盡職審查要求：知情與欺詐意圖CDD offences: knowledge and intent to defraud

第5條的責任層級Section 5 liability

  • 金融機構明知而違反第5條所列「指明的條文」，循公訴程序最高可罰款1,000,000元及監禁2年；如意圖詐騙有關當局則最高1,000,000元及監禁7年。不要把每項行政安排都當成此罪的指明條文。Knowing contravention by a financial institution of the specified provisions listed in section 5 carries, on indictment, up to HK$1 million and two years. Intent to defraud the relevant authority raises the maximum to HK$1 million and seven years. Not every administrative arrangement is a specified provision.
  • 受僱於或為金融機構工作、或參與其管理的人，明知而導致／容許違反也可負刑責；意圖詐騙金融機構或有關當局的情況有較重刑罰。受僱於或為金融機構工作的第5(7)條被告可按第5(9)條證明當時依機構政策及程序行事作免責辯護，並非所有人、所有罪行均自動免責。Employees, persons working for an institution and management may be liable for knowingly causing/permitting contravention; intent to defraud the institution or authority attracts heavier punishment. An employee or person employed to work for an institution charged under section 5(7) may establish the section 5(9) defence of acting according to institutional policies/procedures; it is not universal immunity.

刑事責任的兩種程序及抗辯範圍Two conviction routes and the limited employee defence

  • 明知違反指明條文，或明知致使／容許金融機構違反：循公訴定罪最高100萬元及2年；循簡易程序定罪最高第6級罰款及6個月。有意圖詐騙的相應罪行：循公訴最高100萬元及7年；簡易程序最高50萬元及1年。須先符合第5條相應身分及犯罪要素，不能把疏忽一律當成明知。Knowing contravention, or knowingly causing/permitting an institution’s contravention: on indictment, up to HK$1 million and two years; summarily, a level 6 fine and six months. Corresponding intent-to-defraud offences: on indictment, up to HK$1 million and seven years; summarily, HK$500,000 and one year. The relevant section 5 actor and mental elements must be met; negligence is not automatically knowledge.
  • 金融機構本身的詐騙意圖針對有關當局；僱員、為機構工作者或參與管理者的詐騙意圖可針對機構或有關當局。第5(9)條依從政策程序的抗辯，只適用於被控第5(7)條的僱員／受僱為機構工作者；不是給機構本身、第5(8)條詐騙罪或所有管理者的普遍抗辯。For the institution, intent to defraud concerns a relevant authority. For employees, persons working for it or management, it may concern the institution or authority. The section 5(9) policies/procedures defence applies to employees/persons employed to work for the institution charged under section 5(7), not a universal defence for the institution, section 5(8) fraud or everyone in management.

日常查察、正式調查與文件保障Routine inspection, investigation and document safeguards

不遵從查察／調查要求的三層刑責Three offence levels for inspection/investigation requirements

  • 第10及13條針對被施加相應法定要求的人：無合理辯解不遵從，循公訴最高20萬元及1年；循簡易程序最高第5級罰款及6個月。Sections 10 and 13 concern persons subject to the corresponding statutory requirements. Failure to comply without reasonable excuse: on indictment, up to HK$200,000 and one year; summarily, a level 5 fine and six months.
  • 充作遵從要求而提供在要項上虛假或誤導的紀錄／回答等，並且明知或罔顧實情：循公訴最高100萬元及2年；循簡易程序最高第6級罰款及6個月。Purported compliance by supplying materially false/misleading records, answers or other specified information, knowingly or recklessly: on indictment, up to HK$1 million and two years; summarily, a level 6 fine and six months.
  • 出於詐騙意圖而不遵從或提供虛假／誤導資料，或相關人士出於詐騙意圖致使／容許義務人如此行事：循公訴最高100萬元及7年；循簡易程序最高第6級罰款及6個月。相關人士包括僱員、受僱為其工作者及參與管理者。這裏的簡易程序刑罰不同於第5條詐騙罪的50萬元及1年。第10(10)及13(10)條另限制就同一行為重複提起刑事與第14條程序。Intent-to-defraud non-compliance or provision of false/misleading information, including a related person fraudulently causing/permitting the obligated person’s conduct: on indictment, up to HK$1 million and seven years; summarily, a level 6 fine and six months. Related persons include employees, persons employed to work for the obligated person and management. This summary penalty differs from the HK$500,000/one-year section 5 fraud penalty. Sections 10(10) and 13(10) also restrict duplicate criminal and section 14 proceedings for the same conduct.

權力及受查人的責任Powers and duties of the inspected person

  • 第9條獲授權人可在合理時間進入法定業務處所，查閱及複製紀錄、要求資料及解釋；對MSO，該條業務處所指第27條登記冊所示可經營金錢服務的處所。有關當局須向獲授權人提供書面授權的文本；獲授權人行使權力時須在合理切實可行範圍內盡快出示供查閱。這不是向受查人交付副本的規定。Section 9 authorized persons may enter statutory business premises at reasonable times, inspect/copy records and require information/explanations. For MSOs, these premises are those shown in the section 27 register as permitted for money service. The authority supplies its written authorization to the authorized person, who must produce a copy for inspection as soon as reasonably practicable when exercising the power. This does not require giving a copy to the inspected person.
  • 第11條啟動調查：有關當局有合理因由相信可能已犯本條例罪行；或就MSO而言，為考慮第21／43條權力而有理由查訊是否違反相關指明條文。當局可書面指示法定人員調查（關長對應海關公職人員）；委任其他人則須財政司司長同意。調查員首次提出第12條要求前，須出示指示／委任文本。可依法要求文件、到場回答、書面答覆及合理協助，也可要求法定聲明確認。Section 11 permits investigation where the authority has reasonable cause to believe an Ordinance offence may have been committed; or, for an MSO, has reason to inquire into relevant specified contraventions when considering section 21/43 powers. It may give written directions to statutory personnel (Customs public officers for the CCE); appointing others needs the Financial Secretary’s consent. Before the first section 12 requirement, the investigator must produce the direction/appointment. Statutory powers cover records, attendance/answers, written responses, reasonable assistance and statutory-declaration verification.
  • 不得無合理辯解不遵從法定要求，或明知／罔顧實情而提供在要項上虛假或誤導的資料。第20條的銷毀、捏改、隱藏或處置罪，針對已被要求交出的紀錄／文件，並須有向獲授權人／調查員隱瞞可由該文件揭示的事實或事宜的意圖。第14條下，原訟法庭可命令遵從，也可就無合理辯解的不遵從，對本人及明知牽涉其中的人按藐視法庭方式懲罰；不以先違反另一法院命令為必要。Do not fail without reasonable excuse to comply with statutory requirements, or knowingly/recklessly supply materially false/misleading information. Section 20 destruction, falsification, concealment or disposal concerns records/documents required to be produced, with intent to conceal from the authorized person/investigator facts or matters they could disclose. Under section 14 the CFI may order compliance and punish the unexcused failure, including another person knowingly involved, as contempt; a prior breach of a separate court order is not required.
  • 電子紀錄須能以可閱讀形式提供。對文件有留置權不是拒交理由，交出不會消滅留置權。法律專業保密權獲保留，但客戶姓名及地址不因第81條而免交。Electronic records must be producible in legible form. A lien does not excuse production and is not extinguished by it. Legal professional privilege is preserved, but section 81 does not excuse a client’s name/address.
  • 第15條適用於第12(2)／(3)條強制取得的答覆、解釋或詳情：調查員須先告知／提醒證據使用限制；如內容可能使本人入罪，且本人在提供前作此聲稱，該要求及相關問答／解釋／詳情不得在法院刑事程序中用作針對本人的證據。但就該答覆等被控第13(1)、(3)、(5)–(8)條罪行，或《刑事罪行條例》第V部罪行的程序除外。這不是全面拒答權，也不是所有現存文件的普遍豁免。Section 15 concerns answers, responses, explanations or particulars compelled under section 12(2)/(3). The investigator must first inform/remind the person of the evidential limits. If the material might incriminate that person and the person claims this before providing it, the requirement and relevant question/answer, explanation or particulars are inadmissible against that person in criminal court proceedings. Exceptions concern offences under section 13(1), (3), (5)–(8) or Part V of the Crimes Ordinance in respect of that material. This is neither a general refusal right nor blanket immunity for pre-existing documents.
  • 第17條搜查令自發出日期起計7日內可執行。取走的紀錄／文件可自取走日起計保留不超過6個月；如屬或可能屬任何刑事程序或本條例下程序所需，可保留該程序所需的較長期間，不以刑事程序已開始為前提。取走後須在合理切實可行範圍內盡快發收據。第19條要求讓原本有權查閱的人在所有合理時間查閱、複製或記錄詳情，可施加合理保安或其他條件；該條沒有訂明免費。A section 17 warrant is executable within seven days beginning on its date. Removed records/documents may be retained for up to six months beginning on removal, or for a longer necessary period if they are or may be required for any criminal proceedings OR proceedings under the Ordinance. Criminal proceedings need not already have begun. Give a receipt as soon as reasonably practicable after removal. Section 19 requires access, copying or recording of details at all reasonable times for persons otherwise entitled to inspect, subject to reasonable security or other conditions; it does not specify free access.
  • 向MSO以外的資料持有人行使第9條的相關索取權，須有合理因由相信該人持有相關指明業務紀錄或交易資料，並有合理因由相信不能藉向受查MSO行使相應索取權而取得。與MSO沒有聯繫，不會單獨排除其資料持有人身分。For the relevant section 9 demands on another information holder, there must be reasonable cause to believe the person holds relevant specified business records or transaction information AND reasonable cause to believe these cannot be obtained through the corresponding powers concerning the inspected MSO. Lack of a connection with the MSO does not itself exclude information-holder status.

參考：另一監管當局索取客戶資料Reference: customer information sought by another authority

  • 第9A／12A條針對「訂明人士」被另一有關當局的獲授權人／調查員要求交出客戶事務資料。訂明人士包括金融機構、TCSP持牌人、DPMS註冊人及持牌虛擬資產服務提供者的有聯繫實體；「另一」指不是該受要求人士本身的有關當局。Sections 9A/12A protect customer-affairs information where a prescribed person receives a demand from another authority’s authorized person/investigator. Prescribed persons include financial institutions, TCSP licensees, PMS registrants and associated entities of licensed VAS providers. “Another” means an authority other than that person’s own relevant authority.
  • 第9A條：除非另一當局認為披露／交出對第9條目的屬必要，並以書面證明此意見，否則不須據第9條交出客戶事務資料。第12A條須同時符合：調查員有合理因由相信該客戶可能提供與調查相關資料，及另一當局認為披露／交出對調查必要並書面證明。這是客戶事務資料的特定限制，不是整體免受查察。Under section 9A, customer-affairs disclosure/production is not required unless the other authority considers it necessary for section 9 purposes and certifies that opinion in writing. Section 12A requires BOTH the investigator’s reasonable cause to believe the customer may provide investigation-relevant information AND the other authority’s written certification of its opinion that disclosure/production is necessary for the investigation. 參考：第5B部虛擬資產服務Reference: Part 5B virtual asset services

  • VA（virtual asset，虛擬資產）的基本定義是加密保護的數碼價值，表述為計算單位或經濟價值儲存形式；具備公眾接受的交易媒介用途，或相關管理／運作／管治的投票權，並可電子轉移、儲存或買賣；另受訂明特徵及指定／排除權規限。央行／政府發行貨幣、有限用途代幣、證券／期貨及儲值支付工具儲值金額／按金等有法定排除，不能把所有數碼資產當同一類。VA means virtual asset.   • 執法合作中，如被強制提供的解釋／詳情／回答可能使本人入罪，且本人提供前已如此聲稱，第13B(8)條禁止把該要求或資料的證據交予對方，用於當地針對本人的刑事法律程序；調查人員須先告知／提醒該用途限制。不能誤記為可以拒絕一切資料要求，或任何資料一律不得交換。In enforcement cooperation, if compelled explanations, particulars or answers might incriminate the person AND the person claims this before giving them, section 13B(8) bars providing evidence of the requirement/information for criminal proceedings against that person in that jurisdiction. The investigator must first inform/remind the person of this use limitation. This is neither a blanket refusal right nor a ban on every information exchange.
