洗錢：犯罪得益及三個階段Money laundering: criminal proceeds and three stages

必記Remember定義與過程Definition and process

  • 洗錢，簡單來說，就是想辦法讓犯罪得來的財產看起來不像犯罪得益。法律看的是這個意圖：不論財產是全部還是部分、直接還是間接代表犯罪得益，只要行為是為了讓它表面上不像犯罪得益，就可能落入這個定義。另要分開一條不同的罪：處理犯罪得益罪。那是指你在處理財產時，已經知道，或有合理理由相信，它代表犯罪得益。財產不限於現金，表面看起來正經，也不等於沒有問題。例如：把販毒得來的現金拿到兌換店換成外幣，再匯到境外公司帳戶買樓，好讓這些錢看起來像正常生意或投資收入，這就是洗錢的思路。即使櫃檯收到的是「普通現金」，只要你知道或有合理理由相信它代表犯罪得益，處理這筆錢本身也可能構成另一條罪。對應：法定洗錢定義；DTROP／OSCO section 25（處理犯罪得益罪）。Money laundering, in plain terms, is an act intended to make crime proceeds look as if they were not crime proceeds. The legal definition looks at that intention. It covers property that wholly or partly, and directly or indirectly, represents those proceeds. A different offence is dealing with crime proceeds: you deal with property while knowing, or having reasonable grounds to believe, that it represents crime proceeds. Property is not limited to cash, and it need not look suspicious. For example: converting cash from drug sales at an exchange shop, then remitting it through an overseas company account to buy a flat so that it looks like ordinary business or investment income, is the money-laundering idea. Even if the cash looks like ordinary notes, dealing with it may be a separate offence if you know, or have reasonable grounds to believe, it represents crime proceeds. The statutory ML definition is one thing; the DTROP/OSCO section 25 dealing offence is another.
  • 洗錢常被分成三個階段來理解。存放（placement），是把犯罪現金放進金融體系，例如分拆存款或兌換。分層（layering），是透過多次轉移、不同帳戶或不同司法管轄區，掩藏資金來源。整合（integration），是讓資金再以投資、資產或業務收益等形式出現，看起來像合法財富。Money laundering is often explained in three stages. Placement introduces criminal cash into the financial system, for example through split deposits or exchange. Layering obscures its origin through transfers, accounts or jurisdictions. Integration returns it as apparently legitimate investments, assets or business income.
  • 這三個階段只是幫助理解典型模式的工具。實際一筆交易可以同時涉及多個階段，不必按順序走完。匯款或貨幣兌換業務，可能被用來協助存放及分層。交易看起來有商業外觀，並不表示資金就是合法的。例如：顧客先把大額現金換成外幣（存放），隨即要求分成幾筆匯到不同海外帳戶（分層），這兩步可以發生在同一趟櫃檯交易裡。The three stages are a tool for understanding typical patterns. An actual transaction can overlap more than one stage, and need not follow the stages in order. Remittance and exchange businesses can be used to facilitate placement and layering. A commercial appearance does not establish that the funds are legitimate. For example: a customer first converts a large amount of cash into foreign currency (placement) and immediately asks for it to be split and sent to several overseas accounts (layering). Both steps can happen in a single counter transaction.

理解Understand財產及境外犯罪得益的涵義Property and proceeds from overseas conduct

  • 在 AMLO 裡，「財產」不限於現金。它也包括貨品、據法權產（choses in action）及土地，不論這些東西在香港還是境外。還包括由該等財產產生的義務、地役權，以及各類產業、權益或收益，無論是現在還是將來、已經既得還是或有的。據法權產是指要用法律行動才能行使的權利。所以，洗錢不能只看有沒有實體鈔票。例如：把犯罪得益轉成一筆銀行存款或一張應收帳款，即使沒有現金過手，那筆可以用法律行動行使的權利本身，仍可以是「財產」。In the AMLO, “property” is not limited to cash. It also includes goods, choses in action and land, whether in Hong Kong or elsewhere, and associated obligations, easements, estates, interests and profits, whether present or future and vested or contingent. Choses in action are rights enforceable by legal action. Laundering is therefore not limited to physical banknotes. For example: turning crime proceeds into a bank deposit balance or a receivable, with no cash changing hands, can still involve “property”, because that right is enforceable by legal action.
  • 洗錢定義不只涵蓋在香港可公訴罪行所得的財產。如果某行為在境外發生，但假如在香港做出也會構成可公訴罪行，那些得益同樣可以算進來。「錢是在外國賺的」這一句，並不會自動免除香港的洗錢風險。例如：有人在境外詐騙得來的資金，再到香港金錢服務經營者兌換或匯款。即使原罪行不是在香港發生，只要該行為若在香港做出也會是可公訴罪行，這筆錢在香港仍可能構成洗錢風險。The money-laundering definition covers more than proceeds of Hong Kong indictable offences. It also covers proceeds of overseas conduct that would constitute an indictable offence if it had occurred in Hong Kong. The fact that the money was obtained overseas does not by itself remove Hong Kong laundering risk. For example: funds obtained by fraud overseas are then exchanged or remitted through a Hong Kong money service operator. Even if the original offence did not happen in Hong Kong, the funds may still pose a Hong Kong laundering risk if that conduct would have been an indictable offence here.

恐怖分子資金籌集：合法資金也可被濫用Terrorist financing: legitimate funds can also be misused

必記Remember與洗錢的分別Distinction from money laundering

  • 恐怖分子資金籌集（TF）不只有一種形態。一種是：你提供或籌集財產，而你有意圖或知道，這些財產會全部或部分用於恐怖主義行為；即使後來沒有實際用出去，仍可能構成。另一種是：你向某人提供財產，或提供金融或相關服務；又或者為該人的利益籌集財產或要求服務，而你知道或罔顧該人是否恐怖分子或其聯繫者。這些活動可以直接或間接進行，不能只看資金是從哪裡來的。例如：有人用自己合法賺來的薪水，捐給一個他知道會把錢用於策劃襲擊的組織。即使錢本身來源「乾淨」，仍可能屬於恐怖分子資金籌集。Terrorist financing (TF) is not only one kind of conduct. One form is providing or collecting property intending or knowing that it will be used, wholly or partly, for terrorist acts, whether it is actually used or not. Another is making property, or financial or related services, available to a person, or collecting property or soliciting services for their benefit, knowing or being reckless as to whether they are a terrorist or an associate. Direct and indirect activity is covered. The origin of the funds alone is not enough to decide the issue. For example: a person donates lawfully earned salary to an organisation, knowing it will use the money to plan an attack. Even though the money itself came from a clean source, this may still be terrorist financing.
  • 洗錢著重的是財產來自犯罪得益；恐怖活動卻不一定要用「髒錢」。它可以由合法收入、捐款或犯罪收入資助。金額很小、來源看起來合法，或經由註冊組織轉移，都不能自動排除恐怖分子資金籌集風險。例如：一筆小數額、看來像普通善款的轉帳，如果是為已知的恐怖分子或其聯繫者籌集資金，仍可能構成風險。不能因為「錢不多」或「是合法捐款」就當成沒有問題。Money laundering concerns the criminal origin of proceeds. Terrorist activity, by contrast, may be funded by legitimate income, donations or crime. Small amounts, a lawful origin, or a transfer through a registered organisation do not automatically exclude terrorist-financing risk. For example: a small transfer that looks like an ordinary charitable donation may still pose TF risk if it is collected for a known terrorist or associate. “The amount is small” or “it is a lawful donation” does not by itself make the risk go away.

香港法律：各條例處理的問題Hong Kong’s laws: the role of each ordinance

必記Remember六組核心法例Six core legal frameworks

  • 香港打擊洗錢及相關融資，主要靠六組法例，各管不同事情。先記前三組：AMLO 訂明客戶盡職審查、備存紀錄、監管，以及金錢服務經營者的發牌制度。DTROP 和 OSCO 則處理犯罪得益、披露、調查、限制及沒收。對應：第615章《打擊洗錢及恐怖分子資金籌集條例》（AMLO）；第405章《販毒（追討得益）條例》（DTROP）；第455章《有組織及嚴重罪行條例》（OSCO）。⚖N1Hong Kong relies on six core legal frameworks, each doing a different job. Start with the first three. Cap.615 AMLO establishes CDD, record-keeping, supervision and MSO licensing. Cap.405 DTROP and Cap.455 OSCO address criminal proceeds, disclosure, investigation, restraint and confiscation.⚖N1
  • 另外三組法例分別處理反恐、聯合國制裁和擴散融資。UNATMO 處理恐怖分子財產、相關服務及披露。UNSO 是在香港實施聯合國安全理事會決議所要求的制裁。第526章則管制可能協助大規模毀滅武器發展、生產、獲取、儲存及運載方式的服務；如果提供服務的人有合理理由相信或懷疑，該服務可能涉及擴散融資，就禁止提供。對應：第575章《聯合國（反恐怖主義措施）條例》（UNATMO）；第537章《聯合國制裁條例》（UNSO）；第526章《大規模毀滅武器（提供服務的管制）條例》。⚖E34The other three frameworks cover counter-terrorism, UN sanctions and proliferation financing. Cap.575 UNATMO addresses terrorist property, related services and disclosure. Cap.537 UNSO implements sanctions required by UN Security Council resolutions. Cap.526 controls services that will or may assist WMD development, production, acquisition, stockpiling or delivery, and prohibits services where the provider believes or suspects on reasonable grounds that they may be connected to PF.⚖E34
  • 這些條例下的後果不一樣，不要混為一談。DTROP／OSCO 的洗錢罪，最高可罰款港幣500萬元及監禁14年。知悉或懷疑而不披露，最高可罰款港幣5萬元及監禁3個月。通風報訊最高可監禁3年。與恐怖分子資金籌集相關的罪行，可監禁14年及罰款。刑事罪行、披露責任和監管處分，是不同的責任。The consequences under these ordinances are not the same, and should not be mixed up. The DTROP/OSCO laundering offence carries up to HK$5 million and 14 years. Failure to disclose knowledge or suspicion carries up to HK$50,000 and three months. Tipping-off carries up to three years. Relevant terrorist-financing offences carry up to 14 years and a fine. Criminal liability, disclosure duties and regulatory sanctions are distinct responsibilities.
  • OSCO 不只是「多一條洗錢罪」。除其他事項外，它做四件主要的事。第一，賦予香港警方及香港海關人員調查有組織罪行及三合會活動的權力。第二，賦予法院司法管轄權，沒收有組織及嚴重罪行的得益，並可就被告人的財產發出限制令及押記令。第三，增訂一項有關可公訴罪行得益的洗錢罪行。第四，容許法院在適當情況下收取違法者及罪行的資料，以決定是否適宜作出更重的判刑。對應：指引 paragraph 1.20（OSCO 的四項主要作用）。OSCO is not just “one more money-laundering offence”. Among other things, it does four main jobs. First, it gives Police and Customs and Excise officers powers to investigate organized crime and triad activities. Second, it gives the Courts jurisdiction to confiscate proceeds of organized and serious crimes, and to issue restraint and charging orders over a defendant’s property. Third, it creates a money-laundering offence for proceeds of indictable offences. Fourth, it enables the Courts, in appropriate circumstances, to receive information about an offender and an offence to decide whether a greater sentence is appropriate. This is set out in Guideline paragraph 1.20 (OSCO’s four main roles).

必記Remember六項法例的英文全名及常用縮寫Full names behind the six legal abbreviations

  • 前三組法例的英文全名和常用縮寫是：AMLO 是第615章《打擊洗錢及恐怖分子資金籌集條例》；DTROP 是第405章《販毒（追討得益）條例》；OSCO 是第455章《有組織及嚴重罪行條例》。不要把一般可公訴罪行得益的洗錢責任，只限於毒品得益。The English full names and ordinary abbreviations of the first three ordinances are: AMLO: Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance, Cap.615.   • 讀指引時，「金融機構」（FI，Financial Institution）這個詞，不一定每次都指同一批香港持牌機構。第03份指引的用語一般沿用 AMLO 的涵義；但詞彙表特別說明，除另有指明外，FI 採用 FATF 建議的定義。只有寫明「AMLO 所界定的金融機構」時，才用該法定範圍。本地電傳、合資格中介人及簡化盡職審查（SDD）各有自己的定義和條件，見第3部分及第6部分。例如：看到「金融機構」時，不要自動當成「所有香港持牌銀行、證券行和金錢服務經營者」的同一份名單。要先看該段是否寫明「AMLO 所界定的金融機構」，還是沿用 FATF 的較廣義用法。When you read the Guideline, the term FI (Financial Institution) does not always mean the same list of Hong Kong licensed institutions. Only when the text says “an FI as defined in the AMLO” does the statutory AMLO scope apply. Domestic wires, eligible intermediaries and SDD have their own specified definitions and conditions, which are covered in Parts 3 and 6. For example: do not automatically read every “financial institution” reference as one combined list of Hong Kong licensed banks, securities firms and money service operators. First check whether that passage means an FI as defined in the AMLO, or the wider FATF usage.

FATF與司法管轄區風險FATF and jurisdiction risk

必記Remember標準、名單與法律須分開理解Distinguish standards, lists and law

  • 財務行動特別組織（FATF）是1989年成立的跨政府組織。它制定標準，並推動法律、監管及操作措施的有效實施，以打擊洗錢、恐怖分子資金籌集及擴散資金籌集。香港是成員，必須實施其最新建議。FATF 會透過司法管轄區相互評估及嚴格跟進，監察各地的合規情況，並識別高風險及其他受監察地區。這些建議不是香港法例的替代品。The Financial Action Task Force (FATF) is an inter-governmental body formed in 1989. It sets standards and promotes effective legal, regulatory and operational measures against money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing. Hong Kong is a member and is obliged to implement the latest FATF Recommendations. FATF monitors compliance through jurisdiction evaluations and stringent follow-up, including identifying high-risk and other monitored jurisdictions. Its Recommendations do not replace Hong Kong law.

  • 定向金融制裁，通常是兩件事一起看：凍結指定人士或實體的資金或其他資產，以及禁止直接或間接向其提供資金、資產或相關服務。篩查不只看國籍或姓名，也須涵蓋有關實體、國家、活動及交易參與者。例如：對方護照不是某個受制裁國家簽發，並不表示可以不做篩查。如果付款最終是為指定實體而做，或交易涉及受限制活動，仍可能落入制裁範圍。Targeted financial sanctions generally do two things: they freeze designated persons’ or entities’ funds or other assets, and they prohibit direct or indirect provision of funds, assets or related services to them. Licences are granted by the Chief Executive, and applicants write to the Commerce and Economic Development Bureau. For example: the UN lists a person today, but the relevant Hong Kong regulation may not yet have been updated. 必記Remember物品、門檻與旅客Items, threshold and travellers

  • 跨境要申報的，不只是「大量現鈔」。現金類物品（CBNIs）包括香港或外地的法定貨幣紙幣及硬幣，也包括不記名或其他可透過交付轉移權利的可轉讓票據，例如不記名支票、匯票、旅行支票及本票。要按面值合計，總值超過港幣120,000元，才算「大量」；正好120,000元並沒有超過這個門檻。例如：旅客攜帶正好等值港幣120,000元的現鈔，不算「大量」。若再多一張面值很小的外幣紙幣，合計超過120,000元，就進入申報制度。What must be declared when crossing the boundary is not only “a large amount of cash”. CBNIs include legal-tender notes and coins of Hong Kong or elsewhere, and bearer or other negotiable instruments transferable by delivery, such as bearer cheques, bills, travellers’ cheques and promissory notes. The aggregate nominal value must exceed HK$120,000 to be a large quantity. Exactly HK$120,000 does not exceed the threshold. For example: a traveller carrying cash with a nominal value of exactly HK$120,000 does not have a large quantity. Adding one more low-value foreign note, so that the total exceeds HK$120,000, brings the declaration regime into play.
  • 申報方法要看你從哪裡進出。從指明管制站入境，而且管有大量現金類物品的人，須使用紅通道並作書面申報。從其他地方入境的人，以及離境人士，則須應獲授權人員要求作出披露；一旦披露管有大量現金類物品，還須按要求作申報。例如：從指明管制站入境並帶著超過港幣120,000元的現金類物品，應走紅通道並作書面申報，而不是等到關員主動查問才說。若是從其他地方入境或正在離境，則是在獲授權人員要求時披露，披露後再按要求申報。How you declare depends on how you are entering or leaving. At specified entry points, a person who possesses a large quantity of CBNIs must use the red channel and make a written declaration. A person arriving elsewhere, and a person who is departing, must disclose on an authorized officer’s request, and must then declare if a large quantity is disclosed. For example: if you arrive at a specified control point carrying CBNIs worth more than HK$120,000, you should use the red channel and make a written declaration, rather than waiting to be asked. If you arrive somewhere else, or are leaving, you disclose when an authorized officer asks, and then declare as required after that disclosure.
  • 「管有」不只指放在口袋或手提行李裡的東西。它包括隨身物品、由該運輸工具運載的個人物品，以及托運行李；托運行李不論是否由、或將會由同一運輸工具運載，都可以計算在內。陪同未滿16歲人士的成人，在知道該幼年人管有大量現金類物品時，須承擔相應責任。如果屬於純粹過境、而且在香港機場未通過入境檢查的法定情況，則獲豁免。例如：把超過門檻的現鈔放在稍後另一班機提取的托運行李裡，並不表示你沒有「管有」這些物品；托運行李仍可計入。若你陪同的孩子帶著大量現金類物品，而你是知道的，相應責任可以落在你身上。“Possession” is not limited to what is in a pocket or carry-on bag. It includes personal belongings carried by the person, belongings on the conveyance, and checked baggage. Checked baggage counts whether or not it is carried, or will be carried, on the same conveyance. An accompanying adult has the relevant duties when aware that a person under 16 possesses a large quantity. The statutory airport-transit case, where the person does not go through immigration clearance in Hong Kong, is exempt. For example: placing cash above the threshold in checked baggage to be collected from a later flight does not mean you do not “possess” those items; checked baggage can still count. If a child you are accompanying is carrying a large quantity of CBNIs and you know that, the relevant duty can fall on you.
  • 旅客把外幣換成港元來判斷是否超過門檻時，要用有關事件當日的參考匯價。如果當日不是公布日，或事件發生在當天公布之前，則使用前一個公布日的匯價。若不能用這個方法計算，便按法定指定國際機構的匯價；再沒有，才用管有人通常採用的匯價。When a traveller converts foreign currency into Hong Kong dollars to see whether the threshold is exceeded, use the reference rate on the date of the relevant event. If no rate is published that day, or the event happens before publication that day, use the preceding publication day. If the amount still cannot be calculated that way, the statutory fallback is a specified international institution’s rate, and then the rate the holder ordinarily uses.

必記Remember貨物輸入／輸出與違規Cargo imports/exports and breaches

  • 如果不是以旅客身分，而是用跨境運輸工具輸入或輸出大量現金類物品，負責人必須事先透過指定電子系統申報。合併計算時，要依 section 10 處理同一運輸工具及同一客戶等情況。系統產生接受申報的參考編號，才視為已經申報。法例並沒有訂出一個統一的「提前24小時」期限，不要自行加上這個時限。For non-traveller imports or exports of large quantities of CBNIs on a cross-boundary conveyance, the responsible person must declare in advance through the designated electronic system. Apply the section 10 aggregation rules, including same-conveyance and same-customer cases. A declaration is made when the system generates the acceptance reference number. There is no invented universal 24-hour deadline in the ordinance, and one should not be added.
  • 不是所有貨物都要這樣申報。法定例外包括：適用的旅客物品、只經香港而全程留在飛機或船上的貨物、航空轉運貨物，以及郵包等情況。貨物外幣的匯價基準，通常是上個月15日的參考匯價（若當天不是公布日，則用之前的公布日）。這與旅客以「有關事件當日」為基準不同，不能混用。Not every cargo movement has to be declared in this way. Statutory exclusions include covered travellers’ belongings, goods that remain aboard an aircraft or vessel solely transiting Hong Kong, air-transhipment cargo, and postal packets. Cargo normally uses the previous month’s 15th-day reference rate, or the preceding publication day if that date is not a publication day. That is different from the traveller’s event-date basis, and the two must not be mixed up.
  • 沒有遵從申報或披露規定，或作出在要項上虛假的申報或披露，可罰款港幣500,000元及監禁2年。就部分指明罪行，關長可以發出通知，讓人在指定的合理時間內繳付港幣2,000元而不提控。這並不是人人都有的權利：如果已有既往相關定罪或繳付紀錄、有指定的洗錢或恐怖主義定罪，或已按 section 17 檢取物品等情況，便不適用。Failure to comply with a declaration or disclosure duty, or making a materially false declaration or disclosure, can carry a fine of HK$500,000 and imprisonment for two years. For some specified offences, the Commissioner may issue a notice allowing payment of HK$2,000 within a specified reasonable time instead of prosecution. This is not an entitlement for everyone. Relevant prior convictions or payment records, specified money-laundering or terrorism convictions, or seizure under section 17, exclude it.

認識Recognise現金類物品的五類票據及匯價日期Five instrument forms and the exchange-rate event

  • 可轉讓票據這一類，包括以下形式：持有人形式、無限制背書、向虛構受款人付款、所有權隨交付轉移，以及已經簽署但未寫受款人姓名的票據。常見例子有不記名支票、本票、匯票及旅行支票，但必須符合上述票據形式。法定貨幣紙幣及硬幣是另一類。現金類物品並非只有現金和不記名支票才計算。The negotiable-instrument limb includes these forms: bearer form, unrestricted endorsement, a fictitious payee, title passing on delivery, and a signed instrument without a named payee. Common examples include bearer cheques, promissory notes, bills and travellers’ cheques, but only if they meet those instrument-form conditions. Legal-tender notes and coins form the other limb. 必記Remember檢取與扣留：十個工作日、三個月、兩年Seizure and detention: ten working days, three months, two years

  • 檢取現金類物品，與申報制度的「超過港幣120,000元」門檻，不是同一套規則。如果是行使 section 16(1) 的權力時發現現金類物品，而獲授權人員合理懷疑它們屬 OSCO section 25(1) 的犯罪得益，或屬 UNATMO 所指的恐怖分子財產，便可以依 section 17 檢取。這項權力針對的是懷疑財產，不能把申報制度的超過12萬元門檻套進來。例如：關員合理懷疑某旅客手提包裡的8萬元現鈔是犯罪得益，仍可能依這項權力檢取。不能因為還沒超過12萬元申報門檻，就以為一定不可以檢取。Seizing CBNIs is not the same as the declaration regime’s “above HK$120,000” threshold. If CBNIs are found in the exercise of section 16(1) powers, an authorized officer may seize them under section 17 on reasonable suspicion that they represent OSCO section 25(1) crime proceeds, or UNATMO terrorist property. This suspected-property power does not import the declaration regime’s above-HK$120,000 threshold. For example: an officer who reasonably suspects that HK$80,000 in cash in a traveller’s bag represents crime proceeds may still be able to seize it under this power. The fact that the amount is below the HK$120,000 declaration threshold does not by itself mean seizure is impossible.
  • 在 section 18 及 section 19 的規限下，section 17 通常准許把檢取的物品扣留至檢取日後不超過10個工作日。這裡的「工作日」不包括星期六、公眾假期、黑色暴雨警告日及烈風警告日，不要直接套用 AMLO 的「營業日」定義。這是物品的扣留期，不是拘捕後扣留人的期限。例如：星期五檢取的物品，其後的星期六、星期日及公眾假期都不算入這10個工作日；也不能改用 AMLO 對營業日的計法來縮短或延長這個期限。Subject to sections 18 and 19, section 17 ordinarily allows the seized property to be detained for no more than ten working days after the day of seizure. “Working days” here exclude Saturdays, general holidays, and black-rainstorm or gale-warning days. Do not substitute the AMLO definition of business days. This is a period for detaining property, not a period of personal custody after arrest. For example: if property is seized on a Friday, the following Saturday, Sunday and any general holiday do not count toward the ten working days. You also cannot use the AMLO business-day method to shorten or lengthen this period.
  • 如果懷疑的是犯罪得益，獲授權人員可以在上述期間內，向原訟法庭或區域法院申請 section 18 的繼續扣留令。法院必須信納存在合理懷疑，而且扣留有理據，例如要進一步調查來源，或考慮相關刑事程序、沒收或充公程序。每次命令的期限，不超過自該命令日起3個月。即使再發新命令，總扣留期也不得超過首個法院命令日起計的2年。If the suspicion is of crime proceeds, an authorized officer may, within that period, apply to the Court of First Instance or the District Court for a continuation order under section 18. The court must be satisfied that there is reasonable suspicion and that detention is justified, for example to investigate the source, or to consider relevant criminal, forfeiture or confiscation proceedings. Each order may run for no more than three months from its date. Even if further orders are made, total detention must not go beyond two years from the date of the first court order.
  • 兩年並不是全無例外的絕對上限。如果 section 19 所列的相關程序，在扣留期間已經提起，物品可以扣留至該等程序完結。另外，當 section 18 的扣留條件不存在、不再存在，或再沒有理據繼續扣留時，法院可應關係人或有關人員的申請，指示把物品發還。section 18 的延長機制，針對的是懷疑犯罪得益，不應不加區分地當成恐怖分子財產的同一套延長程序。Two years is not an absolute limit with no exceptions. If the relevant proceedings listed in section 19 are instituted during the detention, the property may be detained until those proceedings conclude. Separately, if the section 18 detention conditions do not exist, no longer exist, or detention is no longer justified, the court may, on an application by an interested person or an officer, direct that the property be returned. The section 18 continuation mechanism concerns suspected crime proceeds. Do not indiscriminately treat it as the same extension procedure for terrorist property.

理解Understand貨物的合併與申報責任Cargo aggregation and declaration responsibility

  • 貨物要不要合併計算，先分清你是不是承運人，以及「客戶」是誰。非承運人：合併同一人以同一跨境運輸工具輸入或輸出的現金類物品。承運人：合併同一承運人為同一客戶、在同一運輸工具上運送的物品。這裡的客戶必須是直接聘用該承運人的人，或直接聘用代運人、再由該代運人把物品運送或安排運送給承運人的人。如果同一客戶聘用了多名代運人，按這項規則，只有同一代運人運送或安排運送予同一承運人的物品，才合併計算。客戶是指非承運人，不能把物流代運人自行當成客戶。例如：同一客戶請了兩家代運人，分別把現金類物品交給同一承運人、用同一運輸工具運走。按這項規則，不會把兩家代運人的物品全部加在一起；只有同一代運人交給同一承運人的那些，才按此規則合併。Whether cargo CBNIs have to be aggregated depends first on whether you are a carrier, and on who the “customer” is. For a non-carrier, aggregate CBNIs imported or exported by the same person on the same cross-boundary conveyance. For a carrier, aggregate CBNIs carried by the same carrier for the same customer on that conveyance. The customer must directly engage the carrier, or directly engage the forwarder who transports the CBNIs to the carrier, or arranges that transport. If that customer engages more than one forwarder, this rule aggregates only the items transported, or whose transport is arranged, by the same forwarder to the same carrier. “Customer” means a person who is not a carrier. Do not automatically treat the freight forwarder as the customer. For example: the same customer uses two forwarders, each delivering CBNIs to the same carrier for the same conveyance. Under this rule, the two forwarders’ items are not all added together. Only the items of the same forwarder to the same carrier are aggregated under this rule.
  • 輸入、輸出，或安排輸入或輸出的人，必須確保事前已經有一份合規申報。如果只是掌管運輸工具，而對該次輸入或輸出沒有其他責任，則有 section 9(2) 的例外。通常以電子方式申報；系統接納並編配參考編號，才算完成。若關長認為電子傳送並非切實可行，則須依關長指示採用另一方式，不是可以自行改用任意紙本程序。旅客、郵包，以及法定過境或航空轉運貨物是否豁除，另按 section 8 判斷。A person who imports or exports, or who causes an import or export, must ensure that a compliant declaration has already been made beforehand. Section 9(2) excepts a conveyance operator who has no other responsibility for that import or export. The declaration is normally made electronically. Submission is complete only when the system accepts it and allocates a reference number. If the CCE considers electronic transmission impracticable, use an alternative manner directed by the CCE, not an arbitrary paper route of your own choosing. Whether travellers, postal packets and the specified transit or air-transhipment cargo are excluded is judged separately under section 8.
