先判斷何時須做客戶盡職審查Decide when customer due diligence is required

先分清業務關係和非經常交易，再檢查門檻、關連交易及懷疑。金額低於門檻，不等於可以忽略風險。Distinguish a business relationship from an occasional transaction, then check thresholds, linked transactions and suspicion. Below-threshold activity does not remove risk obligations.

公司派人開戶，客戶仍然是公司。身分識別資料、身分核實及代表授權是三件相關但不同的事。A company remains the customer when it sends a representative. Identification data, identity verification and authority are related but separate tasks.
