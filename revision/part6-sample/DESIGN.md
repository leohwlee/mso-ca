# Part 6 reading design / 第6部分閱讀設計

Use the shared [root design system](../../DESIGN.md). The owner-selected B direction,
**批注式研讀冊 / The Annotated Study Reference**, replaces this sample’s former green
identity: blue-gray frame, white paper, ink-blue hierarchy and restrained amber annotations.
Green/red remain available for explicit outcomes and existing answer feedback.

所有視覺決定以[根目錄共用設計系統](../../DESIGN.md)為準。使用者選定的 B 方向
「批注式研讀冊」已取代本範例舊有的綠色識別：藍灰外框、白色紙面、墨藍層級及
克制的琥珀色批注。綠色與紅色保留作明確結果及既有答案回饋。

This authored sample and the seven-part reference use [shared reading CSS](../study-notes/style.css).
The build embeds those styles and bundled DM Sans into the generated HTML. Maintain sources
rather than generated pages. Preserve bilingual wording, complete filenames, paragraph locators,
semantic comparisons and authored diagrams.

本篇原創範例與七部分研讀資料共用[閱讀樣式](../study-notes/style.css)。產生程式
將共用樣式及內附 DM Sans 字型嵌入 HTML；請修改來源，不要直接修改產生頁面。
保留雙語原意、完整檔名、段落定位、語義對照表及既有圖解。

See [QA.md](QA.md) for this sample’s verification record. Visual documentation and finish
review do not constitute new independent semantic or legal review.

本範例的檢查記錄見 [QA.md](QA.md)。視覺文件與完成審查不代表新增獨立語義或法律覆核。
