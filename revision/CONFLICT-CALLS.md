# How the pack and the bank settle conflicting sources

In September 2026 a review found 24 places where official texts disagree: two documents, or the
English and Chinese texts of one document. The owner decided each one on 24 September 2026. The
revision pack and the question bank (`web/questions.json`) both follow these calls.

## Between documents: the owner's call applies in both languages

## English and Chinese texts of one document: each language follows its own text

The owner's rule: "when a user selects Chinese, they should see content that matches the Chinese
original, and when they select English, it needs to match the English original." So for these ten, the
English view and the English side of a question say what the English text says. The Chinese view and the
Chinese side say what the Chinese text says. Neither mentions the other. A question whose answer would
depend on the difference is reworked onto what both texts share.
