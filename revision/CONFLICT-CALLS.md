# Conflicting sources and how each is settled

A September 2026 check of the revision pack against the official documents found 24 places where
the sources disagree. The owner decided each one on 24 September 2026 and reviewed the decisions on
25 September 2026. The revision pack and the question bank (`web/questions.json`) follow them.

## Document numbers

The numbers are the files in `docs/`, listed in [`docs/README.md`](../docs/README.md).

## General rules

| When | How it is settled |
|---|---|
| Two documents disagree | My choice applies in both the English and the Chinese view. |
| The English and Chinese versions of one document disagree | Each language view follows its own text, unless I decided otherwise (C02 and C22). No page compares the two language versions, and the question bank avoids questions whose answer depends on the difference. |
| A new conflict is found | It comes to me before either side is taught. |

## The 24 conflicts

| # | Question | Source of the conflict | My choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | At renewal, must a licensee that still has someone who passed the Assessment nominate someone to sit it again? | **Two documents disagree.**<br>• Guidance Notes (01) ¶4.4(ii) and ¶8.1–8.2: a nomination is needed only if no one who passed remains; a pass is a company-based qualification.<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶6.2 and ¶6.4(c): every licensee is invited to nominate 90 days before expiry, and the renewal is invalid without a nomination within 7 days. | Follow the Licensing Guide, the later document: every licensee must nominate within 7 days of the invitation, with no exception. *Confirmed on 25 Sep 2026.* |
| C02 | New licence: if the nominee misses the Assessment session, will the application be refused, or may it be? | **English and Chinese versions differ, and the Chinese texts also disagree among themselves.**<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶5.4: English "may"; Chinese 會導致 ("will").<br>• In Chinese, the Guide's ¶5.10(i) (可能拒絕) and Guidance Notes (01) ¶3.1 (可能導致) both say "may". | "May" in both views. The Chinese view follows ¶5.10(i) and ¶3.1, and shows ¶5.4's 會導致 as easy to confuse. *Decided on 25 Sep 2026.* |
| C03 | Renewal: if the nominee misses the Assessment, will the renewal be rejected, or may it be? | **Two documents disagree.**<br>• Guidance Notes (01) ¶3.1: not attending or not passing "may" lead to refusal.<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶6.2: at renewal, missing the session "will" lead to rejection, in both languages. | Follow the Licensing Guide for renewal: the renewal will be rejected. The Guidance Notes' "may" stays as the general statement. |
| C04 | Must at least one senior manager have passed the Assessment, or only attended it? | **Two passages disagree.**<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶4.15(b) and Guidance Notes (01) ¶3.1 require a pass, and the Guide's ¶5.10(j) makes "no one has passed" a ground for refusal.<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶11.1(h), in the list of a licensee's obligations, says "attended". | A pass is required. ¶11.1(h) describes the attendance step and does not lower the bar. |
| C05 | At renewal, does the 30-day period for sitting the Assessment run from when the letter is issued, or from when it is received? | **Two documents disagree.**<br>• Guidance Notes (01) ¶4.6: from the issue of the reminder letter.<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶6.2: from receipt of the invitation letter, like the 7-day nomination period. | From receipt of the invitation letter, following the Licensing Guide. |
| C06 | If Typhoon Signal No. 8 or above, or a Black Rainstorm Warning, is in force at or after 8 a.m. on the day, is the Assessment suspended or rescheduled? | **English and Chinese versions differ.**<br>• Guidance Notes (01) ¶13.1: English "will be suspended"; Chinese 會改期 ("will be rescheduled"). Both say any other arrangement is announced on the next working day. | Each language follows its own text: English "suspended", Chinese 改期. Either way, the Assessment does not go ahead that day. |
| C07 | Is a conviction or bankruptcy listed in s.30(4) of the Ordinance an automatic bar to a licence? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• FAQ (22), licensing answer A10: "in brief", no such conviction and no undischarged bankruptcy, which reads like an automatic bar.<br>• Ordinance (10) s.30(4): matters the Commissioner must take into account. Fit and Proper Guideline (04) ¶3: each case turns on its facts. | Follow the Ordinance: matters to take into account, not automatic bars. |
| C08 | Which fit-and-proper test applies to the ultimate owner of an applicant that is an individual or a partnership? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶4.1(a)–(b): fit and proper "to operate a money service".<br>• Ordinance (10) s.30(3)(a)(i)(B) and (ii)(B): fit and proper "to be associated with the business of operating a money service". | Follow the Ordinance: every ultimate owner takes the "associated with the business" test. |
| C09 | Which fit-and-proper test applies to a sole proprietor or a partner? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• Guidance Notes (01) ¶2.1: all must be fit and proper "to be associated with the business".<br>• Ordinance (10) s.30(3)(a)(i)(A) and (ii)(A): an individual applicant and each partner must be fit and proper "to operate a money service". | Follow the Ordinance: an individual applicant and each partner take the "to operate" test; only directors and ultimate owners take the "associated with" test. |
| C10 | Who is exempt from MSO licensing under s.25 of the Ordinance? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶3.1 and ¶3.5–3.6: seven categories ending at (g), with old insurance terms.<br>• Ordinance (10) s.25: the current insurance terms, and (h) a stablecoin licensee. | Follow s.25 of the Ordinance as it stands now. |
| C11 | Is the fit-and-proper test fee a one-off fee per applicant, or charged for each person every time? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶4.13: "a one-off fee for each applicant".<br>• Ordinance (10) Schedule 3 items 4–5, repeated in the Guide's fee schedule (§XIV): HK$945 for each person subject to the test, at grant and again at renewal. | Follow Schedule 3: HK$945 for each person, at grant and at every renewal. |
| C12 | Must the licence fee be paid with the application, or later on a demand note? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• Ordinance (10) s.30(1)(b) and s.31(2): applications must be accompanied by the fee, and a renewal must be made at least 45 days before expiry.<br>• Licensing Guide (03) ¶5.4 and ¶6.3: C&ED issues a demand note with the interview notice. | Follow the Ordinance: the fee accompanies the application. The demand note is how C&ED collects it in practice. |
| C13 | Guideline ¶1.3: may, or will, breaching the Guideline count against the fitness and propriety of the MSO's senior people? | **English and Chinese versions differ.**<br>• AML/CFT Guideline (02) ¶1.3: English "may also reflect adversely"; Chinese 將 ("will"). | Each language follows its own text: English "may", Chinese 將. |
| C14 | Do the $8,000 wire-transfer and remittance rules catch a transaction of exactly $8,000? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• FAQ (22), AML question 26: "over $8,000".<br>• Ordinance (10) Schedule 2 s.12(5)(a) and s.13(1), and AML/CFT Guideline (02) ¶10.5: "equal to or above $8,000". | Follow the Ordinance: $8,000 or more, so exactly $8,000 is caught. |
| C15 | What is the full name of the JFIU? | **Two documents disagree.**<br>• Sample questions (23), Example (5), English: "Joint Financial Investigation Unit". The Chinese has the correct 財富情報組.<br>• AML/CFT Guideline (02) glossary and the delivery-channels circular (12): "Joint Financial Intelligence Unit". | "Joint Financial Intelligence Unit". |
| C16 | Guideline ¶9.6: is using a mix of training methods encouraged, or required? | **English and Chinese versions differ.**<br>• AML/CFT Guideline (02) ¶9.6: English "is encouraged to consider"; Chinese 應 ("should"), which ¶1.6 makes a requirement. | Each language follows its own text: English "encouraged", Chinese 應. |
| C17 | Can a non-resident at the counter be verified with a national identity card or a driving licence? | **Two documents disagree.**<br>• FAQ (22), AML answer 1: acceptable for all non-residents.<br>• AML/CFT Guideline (02) Appendix A ¶1–2: a non-resident who is present uses a valid travel document; the wider list is for non-residents who are not present. The main text, ¶4.3.3(a), accepts "other national identity card" for any customer. | Follow Appendix A: a travel document at the counter, and the wider list for non-residents who are not present. Don't learn a flat "never", because ¶4.3.3(a) accepts a national identity card. |
| C18 | Must a beneficial owner always be a natural person? | **A document disagrees with the Ordinance.**<br>• AML/CFT Guideline (02) ¶4.4.1: the natural person(s) who ultimately owns or controls the customer, or on whose behalf a transaction is conducted.<br>• Ordinance (10) Schedule 2 s.1(1), and the Guideline's own ¶4.4.6–4.4.10: "the other person" for a customer acting on someone's behalf, and a trust's settlor and trustee, need not be individuals. | Follow the Guideline's ¶4.4.1: the natural person(s) who ultimately owns or controls the customer, or on whose behalf a transaction is conducted. *Confirmed on 25 Sep 2026.* |
| C19 | For a non-resident who is not in Hong Kong, may the MSO use the listed documents, or should it? | **English and Chinese versions differ.**<br>• AML/CFT Guideline (02) Appendix A ¶2: English "may"; Chinese 應 ("should"). The list of documents is the same. | Each language follows its own text: English "may", Chinese 應. |
| C20 | Guideline ¶5.10: is either of the two warning situations enough on its own, or must both be present? | **English and Chinese versions differ.**<br>• AML/CFT Guideline (02) ¶5.10 lists (a) transactions that don't fit what the MSO knows of the customer and (b) complex or unusual transactions with no apparent purpose. The English view teaches that either situation is enough; the Chinese view does not say whether both are needed. *Confirmed on 25 Sep 2026.* |
